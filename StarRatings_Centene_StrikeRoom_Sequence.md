**"Wait for the October ratings."** The ratings in October report a measurement window that is closing now. Waiting means the next cliff is already set before anyone acts. The half star moves during the year, not on release day.

**"Send me information."** Happy to. The one thing a deck will not answer is which of your contracts are closest to the line and most execution-sensitive, which is a twenty-minute conversation. I will send the overview and we can find time if it is worth it.
