05 · The proof motion · how it runs

Multichannel, two message tracks, staggered.

Channels

LinkedIn-first

Connect, note, voice note, then a call. Email is the supporting channel, kept within an inbox-safe cap.

Message

Two tracks

Ops and Member Experience hear the in-the-moment capacity lever. Finance hears the QBP-recovery and payback math.

Cadence

Four touches

Day 1 email, Day 2 connect, Day 4 voice note, Day 6 call. Waves staggered so no day is bursty.

Success is defined up front: replies and live conversations, not just booked meetings, over a real window. A sharp reply in week two is proof the motion works even if the invite lands in week four.

06 · Built-in guardrails

The guardrails protecting every send.

The motion runs inside standing guardrails, so it moves fast without ever putting the brand or the install base at risk.

In place

Customer exclusion. Current customers are automatically held out of every campaign, so the install base is never at risk of a cold touch, confirmed on live rows before every send.

In place

Verified claims. The only proof used is Humana's public figures: about two hours of capacity per agent per month, and seven times ROI five years in. No unverified numbers reach a prospect.

In place

Deliverability. Sending health is monitored: LinkedIn-first, email kept within a verified inbox-safe volume, so domain reputation stays protected.

In place

Copy quality. Every message clears an adversarial quality gate before it ships, so what reaches a prospect is already pressure-tested.
