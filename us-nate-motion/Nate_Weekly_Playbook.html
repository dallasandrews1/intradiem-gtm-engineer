Deliverability guardrail

Mailbox health is confirmed (reputation 100%). The 20/day send cap is shared across all 10 campaigns on your corporate mailbox, so email stays scarce: cap this motion at 3 emails/day, sent first thing before the other campaigns consume the shared cap.

LinkedIn is the primary channel. If email headroom is gone on a given day, run that day's email as a LinkedIn connect instead and let the voice note and call carry the sequence. Volume never beats inbox placement.

Non-negotiables

Rules of the road

  • Blank connection requests. Day 2 LinkedIn connects go out with no note; the personalization lives in the Day 4 voice note once you're connected.
  • Any reply stops the sequence for that contact. Do not fire a scheduled touch at someone who already answered; route the reply to handling first.
  • Log every touch the day it happens in the scoreboard. An untracked send is an invisible one, and the scoreboard is the proof that funds the tools.
  • A reply is a win even if it's a no. Log the sentiment. A sharp "not now, but interesting" is proof the motion works.
  • Sign your full name on every email. Voice notes and calls, introduce yourself as Nathan.
