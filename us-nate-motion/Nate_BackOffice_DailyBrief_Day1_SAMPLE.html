The standing rules

Rules of the road

  • Any reply stops that person's sequence on the spot. Drop it in the channel; the response comes back drafted the same day.
  • A LinkedIn accept is an event. Flag it the moment it happens; a DM to a connection is a different message than an email, and tomorrow's brief reroutes for it.
  • Log every touch the day it happens. An untracked send is an invisible one.
  • Record your calls (Otter free tier works). The transcript feeds the account brain and sharpens tomorrow's brief.
  • Beat up the scripts. Anything that sounds off in your world, say so in the channel. The fix ships in the next morning's brief.
