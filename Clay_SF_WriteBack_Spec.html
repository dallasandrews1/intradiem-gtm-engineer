Clay to Salesforce Write-Back

For: Genna (Salesforce request / RevOps process owner)  •  implementation by Eric Ebeling once approved
From: Dallas Andrews  •  Date: July 23, 2026

Defines exactly what Clay Audiences will and will not write into Salesforce before any export is turned on. Today the integration is import-only (read-only), all exports off. Nothing writes back until this is signed off. Sharing with RevOps first because write-back creates CRM records and touches AE-owned accounts and campaign reporting, which is a process decision, not just a technical one.

The goal

Close the loop so outbound motions run in Clay feed Salesforce and attribution tracks. A person engaged in a Clay motion appears in SF, tied to a Salesforce Campaign that mirrors the Clay motion 1:1, with status that updates as they move through the touches. Sends go through Clay's own sequencer on our Outlook mailbox, so Clay is the only system that knows send-and-reply state; that is why Clay owns campaign tracking.

● What Clay will write (scoped)

  • Contacts — create net-new Contacts linked to the matched Account (by domain). On create only: Name, Email, Title, LinkedIn, Account link, Lead Source = "Clay [motion]".
  • Existing Contacts — nothing written to their fields; only add campaign membership.
  • Campaign / Campaign Member — one SF Campaign per Clay motion; Clay adds the member and updates status (Sent, Replied, Bounced).

● What Clay will never touch

  • Accounts — never created or edited. Read-only.
  • Leads — imported for dedup and suppression only; never created or edited.
  • Opportunities — read-only.
  • Any AE-owned field on an existing Contact — never overwritten.

Guardrails — all must hold before creation is enabled

  1. Customer-exclusion at the Account level. Clay never creates Contacts under an account flagged as a current customer (Type = Customer, or has a won Opportunity). This gates record creation, not just sending.
  2. Dedup across Contacts and Leads. Match on email so Clay never creates a duplicate for someone who already exists as either object.
  3. Real-row validation first. Run exclusion + dedup against real synced rows and confirm by eye before creation is turned on. Not a synthetic test.
⚠ Why this is gated hard Clay write-back to Salesforce is permanent. Once Clay creates or updates a record, the action cannot be undone. There is no dry-run once live. Combined with guardrail 1, that means the customer-exclusion has to be validated on real rows before creation is ever enabled, so a current customer can never be created as a cold Contact.

Decisions needed from RevOps

  1. Contact ownership on create. Contacts under an existing Account normally inherit the account's AE, giving visibility but putting cold-sourced contacts on named accounts. Confirm the desired owner and socialize with Sales before enabling.
  2. Campaign records: who owns the SF Campaign objects and the naming convention, so Marketing reporting lines up.
  3. Activity logging: does the sending Outlook mailbox already have Einstein Activity Capture? If yes, per-email activity is already handled and Clay will not duplicate it. If no, per-email Task logging stays out of v1.

Rollout order

  1. Sign off on this spec.
  2. Enable the scoped export fields only (Contacts create + campaign membership). Everything else stays "Never write."
  3. Validate exclusion + dedup on real rows.
  4. Turn on creation for one motion (Star Ratings); watch the first batch, confirm no duplicates and no customer records created.
  5. Expand to other motions once proven.
