# Message-Variant Starter Pack (Pilot Wk 3–4)
Three variants per segment — the "three message variants per segment" the Section 05 pilot calls for. Standard: the Jul 9 gold sample (Variant 1A) and MessageGen prompt v2.2: short, one insight, a specific signal/number, natural CTA ("thought it might be worth... Have 15 minutes...?"), contractions always, no em dashes, no retired Call Center measure named as a scored lever. `{{merge_fields}}` = personalization at scale from the Golden List.

**v2.2 compliance pass (Jul 10 2026):** 1B and 2B rewritten. Both previously pitched the Call Center measures as movable; the CY2027 final rule retires them for 2028 Stars, and the v2.2 core position is CAHPS experience, complaints, appeals, customer service only. 1B now runs the WINDOW BEAT (scored through December). All other variants checked clean against the v2.2 banned-words list.

**Rules baked in:** no fabricated stats — any Intradiem metric must come from the verified-metrics repository before sending. Human approval before anything leaves the building. Vary opener/angle so these read as different messages, not a template with swapped variables.
