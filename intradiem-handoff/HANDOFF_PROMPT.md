## 3. The strategy (the plan)

**Headline thesis:** The Star Ratings cliff is a C-suite event, so position it like one.

- Crossing 4.0 Stars turns on the CMS quality bonus, a 5% lift on the plan's payments. That is a CFO and CEO number, not a contact-center metric. Today Star Ratings is pitched to operators, which is why it stalls below the C-suite.
- **The cuts that make it real:**
  1. **Target both sponsor and champion.** The engine enriches and routes to the CFO/CEO (economic sponsor) and the Stars or Quality leader (operational champion), so the team multi-threads instead of single-threading ops.
  2. **Aim at the addressable subset, not all 38.** A Star Rating is a composite. Most sub-4.0 plans miss on measures Intradiem does not move. The winnable set is the plans whose gap sits in the call-center measures Queue Optimizer is built to move.
  3. **Race the window.** The call-center measures phase out after the 2028 Stars. The constraint is proving attributed pipeline before the window closes, not raw volume. Optimize for time to a credited reply.
- **The access play (how the meetings get landed):** Landing the C-suite meeting is the sales team's job, not Dallas's. The engine's job is to make it land more often: find the addressable plans, enrich the two contacts at each, fire on the cliff as a trigger, supply a curated room for whoever runs it, and source-tag every meeting. The exclusive executive room (Intradiem's best-converting motion per the interviews) is the vehicle, with the cliff as the hook. The vehicle and calendar are Naveen's to set, not Dallas's to assume.
- **Day-one asks:** (1) align on attribution, where a sourced reply is counted; (2) a green light to aim the first sequences at the economic buyer, not only ops.

**This is Dallas's original read** (it is the one thing that is his, not reflected from Naveen's onboarding site). Hold it as a hypothesis to pressure-test against the team's real history, not as a verdict.

- Playwright chromium is usually already at `~/.cache/ms-playwright/chromium-*/chrome-linux/chrome`. If it fails on a missing lib, pull it without root: `apt-get download libxdamage1 && dpkg -x *.deb ./libs`, then `export LD_LIBRARY_PATH=$PWD/libs/usr/lib/aarch64-linux-gnu`.
