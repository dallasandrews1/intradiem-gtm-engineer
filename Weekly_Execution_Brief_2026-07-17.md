# Weekly Execution Brief

Date: 2026-07-17

## Objective

Turn the live motion into a measurable operating loop this week. The goal is not to build from scratch again. The goal is to review what is already live, tighten the message and sequence, and force one clear signal from the current wave.

## The posture for this week

- Treat the current motion as live and already in motion.
- Focus on learning and tightening, not expansion.
- Keep the wave small enough to review carefully.
- Make every send tied to a named next step or clear learning objective.

## What matters most this week

1. Review the current target set and choose one clear wedge.
2. Tighten the message so it is specific and easy to answer.
3. Run one small, instrumented wave.
4. Review replies and classify them quickly.
5. Decide whether to continue, adjust, or stop based on signal.

## Weekly operating checklist

### Monday
- Review the current live account list and select one narrow wedge.
- Confirm the buyer angle and the reason this account should care now.
- Pull the motion guide, current message variants, and credit posture before doing anything expensive.

### Tuesday
- Sharpen the message around one core idea.
- Prepare two variants at most.
- Verify claims against the repo guidance and avoid anything unverified.
- Set the approval gate and define the success bar for this wave.

### Wednesday
- Launch one small wave.
- Keep the volume tight and the targeting focused.
- Record the send count, expected credits, and the named next step for each motion.

### Thursday
- Review replies and classify them by type.
- Mark each reply as one of: no response, positive signal, objection, or next-step request.
- Decide whether the current angle is working or needs adjustment.

### Friday
- Write a concise summary of what worked, what did not, and what to change next week.
- Capture the leading indicators for the manager update.
- Flag any risk early, especially around deliverability, credits, or timing.

## Success bar for the week

A good week looks like one of the following:
- one real reply,
- one clear next step,
- or one strong signal that the current angle is wrong and needs adjustment.

## Guardrails

- Do not expand the universe this week unless the current wave proves the angle.
- Do not spend heavily on enrichment or new build work without a clear sales reason.
- Do not let the wave become broad just because it is easy to send.
- Keep the focus on signal quality over volume.

## What not to do this week

- Do not restart from zero.
- Do not chase a big build when the live motion still needs tightening.
- Do not treat the week as a content exercise instead of a signal exercise.

## The decision this week

The main decision is simple: is the current live angle strong enough to keep pushing, or does it need to be narrowed and reframed before more volume goes out?
