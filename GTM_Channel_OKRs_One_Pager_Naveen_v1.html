Measure it like a revenue channel

Fund it on channel economics, differentiate it on what it does for every rep, and make it durable by measuring how it compounds. One ground rule underneath: we don't set a target we can't measure.

The Three Objectives

Three objectives, each one a reason the role exists

Measured like a rep, this role is a quota one slow quarter can sink. Measured like a builder, it's a cost center. Measured like a channel, producing qualified pipeline cheaper than any alternative while making every rep faster, it's something the business funds indefinitely.

Funds the role

The engine is a revenue channel with better economics than any alternative

  • Every engine-sourced outcome is traceable in the CRM, with source fields live within 30 days. This comes first: nothing else counts until it's countable.
  • Engine-sourced qualified pipeline at 5x the fully loaded cost of the role plus tooling.
  • Cost per qualified opportunity at or below half our next-best channel, against a benchmark we agree in writing in month one.
Differentiates it

Every rep with the engine outperforms themselves without it

  • Three reps across two regions each run at least one motion end-to-end weekly, with me out of the loop.
  • Under 48 hours from a market signal to personalized outreach in market, against a baseline measured in weeks.
  • Qualified conversations at 2x each rep's own pre-engine rate. Each rep is compared to themselves, so the credit stays clean.
Makes it durable

The engine compounds: cheaper, faster, and less dependent on me every quarter

  • A new motion goes from idea to in-market in under a week, versus the weeks the first one took.
  • 80% of our target market under always-on signal coverage.
  • The weekly scoreboard becomes the report leadership works from.

These targets are a starting point; the specific numbers are ours to set together once the first 30 days of data exist. The objectives are standing; the measures under them reset each quarter, and any target we hit becomes a floor we maintain. Raw activity and meeting counts on their own stay off the scoreboard: they measure motion, not value.

Before we set any target

Three things exist first: attribution fields live in the CRM, the channel-cost benchmark agreed in writing, and each rep's current baseline captured. An unmeasured target quietly turns back into opinion.

The floor under every target

The deliverability floor (40% open, under 0.3% spam, under 3% bounce), zero current customers in cold sends, and the credit budget with its checkpoint. Hitting a number by breaking one of these doesn't count.
