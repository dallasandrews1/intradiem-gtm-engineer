How a committee is built
  • Champion: Head of Workforce Management or Real-Time. Reads the desktop report, owns the renewal.
  • Ops owner: VP or Director of Contact Center Operations.
  • Evaluator: Director of Operational Excellence, Performance or Quality.
  • Back-office leader: VP or Director of Claims, Shared Services or Payment Operations.
  • Technology gatekeeper: the contact-center platform or telephony lead.
  • Executive: COO or CAO, covered by the DWO Executives campaign, never sequenced twice.

Whoever holds the role is in. A role nobody holds is skipped. A deputy is added where an account has five or more people in the lane.
