# The Outbound Engine — End-Goal State (one page)

## The whole thing in one sentence
A contact goes in, and a full set of ready-to-send, human-sounding, fact-checked emails comes out on a cadence — and a person only ever reviews the batch and hits launch.

## What the finished machine looks like
Think of it as an assembly line with three parts:

1. **The list** — one table per motion holds the people we're reaching and the facts about them.
2. **The engine** — for each person it automatically: writes each email, rewrites it so it sounds like a human, checks every number is true, checks it doesn't sound AI-generated, and throws out anything that fails. Clean emails come out the other side.
3. **The campaign** — takes the finished emails and sends them on schedule through the rep's inbox.

The human is never the writer or the checker. The human is the **reviewer and the launch button.**

## How you run a campaign (the repeatable recipe)
1. **Pick the batch** — tag which contacts go in this wave.
2. **Run the engine** — it writes and gates all the emails for them automatically.
3. **Load the clean ones** into a campaign that stays in Draft (nothing sends yet).
4. **Review and launch** — read the batch, hit go.

Four steps, every time, every motion.

## How you spin up a new motion (a new table)
1. **Copy the standard table** — it comes with the engine already attached.
2. **Swap in that motion's message rules and data.**
3. **Use the same four-step recipe.**

You don't rebuild the machine each time. You stamp a copy and change the message.

## The four rules that never change (the safety rails)
1. **Every number is verified** — no invented figures reach a prospect.
2. **Every email sounds human** — an AI-tell gate blocks anything that reads generated.
3. **Current customers are never cold-emailed** — a hard exclusion, always on.
4. **Nothing sends until a person launches** — the machine drafts, the human decides.

## Why this is the point (say it to anyone)
This lets **one person run enterprise outbound at the quality of a hand-written email, at full scale, safely.** The plumbing is complex, but the story isn't: *contact in → good emails out → a human hits launch.* Anyone can understand that, and everything we build works backward from it.

## Where we are today vs. the goal
- **Today:** Email 1 is fully built and proven (writes, polishes, checks facts and voice, gates). The multi-email engine is being finished, adding the polish-and-check step to every touch so all of them hit that same bar.
- **The goal:** one engine, every touch, every motion, fully hands-off except review-and-launch.
