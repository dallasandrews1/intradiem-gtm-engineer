Benchmark and cohort

Partnerships' own number, read on a small cohort with a holdout.

Benchmark

25%+ to the 12-minute meeting inside 45 days

Primary read. The 40-minute customer meeting is the second read, on the same clock. Speed is reported alongside rate: median days from registered lead to 12-minute meeting scheduled.

  • Baseline comes from the existing 249 records: trailing conversion and median days by stage, computed from the same fields, once the report is shared.
  • Cost per meeting sits next to the Cold row on the council view when the first meetings land, in the council's own units.
Cohort

Five to ten accounts, picked by Frank on Sep 1

From the 3xG whitespace list, operational visualizer accounts first (a first back-office deployment, then nothing), then real back-office deployments, then desktop-analytics-only.

  • Not current Intradiem customers; any that are get held for the account manager, never worked cold.
  • Enough seats to matter and a findable operations leadership layer; both checked before the pick, at no credit cost.
  • 3xG's sensitive columns are stripped before the list touches any tool.

Holdout

A matched set of similar accounts (same segment band, similar seats) stays on the current partner process for the same 45 days and is read from the same fields. If the picked list is too small to split, the comparison falls back to the trailing twelve months of the same stages, stated as such on the readout. Either way the pilot is judged against Partnerships' own history, not against a number GTM Engineering chose.

Weekly partner update

The partner hears what happened with the accounts they gave us, every week, without anyone writing it.

What it is
One short email per partner alliance manager (3xG: Jeremy), assembled Monday morning: accounts received, where each stands in the funnel, meetings booked that week, what we're doing next, and one line the partner can act on (an intro, a nudge, a date).
Sources
Pre-pipeline stage changes for that partner's registered accounts (read-only), lemlist activity for the same accounts, the pilot flag. Nothing typed by hand.
Who sends
Drafted by the engine into Frank's review; sent from Frank. After four consecutive weeks with no edits the checkpoint moves to "send unless stopped by 9:00", the same widening pattern used elsewhere. It never sends unreviewed in the pilot.
Content rules
Account status only; no pricing, no internal notes, nothing about other partners. Any Intradiem figure passes the verified-claims gate before it appears.
Logging
The job writes a log the daily rundown reads. It does not message anyone on its own.

Partner-seller side, separate track

Frank's other gap is staying visible to partner AEs and solution consultants. That's a monthly note to the partner-seller list Frank keeps (webinar recordings, the one-pagers, a customer story), sent from Frank, with the list held in Clay so the engine can see who opened and who went quiet. Same review gate. Marketing supplies the pieces.

Nurture drip

Registered accounts that aren't ready yet stay warm on a slow, light sequence.

Who enters
Registered accounts past the first sequence without a meeting, and accounts the partner says are not ready. Never a current customer, never an account with an open opportunity.
Shape
Four touches over 45 days (day 1, 12, 26, 40), email plus a LinkedIn profile view; no call prompts. Each touch carries one piece: the DMG webinar recording, the back-office one-pager, a short customer story, a partner-co-signed note where 3xG agrees to be named.
Exit
Stops automatically when the stage moves to 12-minute meeting scheduled or disqualified, or on any reply. Replies route to Frank and the sending rep the same hour.
Sender
Proposed: Nate's dedicated sending domain, Frank on reply-to. Frank confirms on Sep 1.
Copy
Drafted from the per-account briefing, sharpened and gated (verified claims, customer exclusion) before load. Frank reviews the four touches once; after that the sequence runs on its own.
Inputs

What comes from 3xG, and what GTM Engineering makes.

From 3xG, per account
  • Account name and headquarters (a domain is enough to resolve the rest)
  • Verint products deployed and roughly when (last 24 months), seat counts: already in the whitespace file
  • Which bucket: desktop analytics only, back-office deployment, operational visualizer
  • Whether 3xG is fine being named in outreach; the original deployment sponsor where they know one (name and title; email is a bonus)
  • One sentence on why the account stalled after the first deployment
GTM Engineering generates
  • The buying committee per account with verified emails and LinkedIn (VITO, champion, evaluator, gatekeeper)
  • A one-page briefing per account: what changed, who's who, the angle. Replaces the Gemini pass Jeremy was running.
  • The multi-channel sequences in lemlist, reviewed by Frank before anything goes out
  • The weekly update back to 3xG and the nurture drip
  • The day-45 read against the benchmark

Stripped before use

Pricing, margins, contract terms, 3xG's internal notes and people. The intake script drops these columns on read and writes a manifest of what it removed, so the scrub is on the record.
