# Ally Financial Inc.: partner-safe account brief (worked example)

_Digital bank and auto finance, Detroit and Charlotte. Two new owners of the functions Intradiem orchestrates, servicing operations and enterprise technology, took their seats in July 2026 while expense growth is held near 1% and revenue grows double digits._

## In one screen
- Why now. Sean Leary took a new seat over Consumer Servicing Operations for Auto Finance on Jul 23 2026 and Mark Mathewson became Chief Information and Data Officer on Jul 20. Both are inside their first 90 days.
- Where the conversation sits. Ally.ai is enterprise-wide and a Director is being hired to build process optimization and automation. Real-time orchestration of back-office work and people is the layer that sits beside the desktop analytics Ally already runs.
- The pressure. 2026 expense growth guided to about 1% while adjusted revenue grew 6% in Q1 and 10% in Q2, after the Focused. Forward. simplification (Ally Lending, credit card and mortgage origination exits).
