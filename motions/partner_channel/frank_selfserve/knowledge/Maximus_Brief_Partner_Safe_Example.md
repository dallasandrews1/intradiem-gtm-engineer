# Maximus, Inc.: partner-safe account brief (worked example)

_Government business process management, Tysons VA: Medicaid and CHIP eligibility, claims and appeals, clinical assessments, 1-800-MEDICARE and other federal contact centers. Productivity is now the margin story on earnings calls, and 75 to 80% of new bids carry AI criteria._

## In one screen
- Why now. On the Aug 13 2026 call Maximus credited its margin to productivity gains across core programs and quantified front-office AI work on five contracts. Back-office case processing was not part of that story. Three AI executives arrived in April and May 2026.
- Where the conversation sits. Case-processing work (eligibility, claims, appeals, clinical exam administration) swings with contract volume. Real-time orchestration of that work and the people doing it is the layer that sits beside the desktop analytics Maximus already runs.
- The pressure. The VA paused Medical Disability Exam incentives on Jul 1 2026 while it rebuilds invoice validation; FY2026 guidance moved to the low end. Timeliness and accuracy reporting on a flagship program is under the customer's microscope.
