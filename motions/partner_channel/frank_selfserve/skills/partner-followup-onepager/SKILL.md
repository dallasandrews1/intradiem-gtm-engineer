# Partner follow-up one-pager

The partner seller just had, or is about to have, the first conversation. The prospect validated some of the challenges. This one-pager is what lands next: Intradiem's story told through THAT account's specific situation, so the reader sells it internally. It is prospect-facing through a partner, so everything on it must survive scrutiny.

## Inputs
A finished account brief in the conversation (run partner-account-brief first if there is none). Ask only one question if unanswered: which pains did the prospect validate, or is this pre-meeting? Pre-meeting, lead with the brief's two strongest sourced pains.

## Structure, one page, in this order
1. **Header**: Intradiem, Dynamic Workforce Orchestration, and one line naming the account's world (their industry and operation, from the brief).
2. **Your operation today**: three short bullets restating the account's own situation from the brief's sourced findings (public facts only, never internal contract knowledge). Their words where possible: quote the earnings call or the job posting.
3. **The gap**: monitoring versus acting. Schedules, dashboards and desktop analytics show what happened; nothing reallocates people and work in the moment. Two sentences, tied to the specific pain validated.
4. **What Intradiem does**: DWO in three bullets, mapped one-to-one to the bullets in section 2. Alongside the existing stack (ACD and WFM named as the layers it works with), never replacing it.
5. **Proof**: only approved claims (list below). Two or three lines maximum.
6. **What happens next**: the 12-minute conversation, then a 40-minute working session with their operations data. Named as small asks, no "demo" language.
7. **Footer**: partner co-branding line ("brought to you with [partner]") only when the partner has agreed to be named; otherwise Intradiem alone with the seller's contact line left as a fill-in.

## Approved claims (the complete set for this page)
- Intradiem sits between the ACD (Genesys, Five9, Avaya) and WFM (Verint, NICE, Calabrio); it acts on top of them, not instead of them.
- Dynamic Workforce Orchestration: continuously sensing demand, reallocating work, and supporting employees as conditions shift through the day, in the idle seconds between scheduled activities.
- Net retention above 114% in 2025; record net new bookings in 2025; customer savings at an all-time high for full-year 2025. (BusinessWire, Feb 18 2026)
- About 350,000 contact center professionals on platform.
- NOTHING else: no dollar savings for this account, no named or blinded customer savings figures, no ROI multiples, no claims from memory. A stronger claim goes on the page only after it's confirmed in the Intradiem Value Repository (check the copy in this project's knowledge; if it isn't confirmed there, it stays off the page).

## Format
Produce a single HTML file (artifact) sized to print as one page. Intradiem brand: Roboto (Google Fonts, weights 400/500/700/900, headlines 900 with letter-spacing -.02em), Roboto Mono for labels (uppercase, letterspaced), ink #202020 on white, deep forest #014637 for the header band, green #2DB56E and green-600 #228752 for accents and section labels, orange #F58220 ONLY for the single next-step accent, light rule lines #E0E0E0, radius 8px. Include the Intradiem logo using the SVG symbol in this skill's logo_symbol.html asset (copy it verbatim, set color #FFFFFF on the forest header). No other colors, no emoji, no em dashes. Body copy stays under 90 words per section; the page must breathe.

## Voice
Calm, specific, peer-level. The prospect is the hero; the page reads like it was written by someone who studied their operation, because it was. Contractions. No superlatives, no "revolutionary", no exclamation points.
