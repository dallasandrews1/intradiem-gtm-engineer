EMEA channel partner map: every partner behind seven platforms, and who carries more than one

The channel partners bringing Five9, Cisco, Avaya, Genesys, Aspect (Alvaria), Verint and Amazon Connect to market across Europe, the Middle East and Africa. Built from the vendors' own partner directories read in full on Sep 2 2026, plus vendor award lists, partner pages and filings; every row carries its sources.

In one screen

  • Seventeen partners carry three or more of the seven platforms. BT Business (five), Sabio, TTEC Digital and Connect Managed Services (four), then Orange Business, NTT DATA, Damovo, Deutsche Telekom, IP Integration, IST Networks, Axians, Activeo, Indra, Seidor, Deloitte Digital, Accenture and Capgemini. Their sellers already sit between an ACD and a WFM layer, which is where Intradiem lives.
  • Start with the Genesys-plus-Avaya bench. IDC ranks Genesys and Avaya as the two largest contact center providers in Europe, and the partners that carry both (Sabio, Damovo, IP Integration, Maintel, Westcon-Comstor for distribution) hold the deepest installed bases in the UK, DACH and Benelux.
  • Market share exists at vendor level only, and mostly global. No analyst publishes partner-level share in EMEA; vendor-level EMEA share sits inside paid IDC, DMG and Omdia reports. Every published figure that matters is on this page with its region attached, so a global number never travels as an EMEA one. Where a partner is the named largest for a vendor, the quote and source are on its row.
  • The UK holds the deepest bench for all seven; the rest of EMEA is platform-specific. Genesys runs the widest network outside the UK (Turkey, Poland, the Gulf, Nordics, Iberia), Five9 is concentrated in the UK, Iberia and DACH, Verint's channel is UK plus Nordic, Swiss and Dutch compliance specialists, Aspect has nine EMEA resellers, and Amazon Connect is delivered by UK, German and Iberian AWS consultancies.

The seven platforms in EMEA

Seven platforms, four different channel shapes.

Five9157 partners mapped

Sells EMEA through a UK-headquartered team with offices in Porto, Munich, Dublin, Amsterdam, Barcelona, Stockholm and Johannesburg. States that DACH and Benelux are sold primarily through partners and that partner-influenced business was over 80% of new bookings globally (Feb 2025). The EMEA channel is concentrated in UK managed service providers (Babble, Onecom, Opus, Exponential-e) plus BT as the strategic carrier and WTG for DACH.

ProgramGold, Silver, Authorized levels; types Reseller, Service Provider, Technology Solution Broker, SI, Referral. EMEA Partner of the Year: Onecom (2022), Opus (2024), Exponential-e (2025); DACH: WTG (2024 and 2025).

ShareNo EMEA figure. International is 11.5% of revenue (FY2025 10-K); 12.7% of global CCaaS revenue (Metrigy 2025); Leader in the IDC European CCaaS MarketScape 2025.

Cisco20 partners mapped

Contact center goes to market almost entirely through partners; EMEA is about 26% of Cisco revenue. The CX channel is recognised through the WebexOne awards (EMEA Customer Experience winners BrightCloud 2023, Cisilion 2024, Natilik 2025) and distributed by Comstor and TD SYNNEX. Carriers (BT, Orange, Deutsche Telekom, Virgin Media O2, Vodafone) resell Webex Contact Center inside broader Webex bundles.

ProgramCisco 360 (Jan 2026) replaced Gold, Premier and Select with Preferred and Portfolio Partner per architecture, plus Cisco Powered Services accreditations including Webex Contact Center. Older sources still cite Gold and Master Collaboration.

ShareNo EMEA figure. Outside IDC's European top three; eighth globally by CCaaS share yet the fastest growing (Metrigy 2025); Niche Player in the 2025 Gartner CCaaS MQ; 10.0% of the global on-prem platform market.

Avaya14 partners mapped

After Chapter 11 (May 2023) Avaya narrowed to its top roughly 1,500 customers, and in 2025 pushed sub-200-seat cloud customers to partners, naming BT, Maintel and Sabio as certified carrier options. Westcon-Comstor is the EMEA master agent and distributor. The channel is deepest in the UK (Sabio, Maintel, FourNet, IPI, Connect) and DACH (WTG, Damovo, T&N, CCT), with a Gulf tier of self-described largest partners (CGC, Syscom, BCS).

ProgramAvaya Edge with Diamond, Sapphire and Emerald tiers as partners describe them; Avaya's own partner page was unreachable, so tiers are partner statements.

ShareNo EMEA figure. 36.3% of the global on-prem platform market (Metrigy 2025) inside a market shrinking about 20% a year; IDC ranks Avaya second in Europe for 2024 behind Genesys.

Genesys166 partners mapped

EMEA is a first-class region (1,600+ staff, 17 countries) run direct to partners with no distributor tier. Elite sits above Platinum and has gone to Sabio (Apr 2026), Kerv (May 2025), Orange Business and KPN. Carriers hold the largest homogeneous installed bases (Orange has 48,550 licences under management; BT, KPN, Telia, Telefonica), and the four named global SIs are Accenture, Capgemini, Deloitte Digital and IBM.

ProgramAscend: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Elite; types Solution Provider, Referral, Services, and Technology Solutions Distributor (North America only).

ShareNo EMEA figure. 20.0% of global CCaaS revenue, second to NICE (Metrigy 2025); nearly 45% of Genesys Cloud revenue is outside North America; IDC's top European provider for 2024; Frost's number one growth leader in Europe (2023).

Aspect / Alvaria9 partners mapped

One holding company, two brands since Sep 2024: Aspect (WFM and WEM) and Alvaria CX (outbound and compliance), majority-owned by Nut Tree Capital since Oct 2025 with a new Aspect board and CEO in 2026. EMEA offices in London, Madrid, Cologne, Geneva, Rotterdam, Rome and Claregalway, but no named EMEA leader and no Alvaria CX partner directory. The route to market in EMEA is Avaya (Aspect's only Alliance partner and the resale route for Alvaria CX) plus a short list of Aspect Diamond resellers.

ProgramAspect: Alliance, Reseller and Referral tracks with a Diamond tier and an 88-partner directory (15 readable). Alvaria CX: a channel program with a Partner Hub still under development.

ShareNo EMEA figure and no global one. Covered in DMG's 2025 and 2026 WFM reports alongside NICE, Verint, Calabrio and Intradiem, without percentages.

Verint44 partners mapped

Private since Nov 2025 under Thoma Bravo and combined with Calabrio; EMEA was about 18% of revenue and a third of headcount in the last public 10-K. Verint co-sells through platform partners (Avaya OEM, Five9 hosts and resells WFO, Cisco via Calabrio, 8x8, Odigo) and decorates its EMEA channel at an annual summit (2025: 90 partners). The UK holds the deepest bench (Sabio, Kerv, BSL, IPI, FourNet, BT), with Nordic, Swiss, Dutch and DACH specialists on recording and compliance.

ProgramFour types published (Technology, Channel, Consultancy, Global Alliance) and no tier ladder; Gold, Platinum, Elite and Premier are partner self-descriptions. Award tracks CX, FC (financial compliance), WFE and Cloud.

ShareNo EMEA figure. EMEA is 18% of Verint revenue (FY2025 10-K); DMG holds the Europe WEM cut behind a paywall; a press estimate puts Verint plus Calabrio at about 40% of WFM globally (unattributed, unverified).

Amazon Connect47 partners mapped

Sold through the AWS Partner Network. The Amazon Connect Service Delivery designation (2019) is being replaced by the Amazon Connect Customer Services Competency (Jun 2026), and Westcon-Comstor, TD SYNNEX, Ingram Micro and Arrow distribute AWS across EMEA without Connect-specific programmes. The EMEA delivery bench is UK specialists (CloudInteract, Connect, Route 101, SVL, Acceleraate), tecRacer and LionGate in Germany, Awiant in Sweden, Storm Reply in Italy, plus the global SIs (Accenture, Deloitte, PwC, NTT DATA, TTEC Digital).

ProgramAPN tiers Select, Advanced, Premier; Amazon Connect Service Delivery (deprecated Jun 2027) and the Amazon Connect Customer Services Competency (two categories). The only Connect-specific EMEA award on record: TTEC Digital (VoiceFoundry), UKI 2021.

ShareNo EMEA figure. Third globally by seats (DMG, Dec 2024 data); $1B annualized run rate (Amazon, Oct 2025); Frost Radar EMEA 2024 Leader; not among the named Leaders in IDC's 2025 European CCaaS MarketScape.

Where to start

Where a partner motion starts.

1
Open with the multi-platform integrators.

Sabio (Genesys Elite, Avaya Diamond, Verint Partner of the Year 2025, Amazon Connect), Damovo (Avaya Diamond, Cisco 360, Genesys Premier across DACH, Ireland and Poland), Kerv (Genesys Elite, Verint Elite), IP Integration (Avaya Diamond, Genesys Premier, Verint), Connect Managed Services (Five9 Gold, Genesys Premier, Amazon Connect, Avaya) and TTEC Digital. Their practice leads already sell an ACD and a WFM layer together, so an orchestration layer between the two fits a motion they already run.

2
Use the carriers for installed-base reach.

BT Business (Five9 Gold, Genesys Elite, Webex, a Verint-powered managed service), Orange Business (Genesys Elite in ten countries, Cisco, three Verint awards), Deutsche Telekom (Genesys Premier, Webex, Amazon Connect), KPN (Genesys Premier), Telia and Tele2 (Genesys and Verint in the Nordics), Proximus and Telenet in Belgium. Carriers hold the largest homogeneous installed bases and already run managed contact centre services; the fit is a managed-service add-on, not a resale line.

3
Work Genesys country by country.

Genesys is the only platform with a deep bench outside the UK: 16 Elite and 36 Premier partners across EMEA, including Indra, Seidor and Evolutio in Iberia, infinit.cx and Unima in DACH and Poland, IST Networks and Ejada in the Gulf, Advania across the Nordics. Filter the map by Genesys and a country to get the local shortlist.

4
Treat Westcon-Comstor as the one distributor that matters.

Cisco through Comstor and Avaya as EMEA master agent, with AWS added in Nov 2024. TD SYNNEX carries Five9 and Cisco in EMEA at award level; KOMSA distributes Five9 and Genesys in Germany; Nuvias UC distributes Five9 in the UK. ScanSource has no EMEA operation.

5
Follow Verint's own award list for the WFM side.

Sabio, Kerv, Orange Business, BT, Axcess Nordic (Nordics), Luware (Switzerland), Tele-Train (DACH), Evcoms (Ireland), 1GLOBAL (Netherlands) and Makwa IT (South Africa) are the EMEA partners Verint decorated between 2022 and 2025. After the Calabrio combination the same partners carry the combined WFM estate.
