# Genesys prospects for the partner lane, Sep 5 2026

> 145 Salesforce Prospect accounts whose own job posts named Genesys as the contact center platform in the last twelve months. Read by the Tech stack read workflow (PredictLeads through Clay) on Sep 5. File: `Genesys_Prospects_Fresh_Sep5.csv`.

## Why this list goes to partners, not to cold outbound

Genesys is the largest single platform in the prospect base (145 fresh reads against 71 for Five9 and 90 for Amazon Connect). A Genesys shop buys through the Genesys ecosystem: its account team, its AppFoundry, its implementation partners. A cold note from us competes with that; a partner introduction rides on it. This is the sourced list the partner pilot has been missing: not "accounts that might run Genesys," but accounts that said so in a job post this year.

## How to use it

- Every row carries the Salesforce owner id, so the AE of record is one lookup away before any partner conversation.
- `acd_last_seen` is the date the posting was last seen. Under three months old means they are hiring Genesys skills right now.
- Cross this against the partner pilot's whitespace and the EMEA channel map; a Genesys implementation partner already in an account on this list is the warmest path in.
- Nothing here is prospect-facing. It is a targeting list for Frank and Haresh.
