## Gaps

1. No open EMEA vendor-share table exists from any analyst. IDC EUR153039425 is the one document that has it and it is paywalled; only the top-3 order (Genesys, Avaya, Zendesk) surfaced. DMG 2025 and Omdia CY24 both hold an EMEA split behind paywalls.
2. European on-prem installed base by vendor: no analyst has published it in the open.
3. Cavell publishes European CCaaS research but no provider ranking or seat share is visible on public pages.
4. Distributors not verified: TD SYNNEX (EMEA vendor pages 404), Ingram Micro UK (timeouts), Arrow ECS (404), Nuvias UC (domains dead; current owner unknown), ALSO (no vendor list), Exertis (SSL error). ScanSource has no EMEA operations (locations page lists US and Brazil only). Exclusive Networks is security-only. Whether TD SYNNEX or Ingram carry Five9 or Genesys in EMEA is the biggest open question for the distributor tier.
5. SIs and carriers not verified this run: NTT DATA (all URLs 404), Vodafone Business (404), Telefonica Tech (404), Atos/Eviden (403), Kapsch/K-Businesscom, Axians, Proximus/Telindus, KPN (404), Telia, Telenor, Elisa. Foundever and Transcom name no platform partners publicly; Econocom names only Microsoft.
6. Partner-level share statements: only two self-claims found (Comstor "#1 Cisco distributor"; Kerv "longest-standing Genesys Cloud partner in EMEA, with the most Genesys Cloud deployments"). No vendor-issued "largest partner in Europe" statement surfaced for any of the seven.
