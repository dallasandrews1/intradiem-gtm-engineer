# Alvaria / Aspect, EMEA channel research pass (Sep 2 2026, signal-researcher, public sources only)

## Vendor context

Alvaria, Inc. is still the single holding company; the 2024 move was a brand split, not a legal separation. On Sep 4 2024 Alvaria relaunched Aspect as the WEM/WFM brand (BusinessWire Sep 4 2024; CX Today Sep 6 2024), and Alvaria CX kept outbound and compliance. Ownership changed in late 2025: the Oct 31 2025 Alvaria release (deal closed Oct 24 2025) names Nut Tree Capital, LP as majority equity owner, with debt cut from about $950M to under $200M. Aspect got its own governance: Jeff Kupietzky CEO (Apr 23 2026), new independent board (Jan 6 2026); Aspect's channel is led by Ryan Martin, VP Channel Sales. EMEA presence: Alvaria lists offices in London (Uxbridge), Madrid, Cologne, Geneva, Rotterdam, Rome and Claregalway, Ireland; Aspect extended Cloud Workforce to the UK and Europe on Nov 18 2025. No currently named EMEA leader was found; the last public EMEA figure, Cameron Thomson, left to become Avaya Group VP EMEA on Aug 22 2024. Program structure: Aspect runs Alliance (Avaya is the only one named), Reseller/VAR and Referral tracks with a visible "Diamond Partner" tier and a directory of 88 partners across 4 pages (only the first 15, A through C, were readable); Alvaria CX publishes no partner directory as of Sep 2 2026, says its Partner Hub is "under development", and sells Alvaria CX through Avaya and Avaya's channel partners (May 13 2024). Published share data: none for EMEA. DMG Consulting's 2025 and 2026 WFM reports cover Aspect alongside NICE, Verint, Calabrio and Assembled (2026 also includes Intradiem) but publish no share figures.

Platform routes (not channel partners): Avaya is Aspect's only Alliance Partner (Diamond) and the resale route for Alvaria CX; EMEA delivery of Alvaria CX runs through Avaya's own partner ecosystem (inferred, MEDIUM). Call Design, Aspect's only global Diamond Partner, operates in Australia and the US only and is not an EMEA route; it publicly lists Intradiem as a partner (May 2023).
