# Five9, EMEA channel research pass (Sep 2 2026, signal-researcher, public sources only)

## Vendor context

Five9 runs EMEA out of a London EMEA HQ with listed offices in Porto, Munich, Dublin, Amsterdam, Barcelona, Stockholm and Johannesburg (five9.com/about/contact, fetched Sep 2 2026). The Porto site is the European engineering hub, opened Jan 17 2023 with 100+ staff and a stated plan for 300. Named EMEA channel leadership from public sources: Thomas John, VP Channels EMEA (2021, still listed as an author on five9.com); Roland Lunck, Senior Director Channels DACH (WTG release, May 10 2023); William Irvine, EMEA Channel Director (UC Today, Nov 4 2021). Global: Kim Hill, SVP Partner Sales (Feb 11 2026). Whether the EMEA titles are current could not be verified.

Program structure: the Five9 Global Partner Program has three levels, Gold, Silver and Authorized, governed annually on revenue, education and KPIs (Credly badge description). Partner types on five9.com/partners: Tech Partners, Systems Integrators, and Channel Partners (Authorized Resellers, Service Providers, Technology Solution Brokers, Solution Providers, Referral). Five9 states 1,400+ partners globally and that partner-influenced business was over 80% of total new bookings in the past year (Five9 release, Feb 4 2025). Five9's Assist Digital release (Dec 7 2023) states Five9 sells in DACH and Benelux "primarily through partners". The IDC MarketScape European CCaaS 2025 (Five9 release, Oct 14 2025) says Five9 is established in UK&I, DACH, Benelux and the Nordics and expanding to other Europe, MEA and APAC, on a "partner-centric model". EMEA Partner of the Year: Babble (year not confirmed), Onecom (2022 awards, announced Jan 31 2023), Opus Technology (2024 awards, Feb 4 2025), Exponential-e (2025 awards, Feb 11 2026); DACH Partner of the Year went to WTG in both 2024 and 2025 cycles. No dedicated distributor award for EMEA exists.

Published share data: Five9's 10-K geographic table shows International revenue of $131.7M of $1,149.1M total in FY2025 (11.5%) and $114.2M of $1,041.9M in FY2024 (10.9%); International is not broken out by region. IDC sizes European CCaaS at $1.5B in 2024 growing to $3.7B by 2029 (20% CAGR) and names Five9 a Leader (Oct 14 2025), with no share percentage published. Metrigy's global CCaaS share for 2025: NiCE 22.3%, Genesys 20.0%, Five9 12.7% (Mar 30 2026), with no Europe breakout public. No public EMEA or Europe share figure for Five9 exists in any source found.
