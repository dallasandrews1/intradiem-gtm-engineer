# Verint, EMEA channel research pass (Sep 2 2026, signal-researcher, public sources only)

## Vendor context

Verint went private on Nov 26 2025 when Thoma Bravo closed its $2B purchase (announced Aug 25 2025) and combined it with portfolio company Calabrio; former Calabrio CEO Dave Rhodes was named CEO of the combined company in Feb 2026. CX Today (Mar 4 2026) reported layoffs including a UK consultation covering 400+ staff. 2026 status: "no plans to sunset anything," Calabrio customers gained Verint bots Mar 10 2026, Engage 2026 (Jun 2026) was the first joint event. EMEA weight: FY ended Jan 31 2025, EMEA was about 18% of revenue ($169M of $909M) and 33% of the roughly 3,800 staff sat in EMEA; Verint sells through direct sales teams and co-sells with indirect channels (10-K, Mar 26 2025). Program structure: Verint publishes four program types, Technology, Channel, Consultancy and Global Alliance Partners; it does NOT publish a tier ladder. Tier words (Gold, Platinum, Elite, Premier) appear only on partner sites and are self-described. EMEA channel org: Mike Pryke-Smith, VP Partner and Alliances EMEA (May 2023). EMEA Partner Summits: 2024 Dubrovnik; 2025 La Zambra, Andalusia (May 14-16, 90 partners, "largest yet"); no 2026 EMEA summit release found as of Sep 2 2026. Award tracks are CX, FC (financial compliance), WFE and Cloud, a useful proxy for which partners carry WFM versus recording.

Platform routes (not channel partners): Avaya (20+ year strategic partnership, Avaya OEM WFO products serviced by DACH partners; most-awarded platform partner at Verint EMEA summits 2022 to 2025), Five9 (hosts and resells Verint WFO; EMEA Enterprise Expansion Partner of the Year (WFE) 2025; Global Partner of the Year 2025 and 2026), Cisco (Calabrio integrates with all Cisco platforms), AWS (marketplace listing; no Amazon Connect partner-page listing), 8x8 (EMEA Solution Innovation Award 2025), Odigo (CCaaS platform partner, Marketing Partner of the Year 2023), AnywhereNow (WFM plugin). No Verint partner page exists for Genesys; the Genesys route into Verint in EMEA runs through shared partners (Sabio, Kerv, Orange Business, IPI).

## Gaps

- Verint tier ladder: Verint publishes only four program types. Gold, Platinum, Elite, Premier appear only on partner sites and cannot be reconciled into one hierarchy.
- Partner directory enumeration: verint.com/partners renders the same first 18 entries on every page, so the full EMEA list (with the Regions filter) was not extracted. A browser session with the Regions filter set to EMEA would close this.
- Not verified either way: Vodafone/VodafoneThree (a Verint customer, not a verified partner), Deutsche Telekom/T-Systems, Telefonica, Atos, Capgemini, Deloitte, KPMG, Cognizant, Infosys, TCS, Wipro, NTT DATA, Mannai, Injazat, Alpha Data, Datatec, Jasco, Bytes.
- Confirmed NOT Verint partners on public pages: Call Design, Ventrica, Cirrus, Damovo, GBM Qatar, 1Stream.
- No 2026 EMEA Partner Summit or EMEA award release was found on verint.com as of Sep 2 2026, so 2026 partner standing under Thoma Bravo/Calabrio is unknown.
