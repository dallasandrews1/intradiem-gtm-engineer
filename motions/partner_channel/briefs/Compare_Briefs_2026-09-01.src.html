In one screen
  • Same spec, different output. Jeremy's prompt is a good section list and the engine keeps it. The engine returns structured fields with a source and a flag on every line, a named buying committee with LinkedIn URLs, and two versions per account so the partner-safe copy never carries an estimate.
  • The savings table stays home. The first pass modeled dollar savings from seat counts and assumed productivity lifts. Useful sizing to think with, but seats are not FTEs and none of it is in the Value Repository, so the engine keeps scenario math in an internal note and out of anything a partner seller or prospect sees.

What changes for Frank

What changes for Frank and Jeremy from here.

Frank

Picks the cohort, gets the pages

Every account on the cohort and the holdout gets the same two pages inside a day of the pick. Frank reviews the partner-safe version before anything travels to 3xG; the internal version is what the sequence copy is drafted from.

Jeremy

Contributes what only 3xG knows

Per account, 3xG contributes what only they know: whether the Verint deployment still runs and on which programs, and whether 3xG can be named in outreach. The engine takes the research load off his calendar.

The gate

Same rules as every other motion

Customer and partner exclusion on every account, verified claims on every line, human review before any send. Ally and Maximus cleared both gates on Sep 1: not customers, not partners, not registered by another partner in the pre-pipeline.
