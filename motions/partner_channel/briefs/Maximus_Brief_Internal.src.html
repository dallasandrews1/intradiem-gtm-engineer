In one screen
  • Why now. On the Aug 13 2026 call Maximus credited a 15.0% adjusted EBITDA margin to productivity gains across core programs and put a number on front-office AI (IVR, chatbot, proactive engagement): a 3.5% operating-margin lift on five contracts. No back-office figure was given. Three AI executives arrived in April and May 2026.
  • The gap. Verint Operations Visualizer and Operations Productivity, 1,510 seats (3xG sheet), is a monitoring layer on case-processing work. Nothing public shows rules-based reallocation of people or work behind it, while the business runs on contract-driven volume swings (Medicaid unwinding, VA exam volume, UI backlogs).
  • The pressure. The VA paused Medical Disability Exam incentives on Jul 1 2026 while it rebuilds invoice validation; that alone cut FY2026 EPS guidance about $0.35 and pushed revenue guidance to the low end. Timeliness and accuracy reporting on a flagship program is under the customer's microscope.
  • Gate. Not a current Intradiem customer or partner (Salesforce segment Sep 1 2026), not registered by any partner in the pre-pipeline. Clear to work. The enterprise WFM owner Jeremy's brief could not confirm is Marc Hillman, Senior Director of Workforce Management, found on the free Clay path.

On the savings table in the 3xG brief

The 3xG brief models $9.75M to $29.25M in annual savings from a $65,000 fully loaded FTE cost times 10, 20 or 30% of the 1,500 Verint seats. Seats on a 2024 licensing sheet are not back-office FTEs, the lift percentages are assumptions, and none of it is in the Value Repository. It stays out of anything a partner seller or Maximus sees. The business case comes from discovery data through the ROI skill after the 40-minute meeting.
