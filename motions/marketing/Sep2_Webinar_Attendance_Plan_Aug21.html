How the net-new list was built

Four steps in Clay

01

Accounts

179 companies that already produced a webinar registrant: the accounts the topic is landing at.

02

People

Clay search for current WFM titles and contact-center operations leaders at those companies. 2,015 found.

03

Not already ours

Checked every name against the 21,514 Salesforce contacts at those companies. 1,652 were new.

04

Verified emails

Clay's email waterfall, then ZeroBounce. 1,165 valid. The rest stay in the file with their status. 3005.6 Clay credits for the whole run.

Two notes

Nothing has gone out

Verified means the mailbox is real

ZeroBounce confirms the address exists; a small share of waterfall addresses can belong to a different person at the same company. Bounces and replies settle it quickly, and the reps' one-liners are worth a glance at name vs address before sending.
