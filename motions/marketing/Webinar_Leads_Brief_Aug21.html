How it runs

The workflow

01

Triage

Personal email, competitor domain, Intradiem address, or no email: stop, no spend.

02

Enrich

Person enrichment by email, 0.5 credits only when a profile is found.

03

Classify

Best available title to a role category (shared Function plus a rules fallback).

04

Route and store

Target role: bdr_review, otherwise nurture. Results written to the five Clay fields on the record.

Live on the "Webinar Leads" segment for new registrants; the backlog ran once. Fields: Clay Persona Key, Clay Webinar Route, Clay Enriched Title, Clay Company Domain, Clay Triage. Rename or remove at will.

Caveats

Before using the list

Salesforce Account Type is incomplete

Elevance Health and TD Bank are absent from the synced companies; Cigna, Assurant, Farmers, McKesson, Citi and British Gas are tagged Prospect; about 300 accounts have no type; brand domains (syf.com, evernorth.com) don't match the one domain stored per account. The customer flag here combines the Salesforce flag, the install-base list and known brand domains. Suppression on Account Type alone would include customers.

Role classification is a first pass

315 reachable registrants landed outside target roles (analysts, consultants, sales and project titles without a function word). A short review would move some of them; the workflow applies any change on the next run.
