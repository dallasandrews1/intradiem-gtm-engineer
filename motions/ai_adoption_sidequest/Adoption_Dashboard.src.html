Level 2 · risk signals to playbook

A trigger is a condition in the data; the playbook says what happens next.

Three of the four triggers fire from usage telemetry. Sustained workaround needs a source it doesn't have yet. Each firing carries the action, owner and response window, then whether a response was logged and whether usage lifted inside 14 days.

Owners and response windows are placeholders. The playbook sets the real ones.

Send anything. It lists what fired, who owns it, and when it was due. The playbook decides what goes out and where.

AI change management side quest · telemetry

Every signal in the framework, on one page.

Usage log in, three levels of metrics out, four triggers wired to the playbook. Sample data until Greenlight lands.

What it measures

Process, behavior, impact.

Level 1 · process integrityReadiness assessments done, baselines documented, managers briefed, triggers monitored, milestones held. From project records.
Level 2 · adoption outcomesActive use, time to first and sustained use, agents used, spread inside a team, manager engagement. From the usage log.
Level 3 · strategic impactAdoption at close, time to sustained adoption, intervention effectiveness. Productivity and cost wait for a baseline.
Risk signals to playbook

A condition in the data, an owner and a clock.

  • Usage dropMicro-coaching prompt, manager alert. Manager owns it, five days.
  • PlateauTargeted enablement in the user's own workflow. Enablement owns it, ten days.
  • Zero useManager escalation, individual outreach. Manager owns it, five days.
  • WorkaroundWorkflow adjustment review. Defined, unmonitored until it has a source.

Each firing records the response and whether usage lifted inside 14 days.
