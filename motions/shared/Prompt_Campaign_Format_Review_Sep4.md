The standard to judge against. A campaign earns a pass on each line or gets a specific fix:
1. 2. Branching: conditions on what actually happened. Has LinkedIn URL or not (LinkedIn-first path for rows with no verified email), invite accepted or not (message versus second email), email opened or clicked (call task fires on a click), replied (exit). Name every branch that is missing.
3. Testing: an A/B on Email 1 subject and body in every campaign, with a stated hypothesis per test and a read date; A/B on the connect note (with note versus without) where it is cheap to learn.

6. Reply handling: the relay routes every campaign to the right Slack channel (check campaign_channel_map covers all eleven ids), the objection handler drafts the answer, and the action brief lists the call tasks. Fix the map where a campaign is unrouted.
