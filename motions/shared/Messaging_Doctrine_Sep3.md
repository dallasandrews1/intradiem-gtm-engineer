# Messaging Doctrine (Sep 3 2026)

The single set of rules every Intradiem outreach message follows from today. It supersedes the conflicting rules in the first-draft engine, the copy sharpener, the motion-stamp voice core and stage ladder, and the Aug 3 first-touch doctrine. Those files now implement this one.

Why it exists: on the Sep 3 call Naveen said the campaign copy states a problem and never says what we offer or why the message exists. Jack shared the skill behind his UK messaging, built from two years of booking meetings every month and a year of refining the rules in Claude. Jack and Nate are the only BDRs and the experts on what gets a reply; the engine's rules were written before either of them had a hand in them. This doctrine adopts their practice and keeps only the engine rules that protect the company (verified claims, customer exclusion, solicitation law).

## What stays, unchanged

- Open on their world. A dated, specific signal or the operational moment. Never narrate their situation back to them, never explain a mechanism to someone whose career proves they own it.
- One idea per message, stated in one sentence before writing.
- Proud, not wound. A downgrade, outage, miss or layoff is never the opener. Lead with the response they are proud of.
- The verified-claims gate is a hard stop. A number, customer outcome or peer claim about Intradiem ships only from a VERIFIED row in `04-value-repository/Intradiem_Value_Repository.md`. Everything else ships as [UNVERIFIED] or not at all. Jack's UK product facts are now recorded there at CONFIRM tier and stay out of US copy until confirmed.
- Star Ratings humility clause wherever a computed number appears.
- WFM vendors (Verint, NICE, Calabrio) and CCaaS platforms are the layer we act on, never rivals to trash.
- No em dashes. Contractions always. No "hope you're well", no "quick question", no "circling back", no exclamation marks, no leverage, synergy, seamless, transform, innovative, robust, empower. Subject lines short and lowercase.
- Private intel never appears in writing. Referral names only to people the referrer actually knows.
- Customer-exclusion gate on every load. Opt-out line on UK and customer-lane email 1.
- Netherlands copy written natively, lead with the relevance ask, never localised.
- Batch honesty: after drafting a whole account, reread every message as if it were the only one.

## The message shape

Cadence, stated once at the top of any messaging doc: Email 1, LinkedIn connect (day 2), Email 2 (day 4), LinkedIn message (day 7), Email 3 breakup (day 11). Phone between touches for planning, WFM and performance personas, who answer. Ops directors get the emails. A blitz motion may compress this to ten days; it does not add emails.

**Email 1**: their world and the dated signal in sentence one. What we do in one concrete sentence by sentence two or three, Intradiem named once. One idea. One question at the end that names the topic. Under 110 words. Sign with the first name.

**Email 2** (reply in thread): one new angle or one new number from the library, never "bumping this". Shorter than email 1. One question.

**Email 3** (breakup): shortest. Names the re-entry condition ("if the hub consolidation lands the intraday question back on your desk") and wishes them well specifically, never generically.

**LinkedIn connect**: under 300 characters, counted, references the email ("shot you a note by email, thought this might be quicker").

**LinkedIn message** (day 7): the short version of the email plus the conversation offer.

**Exec air cover**: one email under 80 words, sent only after an operational thread is live, naming the colleagues already engaged.

## Persona to angle library

Pick the angle by what the title owns. Same verified numbers everywhere in an account, different framing per person.

**Front office**

| Persona | Angle | Worldview sentence they nod at |
|---|---|---|
| Head of Resource Planning / WFM | The intraday layer WFM never finished. Practical, no dumbing down. Sits on top of what they run. | "The plan is right at 8am and wrong by 10, and your team absorbs the drift by hand." |
| Contact centre or Ops Director (estate owner) | Scale and timing. Tie to their live event: new site, merger, platform migration. Delivered as productivity while hiring, never as redundancy. | "Growth becomes contact volume." |
| Performance / Operational Excellence | The last unmeasured variance. | "The biggest number never makes your dashboards." |
| Customer / Service Director | The one lever that moves cost and service the same direction. Coaching happens "when we're quiet", which is never. | "Recovered time funds daily coaching without CSAT dipping." |
| Complaints / Quality owner | Upstream lever. | "The rushed call becomes the repeat contact becomes the complaint." |
| Transformation / Automation lead | Highest-certainty item on the roadmap. Rules-based, nothing new to govern. | "A known number in a portfolio full of modelled benefits." |
| VP/SVP Stars, Quality, Medicare | Measure math and cut points, the 2028 window. Evidence evaluator, humility clause mandatory. | "The call centre measures are the cleanest lever left." |
| Group or C-level exec | Air cover only. One line on why now, then "I've written to [named colleagues] with the detail." | |
| New-in-role exec (six months or less) | A visible first-year win that needs no restructure. | "The listening tour ends and the agenda gets written." |

**Back office**

| Persona | Angle | Worldview sentence they nod at |
|---|---|---|
| VP/Dir Claims Ops, Appeals, Payment Ops | The backlog is a timing problem, not a staffing problem. The idle windows already inside the week are the capacity. | "The backlog cleared by Friday grows back by Monday." |
| VP/Dir Shared Services | Utilisation they cannot prove, multi-function queues with no live view. | "You can't see idle capacity you don't measure." |
| Document processing / intake leads | Peak intake is visible in the data days before the queue feels it. Staff by trigger, not calendar. | "The weeks that age cases show up in intake first." |
| Back-office technology / ops systems | Integration surface, sits on top of the case system and the WFM. Third-party validation over claims. | "Nothing new to govern, nothing to rip out." |
| COO / SVP Operations | Operational relief, never brand vision. Imply mechanisms, name consequences. | "The cost of the backlog is paid twice: overtime and rework." |
| CFO / VP Finance | Capacity without headcount, payback, cost per transaction. No adjectives. | "Headcount stops absorbing backlog at a point nobody has found yet." |

Vertical translation keeps the mechanics identical and swaps the pressure: insurance to claims surges and Consumer Duty; utilities to price-cap cost pressure, service scores, winter surge; telco to churn and complaints league tables; banking to branch-to-contact-centre migration; healthcare to Stars, patient access, appeals timelines; BPO to client SLAs and margin per seat. Mutuals and public-facing brands get "service up", never "heads down".

## Situational plays

- **New in role (six months or less)**: congratulate, name the moment, position a visible first-year win. Email 2 names the internal pressure-testers. Breakup respects the first-quarter inbox.
- **Win-back or ex-user**: never a stranger, never pleading. "You'll know Intradiem from your [company] days, so I'll skip the intro and give you the update." Never criticise the tool they switched to. Ask why they switched on a call, never in writing.
- **Exec air cover**: one email, under 80 words, only after an operational thread is live, naming the engaged colleagues.
- **Prior no**: quarantine. No sequence, no cold re-approach, never mention the rejection to their team. One re-approach email that quotes their words, leads with what changed, and fires only on a named trigger.
- **Gatekeeper known to kill deals**: one touch positioning them as the expert. Ask for their read, never pitch.
- **Build-in-house accounts**: no email cadence. One LinkedIn touch offering benchmark participation.
- **Bounces citing policy or administrative prohibition**: mark the account email channel dead, stop sending, convert to phone and LinkedIn.

Encode every piece of account intel as a rule in the account header of the messaging doc so it cannot be forgotten by the next person or the next run.

## Coordination note (every account)

Contact order by day. Who is estate owner, evaluator, sponsor, budget holder in waiting. Hit everyone the same week and say so ("I've also dropped [name] a note"). Who pulls whom into a call on a reply. The line "this team compares notes, keep every message identical on the numbers." Account-specific rules: quarantines, dead channels, names still to find.

## Eight-line QC before anything loads

1. Email 1 opens on their world with a dated signal, and says what we do by sentence three.
2. Every number about Intradiem traces to a VERIFIED Repository row. Every number about the prospect has a source under 90 days old. Nothing else carries a number.
3. Email 1 ends on one question, no calendar, no demo, no minutes.
4. Word counts: E1 under 110, E2 shorter, E3 under 60. Connect note under 300 characters, counted.
5. No two people in one account share the same angle.
6. No em dashes, no banned words, no "hope you're well", first-name sign-off.
7. Coordination note present, account rules encoded in the header.
8. Customer-exclusion gate passed on every row, opt-out line where required.

## Sep 4 addendum: the pressure standard (Dallas, Sep 4 2026, "the new standard of quality for messaging")

Set on the DWO executive build and applies to every net-new sequence from Sep 4. Where this section and the cadence above disagree, this section wins.

**Cadence.** Three channels for four weeks, a touch every one to two business days. Emails 5 across two threads (Email 3 opens a new subject on the vertical peak), calls 3, LinkedIn 6 on the connected path (visit with Email 1, connect, DM 1, like, DM 2, DM 3), voice note where the persona warrants it, pending invite withdrawn after the breakup. Sixteen to seventeen touches over 18 business days. Every step carries one new thing. Nothing bumps.

**Calls are the spine.** Every net-new sequence carries at least three call steps and every call leaves a voicemail, each one different: Call 1 points at the note already in their inbox, Call 2 carries the number and pairs with a same-hour "just tried you" email in the thread, Call 3 asks the this-year-or-next question. No direct number means the EA route: main line, the office of the executive, one line left with the assistant by name. Phones are sourced before the campaign starts, never after. C-level seats get calls too; the Sep 3 "no phone on C-level" line is retired.

**Never admit you don't know the org.** The pool is mapped. "I can't tell whose desk this lands on" or "if this is the wrong desk, a name is all I need" reads as no research and backfires. State what you know: "Aldemaro owns the transformation side of this at AES, so I've sent the same note there."

**No apologies, no exit talk.** Banned in every touch: "sorry", "fair enough", "I'll be quick", "I'll stop", "I'll leave you be", "closing the file", "closing this out", "no need to reply", "either answer helps me stop guessing". Exactly one breakup email, last in the arc. Every other step asks for the next thing.

**Direct asks after Email 1.** Email 1 keeps the doctrine's no-calendar question. From Email 2 on, a direct ask is fine and expected: "Twenty minutes before open enrollment. Which week works?"

**Multi-thread the account in the close.** Where an account has two or more seats, Email 4, DM 3 and Call 3 name the colleague as a fact (`colleagueLine`, `colleagueFirst`, inside a Liquid if-block so single-seat leads see nothing). The colleague is a different family first, then seniority, one name only. All seats at an account enter the sequence on the same day so the line is true by day 3. Multi-threading stays out of the first two weeks, where it reads as pressure.

**QC lines 9 to 12, added to the eight above.**
9. Every call step has a voicemail script and an EA route; no "no voicemail" calls.
10. No admission of not knowing the org; every colleague reference is a stated fact from the account map.
11. No apology or exit phrase anywhere except the single breakup; every touch ends on an ask or a question.
12. Delays are written as relative days before they go into lemlist; the absolute-day drawing and the built tree agree.
