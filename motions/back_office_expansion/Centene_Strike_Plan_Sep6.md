**"We built our own scripts / RPA."** RPA runs a task on a trigger. This is about deciding what the floor should be doing right now given conditions across queues. Ask what happens today when two queues spike at once and a third is quiet.

**"Send me something."** Ask one question first so the one page is the right one: is the pressure showing up more as overtime or as turnaround right now? Send accordingly. No Intradiem numbers in whatever gets sent.
