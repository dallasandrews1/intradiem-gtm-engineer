# Back-office campaign copy: three versions compared, and the one to run (Sep 9 2026)

Naveen's Sep 9 ask: update the back-office lemlist messaging from Sierra's doc. Sierra's doc is `BOO_Industry_Outreach_Messaging_Framework_V04.pdf` (12 pages, three tracks, five emails and three LinkedIn touches each). This note puts three versions of the same five campaigns side by side and picks one.

- **Version A**, the copy live in lemlist today: the Sep 2 shells (`BO_Lemlist_Campaign_Copy_Sep2.md`), read back from the five campaigns Sep 9. Mechanism-only, no product, no Intradiem, no number.
- **Version B**, Sierra's V04 framework as written: proof-led, executive-framed, demo ask on every touch.
- **Version C**, what I would write now: Sierra's executive frame and proof set, gate-corrected, on the Sep 3 doctrine and the Sep 4 pressure standard Dallas set on the DWO build.

## 1. Verdict

Run C. It keeps what each of the other two gets right and drops what the gates reject. There is a newer standard than either A or B, and it is ours: the Sep 4 pressure standard (`Messaging_Doctrine_Sep3.md`, addendum) went onto the DWO executive campaign and the UPT attack sequences and never reached the five back-office campaigns, which are still on the Sep 2 shells Naveen read as "not going to get replies." C is those shells rebuilt to the standard the DWO copy already meets, with Sierra's proof and executive question folded in.

Sierra's V04 is not wasted. Its proof set is desktop-productivity proof, which is UPT proof, and its audience is the COO and EVP tier. Both belong in two places we already have: the UPT displacement sequences (Verint and NICE attack, `motions/upt_displacement/`) and the reserved B variants of BO Net-New, where the economic-buyer titles sit. Section 6 says exactly what moves where.

One rule governs the proof: V04 is marketing's sign-off (Dallas, Sep 9: anything Sierra delivers on behalf of marketing is approved as-is). So Optum is named in email and LinkedIn outreach, and the figures ship as marketing wrote them: 18.4 percent productivity within nine months, more than 14 fewer idle hours per associate per month, 15.4X ROI. The healthcare-services story stays blinded ("a healthcare services company"): 90-day pilot, 5.9 percent more active work time, 1,649 active work hours gained. Both recorded in the registry and the Value Repository today. For the file only: the public Optum story page reads 15 percent; marketing's figure governs outreach.
