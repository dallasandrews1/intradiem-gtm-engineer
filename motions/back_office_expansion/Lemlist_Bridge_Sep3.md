# Clay to lemlist contacts bridge (built Sep 3 2026)

One rule: Clay Audiences is the system of record (Salesforce-synced, carries the customer flag); lemlist Contacts is the execution mirror Nate and Jack pull from. Enrichment stays in Clay. lemlist never resolves an address.

## How a person gets to lemlist
1. Set `Lemlist Push Motion` on the Audiences person (`bo_netnew` or `bo_customer`; new motions are added to `lemlist_bridge_config.json` and the node rebuilt).
2. The person enters the segment **Lemlist Push Queue** (`audseg_0tksulu9n6WSaxUpUVB`): motion set, status empty, company Account Type not Customer or Partner, has an email or LinkedIn URL.
3. The bridge workflow (`wf_0tksukwf5aTgJAeDNYp`) re-checks the denylist domains, upserts the contact in lemlist (lemlist dedupes on email or LinkedIn URL), adds it to the motion's list, and stamps `Lemlist Status = pushed` and `Lemlist Contact Id` on the Audiences person. Excluded people get `Lemlist Status = excluded:<reason>` and never reach lemlist.

## Queue people
- One person: `clay workflows runs test wf_0tksuthtP8TEFF2WxHF --inputs '{"email":"<email>","motion":"bo_netnew"}'`
- A list: paste to Claude: `Queue these people for lemlist under motion <bo_netnew|bo_customer>: <emails or a CSV path>` (the agent runs the stamp workflow per row, or the routine bulk path once Clay registers the workflow as a routine).
