## 1. The decision this prototype settles
One sentence. What will we know after people click through it that we do not know from a document? (Example: whether a three-panel skills editor is usable at 1280px, or whether the compare-to-template view belongs in a side panel or a modal.)

## 2. Who uses it and what they are doing
Role, situation, the job they are in the middle of. Two or three lines.

## 3. What the prototype does
Bullet the screens and interactions that exist. Everything not listed here is out of scope for the prototype.

## 4. Data it shows
Where the mock data comes from and what it is standing in for. Name the real records it imitates.

## 5. Design system
Which tokens and components it uses (link the DS reference or list the values). The prototype must not invent chrome.

## 6. Acceptance checks
Numbered, testable. Each one is either checkable by the prototype itself (a self-check panel) or by a person in under a minute. Aim for 5 to 8.

## 7. Open questions for the review
The two or three things the room decides on the prototype. Write them as questions.

## 8. Decision record (after the review)
Date, who was in the room, what was decided per question in section 7, what changes in the next iteration. This section is the record engineering builds from.

## 9. Out of scope
What the prototype deliberately does not do.
