01 · The problem

Supervisors own three things and can see none of them in one place.

The signals already exist. They sit in four separate systems, on four different grains, and none of them were built for a supervisor. Click each responsibility to see what it costs when it stays invisible.

  • The costBurnout that surfaces only after attrition has already landed. Replacing an agent costs far more than retaining one, and the early signals were in the data the whole time.
  • The costCoaching outcomes that depend on which supervisor read the spreadsheet, not what the data said, with no consistent peer or cohort benchmark to separate a real outlier from normal variation. Consistency becomes a matter of luck.
  • The costAdoption gaps that stay invisible to managers above the supervisor. The platform quietly underdelivers and nobody knows where or why.

02 · How it works

One dashboard. Two layers. Click each step for the deck's description.

Four steps in a continuous optimization loop.

  • Deck textPeer-relative cohort scoring: agent ranked against teammates on same channel, queue, and tenure band. Operational metrics only, no PII enters the prompt.
  • Deck textRecommendation appears inline on the dashboard. Supervisor accepts, declines, or modifies. Auto-apply available for low-touch actions where the customer opts in.
  • Deck textRe-evaluation on a configurable cadence. If performance improves, the model logs success. If not, it sequences the next action, never repeating a failed recommendation.
  • Deck textEvery step is logged: timestamp, action, confidence, rationale, and outcome. Full audit chain re-constructable end-to-end for AI governance review.
