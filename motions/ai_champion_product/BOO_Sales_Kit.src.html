05 · Objection handling

Four objections, four talk tracks.

  • Talk trackBack Office Optimizer sits above your existing WFM and BPM systems. It makes them operationally intelligent without replacing them. Those tools measure; Back Office Optimizer prevents. If they were solving your SLA problem, you wouldn't be having this conversation.
  • Talk trackThe beta is 90 days at no cost. The question is whether not solving this has a cost. A customer with $1M in annual SLA penalties who delays six months loses $500K in the wait. Let's build the business case together.
  • Talk trackBack Office Optimizer uses standard connectors to your existing WFM and case systems. No rip and replace. It sits above your stack, not inside it. Your IT team approves an API layer, not a platform migration.
  • Talk trackManual management means your best analysts spend 30% of their day on tactical re-skilling decisions. Ask them what they'd rather do with that time. And ask your COO how many SLA misses last quarter were caught before the breach, not after.

Why buy now

Regulatory

Rising SLA exposure

Penalty regimes intensify year on year. Boards want operations to prove compliance, not report it after the fact.

Workforce

Skill scarcity is structural

The specialist pool is shrinking. Firefighting burns out the experts you have.

Technology

AI demands orchestration

Blended human and bot teams are already live. Without orchestration, AI workers idle or create new bottlenecks.

Competitive

Cost of delay is real

Every quarter of delay is another quarter of avoidable penalties, overtime and attrition.
