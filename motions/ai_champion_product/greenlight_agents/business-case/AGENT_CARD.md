# Business Case Builder

**Name:** Business Case Builder

**Gallery description:** Turns a discovery call or a set of workforce numbers into the one-page Economic Impact Statement an Economic Buyer signs off on, with every Intradiem figure sourced from the value repository and every projection showing its math.

**Who it is for:** Account Executives, Solutions Consultants, and Customer Success leads preparing for a CFO, COO, VP Finance, or VP/SVP Operations conversation. Works for contact-center and back-office workforces across all six verticals.

**Knowledge files to attach (owner attaches):**
- Intradiem_Value_Repository.md (required; without it the agent produces no Intradiem figures)
- Customer_Value_Registry.md (optional; used only for expansion cases to an existing customer's own buyers)

**Example prompts:**
1. "Build the business case for Regions Bank. Economic Buyer is the COO. Here is the transcript from Thursday's call." (paste transcript)
2. "ROI for a claims back office: 640 processors, 9% idle, 14-day backlog, $58K loaded cost per FTE, 22% attrition. Vertical is Insurance. Buyer is the CFO."
3. "Economic impact statement for our Cleveland Clinic patient-access team. Here are my call notes and the numbers they shared." (paste notes)

**What the agent will not do:**
- Produce an Intradiem outcome figure that is not in the attached value repository, or use one from memory
- Attach a dollar, hours, or percent unit to a customer's CTO figure, or cite one customer's realized value to another account
- Present a 1:1 or CONFIRM-tier figure as safe for a deck, webinar, or wide send
- Frame WFM vendors as competitors or Intradiem as a call-center tool
- Write outreach emails, discovery scripts, or competitive comparisons
- Read transcripts, CRM, or the web on its own; it works only from what you paste
