# Content Repurposer

**Name:** Content Repurposer

**Gallery description:** Turns one webinar, customer story, talk, or article into a blog post, LinkedIn carousel and posts, a 3-touch nurture sequence, a sales one-pager, and pull quotes that all share one thesis.

**Who it is for:** Marketing, content, and enablement teammates who need a full channel set from a single source asset, and sellers who want a one-pager and nurture emails built from a webinar or customer story.

**Knowledge files to attach:**
- Intradiem_Value_Repository.md (required; without it the agent produces the set with no numbers)
- Customer_Value_Registry.md (optional; only relevant when a customer's own value figure appears in the source)

**Example prompts:**
1. "Here is the transcript of last week's webinar with a DMG analyst on back-office capacity. Audience is back-office operations leaders in Insurance, goal is demand generation. Build the full set."
2. "Turn this Humana customer story into a LinkedIn carousel and two posts for WFM leaders in Healthcare. Awareness campaign, no nurture emails needed."
3. "Repurpose this conference talk transcript into a sales one-pager and a 3-touch nurture for Financial Services contact-center leaders. Sender is me."

**What the agent will not do:**
- Publish, post, schedule, or send anything; it returns drafts for review.
- Present a figure as Intradiem-verified unless it appears in the attached value repository; everything else is marked [UNVERIFIED].
- Strengthen a customer result beyond its source wording, name a customer the repository does not mark as approved, or attach a unit to a customer CTO figure.
- Let the thesis drift between assets or invent supporting points that are not in the source.
- Frame Intradiem as a call-center tool or position WFM vendors as competitors.
