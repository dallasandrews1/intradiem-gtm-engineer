# Signal to Play

**Name:** Signal to Play

**Gallery description:** Paste one account signal (leadership change, earnings, hiring surge, new system, outage, regulatory deadline, intent spike, merger) and get a Marketing campaign brief, a short Sales alert, and a personalized outreach draft that all act on the same trigger.

**Who it is for:** Sellers and account managers who spot a signal on a target or customer account, and marketing or demand-gen when a triggered campaign is needed in the same week. Built so both teams work from one "why now" thesis instead of discovering the signal days apart.

**Knowledge files to attach:**
- Intradiem_Value_Repository.md (required; without it the agent produces no numbers)
- Customer_Value_Registry.md (optional; internal-only figures, used only for outreach back to that same customer when cleared)

**Example prompts:**
1. "Truist, Financial Services, back office. New COO announced yesterday, here's the press release: [paste]. Persona is VP Operations. Run the play."
2. "National Grid, Utilities. Major storm outage across three states this week, here's the article: [paste]. Persona is Director Contact Center Ops and VP Field Operations. Net-new account, no prior touches. Sender is Nathan."
3. "Paychex posted 40 open customer-service roles in one week, here's the listing summary: [paste]. Healthcare-adjacent, contact center. Persona is VP Workforce Management. Give me the brief, the alert, and the email."

**What the agent will not do:**
- Pull a signal live or check any system. It has no web access or account database; it works only from what you paste.
- Quote a number that is not in the attached Value Repository, put a one-to-one figure in a Marketing brief, or use one customer's figure with another account.
- Produce one or two outputs. It always returns all three, anchored to one why-now thesis, or asks for what it needs first.
- Write in a marketing voice. Outreach uses contractions, a natural CTA, no exclamation marks, and never "I would value 15 minutes."
- Send anything. Every draft is copy for a person to review and send.
- Frame Intradiem as a call-center tool. Every play is matched to the vertical and to contact center, back office, or both.
