01 · Market challenges, the problem

Your ACD routes calls. It cannot protect commitments.

The forecast is built the day before, the operation is staffed to it, and the ACD routes one interaction at a time with no view of the monthly SLA trajectory. Then volume moves, agents call out, handle time drifts.

Three pools of risk, one root cause

Penalty risk

Missed SLAs (B2B), regulatory fees (B2G).

Reputation loss

High abandonment, customer churn risk (B2B, B2C).

Administrative burden

FTE and RTA burden, misallocated analysts, overstaffing, agent burnout.

Routing, not SLA management

Modern ACDs use AI-driven routing to match caller to skill in real time. That optimizes the moment's customer experience, not the period's SLA or penalty trajectory.

Penalties and lost revenue

Missed guarantees trigger contract penalties that can reach millions per month, while abandoned calls bleed revenue and client trust.

Overstaffing as a hedge

Excess headcount gets carried as unavoidable OPEX, a blunt insurance policy against the miss.

Manual firefighting

Analysts spend hours daily watching dashboards and reassigning agents by hand. Most organizations still run SLA management this way.

Wording above is the Product roadmap deck, All Hands 2026. "Millions per month" is the deck's phrase with no source cited there; customer-facing use of any figure goes through the Value Repository.

04 · How it works

Four services, one continuous loop. Click each for the deck's description.

The Recommendation Engine analyzes business goals, forecasts, and ACD and WFM data to decide which agents to add or remove from each queue.

  • Deck textCalls offered and answered, SL, ASA, and average handle time captured and stored to track queue performance and trends over time.
  • Deck textIntraday service level and ASA tracked per queue against monthly and quarterly commitments, prioritizing the highest penalty first.
  • Deck textUser to queue assignments update automatically, with supervisor approval or through full automation, straight into the ACD.
  • Deck textAll agent queue changes revert automatically at shift close. No manual cleanup, no configuration drift.

Business objective (SLA / ASA)

A service level or ASA for each queue in the ACD, along with the associated constraints.
