How to record it

Three things on your Desktop, in this order

  1. Open AllHands Narration Booth.html in Chrome or Safari. It plays the cut on the right and puts the line you are speaking on the left in large type, with the seconds left on that line counting down. Space plays and pauses, R restarts, the arrows step a cue at a time, and clicking a cue jumps there.
  2. Record your voice on its own, not the screen. QuickTime, File, New Audio Recording. One continuous pass from the top. If you fluff a line, pause, breathe, and pick that line up from its start.
  3. Hand me the audio. python3 add_narration.py voice.m4a cleans it, matches it to the picture and writes the narrated file.

The new scenes read themselves. The system walk and the three act openers put their own words on screen, so the lines over them are short on purpose. Say the line, then let the room read. The nine-second silence over the enrichment is still the strongest beat in Act 2.
