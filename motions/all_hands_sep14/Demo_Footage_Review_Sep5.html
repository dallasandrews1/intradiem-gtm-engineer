The fit score

The 82 on screen is not the engine's number.

The plan in the Act 1 take reads "Fit score 82 of 100. Tier 1. Dimension scores from the TAM engine: industry 25, scale 18, tech 25, pain 14." The engine returns 75 of 100, Tier 2, tech 18. The gap is the tech dimension: AmeriHealth Caritas's Avaya and Verint observations are dated 30 January 2024, older than the verified window, so the engine scores tech at 8 plus WFM rather than full partner credit and prints "tech stack unverified, not used in copy" on its own output. The Fable run took full credit anyway and promoted the account a tier.

So the answer on models is no. Fable did not find a number Opus missed, it composed one that sums to a plausible total. Opus leaving the fit score out was the safer failure. The real fix is neither model: run python3 tam-outbound-engine/account_engine.py --plan amerihealthcaritas.com and put its numbers in the plan. The skill now carries that as a rule, so the next run pulls the score instead of writing one.
