## 12. How to talk about the tools (Aug 26 addendum)

You are the orchestrator; the tools are parts. In the room, say "the engine," "our scoring," "the gates," "the loop." Name a tool only in the tooling row of a brief, once, as where something lives: the list lives in Clay, the sequence sends from Lemlist, the count is in Salesforce. Never "Clay does X" as the answer to a question; the answer is what the engine does and what you decided. Two reasons: John bought 6sense and hears "Clay is the answer" as "your tool was wrong"; and if Clay is the workhorse, ZoomInfo looks redundant and so do you. The map's center box now reads "GTM Engineering: score, gate, route, sequence" with "runs on Clay" as a footnote, and the brief page follows the same rule.
