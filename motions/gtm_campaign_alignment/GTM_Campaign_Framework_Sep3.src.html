It starts as one page

The seven rows at the top are the agenda. The three at the bottom are how we count it.

Type into any row; the draft stays in this browser. "Load the Stars example" shows what a finished one looks like, and "Copy brief" puts the text on the clipboard for email, Slack, or the Zoom agenda doc.

Target market01Vertical, segment, size, and the list it comes from. Customers in or out, and who clears them.
Roles and titles02The VITO for this market, and the champion thread beneath them. Proposed per vertical, the AE confirms.
Data collected03The fields every contact carries before it enters a sequence: fit, trigger, verified email, LinkedIn, reports-to, Salesforce owner.
Key signals04What makes an account fire now, with a freshness window. Signals are config, so they change without a rebuild.
Tooling05Where the list lives, where it sends, where it's counted.
Marketing support06Content, ad audience, landing page, and who owns budget and creative.
Execution07How the SDR sees leads, the cadence (touches, channels, days), and what we expect: spend, meetings, conversion, opps, against the Cold baseline.
Gates08 · same for every campaignCustomer exclusion, verified claims only, a person approves the first sends.
Tracking09 · same for every campaignLead Source value proposed as GTM Engineering, one Salesforce campaign per sequence, meetings logged monthly in Sales Ops' schema.
Owner and next read10One name, and the date the results come back to this group.
Then it runs

Once it's written, it runs the same way every time.

A wave is 30 to 50 contacts and takes about nine business days, so every campaign is back at this meeting with a read before the next one. Who does what along the way is my proposal; this group sets it.

  • 1
    Brief
    Anyone brings it · I write it
    The ten rows above, reviewed here, with an owner and the date it comes back.
  • 2
    Build the list
    GTM Eng
    We source it, take current customers out, verify every email, and give it a Salesforce campaign name. What you get back is a count.
  • 3
    Confirm the targets
    AEs · AMs
    The AE tells us who the right VITO is in each account. Inside a customer, the AM clears or holds each name.
  • 4
    Messaging
    Marketing · GTM Eng
    Marketing gives us the options per role and vertical. We build the sequence from them and run every claim through the check.
  • 5
    Run a wave
    SDR
    30 to 50 contacts. Someone signs off the first sends, then the SDR sends, calls, and logs the replies.
  • 6
    Read it
    GTM Eng · Sales Ops
    Replies, meetings, and what each meeting cost, in the council's terms and on its own row.
  • 7
    Decide
    This meeting
    Change the lane, the copy, or the list and run the next wave. Or stop it.

Two weeks later

It's back here with a number.

The bar

$3,402 per meeting

What a Cold meeting costs today, in the council workbook, with 19% of meetings becoming opps. Every campaign reports against that. Targets get set here, after two waves.

The row

Its own line at the council

Each sequence is its own Salesforce campaign, with meetings logged monthly in Sales Ops' schema, so it shows up as its own line. The Lead Source value is a proposal for Sales Ops.

The read

What we bring back

Replies, meetings, and what each meeting cost. Then this group changes the lane, the copy, or the list, or calls it.
