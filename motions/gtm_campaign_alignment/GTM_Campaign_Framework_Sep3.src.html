Before it runs

Write the idea down once, in the agenda's order.

The first seven rows are the agenda from the invite. The last three are what lets the campaign be counted later. Type into any row; the draft stays in this browser. "Load the Stars example" shows a finished one, and "Copy brief" puts the text on the clipboard for email, Slack, or the Zoom agenda doc.

Target market01Vertical, segment, size, and the list it comes from. Customers in or out, and who clears them.
Roles and titles02The VITO for this market, and the champion thread beneath them. Proposed per vertical, the AE confirms.
Data collected03The fields every contact carries before it enters a sequence: fit, trigger, verified email, LinkedIn, reports-to, Salesforce owner.
Key signals04What makes an account fire now, with a freshness window. Signals are config, so they change without a rebuild.
Tooling05Where the list lives, where it sends, where it's counted.
Marketing support06Content, ad audience, landing page, and who owns budget and creative.
Execution07How the SDR sees leads, the cadence (touches, channels, days), and what we expect: spend, meetings, conversion, opps, against the Cold baseline.
Gates08 · same for every campaignCustomer exclusion, verified claims only, a person approves the first sends.
Tracking09 · same for every campaignLead Source value proposed as GTM Engineering, one Salesforce campaign per sequence, meetings logged monthly in Sales Ops' schema.
Owner and next read10One name, and the date the results come back to this group.
While it runs

Then it moves through the same seven steps, one wave at a time.

This is how GTM Engineering runs one. A wave is 30 to 50 contacts and takes about nine business days, so each campaign is back at this meeting with a read and a decision. Owners along the way are as proposed; this group sets them.

  • 1
    Brief
    Anyone proposes · GTM Eng writes
    The ten rows above, reviewed here. Output: a brief with an owner and a read date.
  • 2
    Source and gate
    GTM Eng
    The list, customers out, verified email, one Salesforce campaign name. Output: a count.
  • 3
    Confirm the targets
    AEs · AMs
    AEs confirm the VITO per account; AMs clear or hold names inside customer accounts. Output: cleared names.
  • 4
    Messaging options
    Marketing · GTM Eng
    Marketing supplies options per role and vertical; GTM Eng compiles the sequence through the claims check. Output: a draft sequence.
  • 5
    Launch a wave
    SDR
    30 to 50 contacts, a person approves the first sends, the SDR sends, calls, and logs. Output: a wave live.
  • 6
    Read
    GTM Eng · Sales Ops
    Replies, meetings, cost per meeting, in the council's schema and on its row. Output: a scorecard.
  • 7
    Adjust or retire
    This meeting
    Change the lane, the copy, or the list, then the next wave. Or stop. Output: a decision.
Step 7 starts the next wave, so the loop is two weeks long.

After it runs

And it comes back here with a number.

Baseline

$3,402 per meeting

Cold's cost per meeting in the council workbook, with 19% meeting to opp. Each campaign reports against it; targets are set with this group after two waves.

Row

One row on the dashboard

One Salesforce campaign per sequence and meetings logged monthly in Sales Ops' schema, so each campaign is its own line at the council. The Lead Source value is proposed, with Sales Ops.

Read

Back here in two weeks

Replies, meetings, cost per meeting. The group adjusts the lane, the copy, or the list, or retires the campaign.
