# Back-Office ICP v0 (straw man for the Scott Kemme session)
**Rule this document obeys:** no contact gets sourced before Scott corrects this. It exists to be torn up; his edits become v1, and the first fifty sourced contacts get reviewed together before anything scales.

## The two universes (never conflated in the tables)
1. **Install-base back offices (Mandate 3, the 200):** back-office functions inside companies whose front offices already run Intradiem. Front-office contacts cannot hand these over; we source them. Every row tagged universe = install_base.
2. **Net-new back-office logos (BOO launch universe, Sep 2026 GA):** same personas, no relationship warmth, tighter claims discipline. Tagged universe = net_new. Counts toward the 200 ONLY if universe = install_base, persona-matched, dedup survivor.

## Firmographic hypothesis
Six verticals, 200+ back-office FTEs in transaction-processing functions. Install-base first, weighted by front-office deployment health: before any account enters the pull, check the signal engine; accounts with open risk signals (automation_volume_drop, champion_job_change) or active renewal escalation are suppressed until the AE/CSM clears them. A struggling front office is a bad expansion target.

## Function hypothesis (where the manual work concentrates)
Claims processing and adjudication (insurance, healthcare) · payment ops, disputes, chargebacks (financial services) · order management, returns, fulfillment exceptions (retail) · service order and dispatch processing (telecom, utilities) · document processing, underwriting support, onboarding/KYC (financial services, insurance).

## Persona hypothesis (Director+, first pull)
VP/Director Back Office Operations, Operations Support · VP/Director Shared Services · VP/Director Claims Operations or Claims Transformation · VP/Director Payment Operations · Head of Operational Excellence (secondary; champion, rarely buyer). COO / SVP Operations tier enters later in sequence, never in the first pull.

**Exclusions:** front-office titles, IT/engineering (RPA owners are an objection source, not the buyer), contact-center titles already covered by the relationship, functions under 50 FTEs.

## Pain map (downstream messaging, per persona)
- Claims/payment ops: the backlog that regrows every Monday, aging queues, overtime spend, SLA penalties, rework loops, surge staffing by guesswork.
- Shared services: cost-per-transaction scrutiny, multi-function queues with no real-time visibility, utilization they cannot prove.
- COO tier: capacity without headcount; the back office runs on yesterday's report and the cost lives between the reports.
