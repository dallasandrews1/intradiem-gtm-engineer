# Using the GTM Strike Tool: Rules of the Road

Ask for any target account and you get a ready-to-personalize plan: the buying
committee, why the account is hot now, and a draft sequence for each persona. It does
the blank-page work. You do the judgment. A few things to know before anything goes out.

**1. It's a draft, not a send button.** Nothing sends automatically. You copy what you
want into your normal sender, where the usual review and compliance steps still apply.

**2. Verify the numbers before they leave your hands.** The ROI is an estimate of
recoverable cost, not a promise. The idle-time benchmark and any peer or customer
reference in the copy must be true and approved before a prospect sees them. If you
are not sure a claim is verified, do not send it.

**3. Personalize it.** Fill in the real first name, your number, and anything you know
about the person and the account. The draft is a strong start, not a finished email.
Generic blasts hurt your reply rate and the brand.

**4. Use the trigger, don't fake it.** The "why now" is real signal. If it looks stale
or wrong for an account you know well, trust your read and tell the GTM owner so the
engine gets sharper. Never invent a trigger that isn't there.

**5. You can't change the engine, and that's on purpose.** Thresholds, triggers, data,
and copy live with the GTM owner. Want a change, a new account added, or a different
angle? Send the request to the GTM owner. Do not try to wire up Clay, data feeds, or
config yourself.

**6. Sample data for now.** Until live data is connected, treat every output as a
realistic demo, not confirmed customer truth.

Questions or change requests go to the GTM owner.
