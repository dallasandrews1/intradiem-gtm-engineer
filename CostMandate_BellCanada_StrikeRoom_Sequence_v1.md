## Account snapshot

**Bell Canada / BCE Inc.** Canada's largest communications company. Confirmed non-customer: screened against Nathan's Salesforce Active Customer Account Report (Jul 10, 2026). Bell and BCE do not appear. Royal Bank of Canada and Rogers Communications Canada do appear as customers, so neither may be named in any Bell-facing material.

### The forcing function

On October 14, 2025, at its Investor Day, BCE committed publicly to **$1.5 billion in cost savings by 2028**. Bell's own release names where the money comes from, verbatim:

> "expanding self-serve capabilities, new call centre and digital investments, other improvements to the customer service experience"

and

> "management workforce reductions including attrition and retirements"

This is what makes Bell the sharpest of the three accounts Nathan sent. Most cost mandates have to be inferred from an efficiency ratio or a layoff headline, and the connection to customer operations is ours to argue. Bell put the call centre inside the savings thesis itself, in a dated board-level commitment, with a deadline. The customer-operations executive at Bell is personally accountable for delivering a number the market is already holding the company to.

### The tension the whole sequence hangs on

BCE's own 2025 Annual Information Form (Mar 5, 2026) lists as a risk factor:

> "the potential deterioration in employee morale and engagement resulting from staff reductions, cost reductions or reorganizations, and the de-prioritization of transformation initiatives due to staff reductions"

Bell has told its own investors that the reductions funding the cost program may be draining the capacity to deliver the transformation the cost program depends on. That gap is the entire opening. Capacity recovered from inside the existing headcount is the one lever that books against the mandate without widening that risk, and the one that does not trade against what Bell owes the CRTC by April 2027.

### The vise closing around it

- **CRTC Telecom Regulatory Policy 2026-78** (decided Apr 24, 2026) requires every provider to give customers a self-service mechanism to modify and cancel plans, to send written confirmation of every self-service action, and prohibits charging any fee for access to customer service channels. **Compliance deadline: April 26, 2027.**
- **CRTC 2026-43** (Mar 12, 2026) already removed activation and modification fees, with a June 12, 2026 deadline now passed. The CRTC explicitly acknowledged providers needed transition time to update billing systems.
- Bell must cut cost, improve service, and re-plumb customer-facing operations for a regulatory date, all in the same eighteen months.

### What Bell has disclosed about its own operations

All from the 2025 BCE Integrated Annual Report (dated Mar 5, 2026):

- **"over 240 million customer interactions each year"**
- Virtual Assistant handled **15 million interactions in 2025**, resolving more than half without a live agent
- Virtual Repair Tool has eliminated **over one million support calls** since launch
- **"AI-enabled quality management now supports evaluations for over half of customer-care agents"**

That last line is the most operationally useful sentence Bell has published. It confirms agent-level quality data already exists and is being acted on, and it confirms the coverage is partial. Bell has built the measurement layer and has not yet closed the loop from measurement to action across the whole floor.

Bell is, and the published results are real. The distinction worth drawing is between AI that resolves contacts and AI that orchestrates the workforce handling the contacts that are left. Containment and capacity are different problems and most programs only fund the first.

True, and Bell's Genesys partnership is a commercial business serving Bell's clients. This is about what Bell runs for its own 240 million interactions, which is a different question, and having deep in-house platform competency usually makes the technical evaluation faster rather than slower.
