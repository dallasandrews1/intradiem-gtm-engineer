# Net-New Pilot: You + the Strike Engine

A two-to-three week pilot. You pick the accounts and work them your way. I handle the
research, the committee mapping, and the drafting. We prove one thing together: a real
meeting booked off a strike play.

## Why you

You ran Intradiem from the customer side and you build pipeline here now, so you know
which signals are real and which are noise long before I will. That calibration is the
part I cannot fake, and it is exactly what makes this accurate. I am not here to take
the motion over. I am here to remove the blank-page work around it so you get more
at-bats on better accounts.

## What it does for you

You name a target account and get back, in seconds, a complete play: the buying
committee mapped, a quantified ROI for that floor, the why-now trigger, and a
ready-to-personalize sequence for each persona. No staring at a blank email. You start
from a strong draft and spend your time on the judgment, not the typing.

## The pilot, concretely

- **Scope:** five to eight of your TAM accounts. Your picks.
- **You do three things:** pull the plan, personalize and send it your way, and flag what is off.
- **I do the rest:** keep the research and drafts current, measure what happens, and tune the engine to your feedback.
- **Length:** two to three weeks, enough to work a full cadence and see replies.

## What you get out of it

The blank-page work disappears, you get more at-bats on higher-fit accounts, you get
first crack at the system before the team does, and the win has your name on it.

## The guardrails (so neither of us gets burned)

Every output is a draft, not a send button. The ROI is an estimate to verify, not a
promise. The benchmark and any peer reference are sample numbers until they are
confirmed, so do not send a claim you cannot stand behind yet. Personalize every touch.
Nothing sends automatically; it all goes through you.

## The part that makes it better

When you tell me "this trigger is gold" or "this one is noise on accounts like these,"
that tunes the engine for the whole team. You are not just using the tool in this pilot,
you are shaping the version everyone else gets. Your read is the calibration layer.

## What we are proving

One booked meeting off a strike play inside three weeks. That is the bar. If we clear
it, we have a motion worth scaling. If a play falls flat, that is data too, and it makes
the next one sharper.
