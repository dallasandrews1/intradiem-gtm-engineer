# UK Expansion — Engine Straw-Man v0 (Jul 14 2026)

**Posture:** hypothesis to shape with Steve Rumins and O'Hagan, not a plan. Built in 30 minutes off the Stars pattern the night before the conversation; every data-source claim below gets verified before anything is built. The point of this page is the question set, not the answers.

## The transferable pattern (what Stars actually taught us)

The Star Ratings motion works because of a structure, not because of CMS: **a public, recurring, regulator-published score that (a) ranks companies against each other, (b) carries real money or regulatory consequence, and (c) is partly driven by customer-service operations we credibly move.** Find that structure in the UK and the whole engine forks: same universe table, same grading formulas, same committee pull, same gates, new inputs. One workbook per motion, kill-switch polarity set per market.

## Three UK forcing-function candidates (straw-man ranking)

**1. Water — Ofwat C-MeX (strongest Stars analogue).** Annual customer-experience league table across all water companies, 2025–2030 framework live, with financial rewards and penalties tied to rank. Public rankings, small fixed universe (~17 companies), money attached to customer-service performance. This is structurally the closest thing the UK has to a Stars cliff edge: a bottom-quartile water company is paying for its service scores the way a 3.5-star plan forgoes bonus.

**2. Energy — Citizens Advice star ratings + Ofgem standards.** Quarterly public league table of domestic energy suppliers scored heavily on customer service (call waits, complaints, billing). Recurring cadence (quarterly beats annual for signal freshness), suppliers publicly compete on it, and the measures it scores are exactly the operational surface Intradiem touches. Smaller money hook than C-MeX but faster-moving list.

**3. Financial services — FCA Consumer Duty + published complaints data.** Firm-level complaints data published half-yearly, Consumer Duty board reports now in their second annual cycle, and FOS complaint volumes public by firm. Biggest TAM of the three and the deepest back-office overlap (claims, disputes, collections = the BOO surface), but the "score" is diffuse: no single league table, so the wedge is complaints-trajectory and Duty-outcomes evidence rather than one ranked number.

## Why this isn't starting from zero

- **Capita and Foundever are already in the back-office sample-50 cohort** — both run massive UK operations. The BPO lane is the bridge market: they sell operational efficiency to all three verticals above.
- The engine components that fork with zero credits: universe import + grading formulas, tier logic, committee-pull spec, send gates, ledger discipline, signal columns. Only sourcing and signals spend, and both are priced per the existing steward rules.
- The addressability cut travels too: same honesty test, "is this company's gap in measures we credibly move," just pointed at C-MeX subscores or complaints categories instead of CAHPS.
