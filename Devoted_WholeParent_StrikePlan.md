# Devoted Health: whole-parent strike plan (Star Ratings motion)

## BLUF
Devoted is the cleanest whole-parent fit in the long tail. Twelve sub-4.0 contracts, ~97k members, ~$51.5M gross forgone QBP, ~$37.3M CS-addressable (72% of the gross gap sits in the measures we move). On the four highest-conviction contracts the clinical stars are 4.2 to 4.4 while customer service is 2.6 to 2.8, so the entire reason they miss the bonus is the service side, and service is the brand Devoted sells on. This is a quality-identity play, not a cost-distress play: they raised ~$366M in late 2025 and early 2026 and grew ~71%, so budget and executive attention exist. The wedge is the contradiction between a service-first brand and a sub-4.0 CS score.

## Why now
- Fresh capital and growth strain, not distress. Devoted raised roughly $366M across two tranches in late 2025 and early 2026 and reports serving members across 29 states with ~71% annual growth (per public reporting; sources in `StarRatings_Earnings_Signals_2026.csv`). Rapid multi-state expansion is exactly when service measures slip: new markets, new call volume, new agents. Read the sub-4.0 books as the expansion-market contracts, not the established flagship. Verify per contract on access.
- The brand contradiction. Devoted's whole identity is the member service experience (the guided, high-touch model). A CS Star of 2.7 while clinical sits at 4.2 is the marketing promise and the CMS scorecard pointing in opposite directions. That gap is a reputational exposure and a revenue exposure at once.

## Core narrative (the wedge)
"Your clinical stars already clear 4.0. The only thing holding these contracts under the bonus line is the customer-service measures, CAHPS, complaints, call center, appeals. That is the exact experience your brand is built on, and it is the part operations can move without touching clinical. Across the sub-4.0 book that is roughly $37M a year in forgone bonus, and on several contracts all of it is on the service side."

Routes to the `quality_identity_gap` signal (cx / cc_ops) plus Persona 2 on the dollar. This is not a "you're losing money, cut costs" pitch; they have cash. It is a "your scale is outrunning your service scores, and that contradicts your brand" pitch.

## Sequencing
1. Enter through CX / Member Experience on the identity wedge, referencing the four all-of-it contracts. This owner feels the brand contradiction most.
2. Parallel Persona 1 (Stars/Quality) on the measure-level breakdown for H2923 and H8173 (largest all-of-it contracts).
3. Persona 2 on the aggregated addressable dollar (~$37M) once one of the above engages, framed as the fixable slice.
4. If it converts to a conversation, this is a whole-parent account plan, not a single-contract sequence: one narrative across the book, one set of contract-level one-pagers.

## Proof and disciplines
- External proof, verified: UnitedHealthcare told a federal court that a single failed call-center secret-shopper call cost it $190M in Star bonus payments under the prior rules (that call-center measure is retired for 2028 Stars). Use once, for the skeptic, as historical proof that one CS measure priced real money; never imply the measure still earns bonus.
- Their own numbers carry the message. Do not state any Intradiem performance metric or customer result unless it comes from the approved repository. If no approved claim exists, make none.
- Label every dollar as a public-data estimate from CMS enrollment and ratings.
- Addressability is the credibility. Lead with the addressable slice, concede the clinical remainder, never quote the gross as the movable number.

## Risks and qualifiers
- Growth-strain, not distress. They have capital, so urgency comes from the brand contradiction and the compounding of another cycle out of bonus, not from a cash crisis. Do not use cost-cutting language.
- Confirm product line per contract before sequencing (MAPD vs D-SNP); Devoted runs both in places.
- Small per-contract enrollment on the wedge books means the per-contract dollar is modest; the whole-parent aggregate and the brand story are what make this worth a dedicated plan, not any single contract.
- Verify the expansion-market read once you have access to their contract-level history.
