Star Ratings outreach, 8 samples across the range

Prepared by Dallas Andrews · GTM Engineering · v2.4.1 messaging · 2026-07-22

Eight samples from the Stars engine, chosen to show the range rather than volume: both personas, full-star and half-star gaps, single and multi-contract books, and the clinical-clears-4.0 case. Each email is generated from the plan's own public CMS Star Ratings data.

What's shared vs what's personalized

Every email runs on the same deliberate spine, one service-measure insight, the Dynamic Workforce Orchestration platform line, and a single verified Humana proof framed operationally, so we never overclaim. What changes per plan is the CMS star position, the addressable dollar, the persona angle, and the clinical-vs-service read. The consistency is the guardrail; the plan-specific math is the personalization.
