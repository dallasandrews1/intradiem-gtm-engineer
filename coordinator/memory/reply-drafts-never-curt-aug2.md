Dallas rejected the Aug 1 Marc Giles sample draft that opened "I'm not going to give you a list": "we should never be so rude." Same message: there was still non-human phrasing throughout all the brief copy, and the bar is "if it doesn't sound like something someone would say out loud in a real conversation then it is not natural and will not work," treat every script as something you'd confidently send as yourself.

**Why:** Curt refusals and writerly phrasing both burn the prospect relationship and read as automated. The reps deliver this copy live, on calls and voicemails, so anything that can't be said naturally at talking pace fails in the field, not just on the page.

**How to apply:** When declining to answer a prospect question (like "who else are you working with"), the shape is respect first, then redirect: "fair to ask, I would too" plus an honest reason, then pivot to their situation. Never open with a refusal. For all scripts and drafts: read it aloud mentally; kill noun-stacks ("the movable points this cycle are operational"), antithesis flourishes, and email-register phrasing in anything spoken. The rules are codified in the SPOKEN VOICE section of automation/config/action_brief_format.md (v2.1) with two reference drafts as the tone bar, and mirror [[voice_core]] in the motion-stamp library.
