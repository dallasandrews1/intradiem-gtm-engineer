Dallas, Sep 10 2026, on a Slack draft to Sierra: "shouldn't I try not to tell her what to do or how to do her job? I feel like I'm doing that in the third line." The line was "If you want a completion action, drop it on the lander form." Cut.

**Why:** a peer owns her tool; a suggestion about where to put a rule inside it reads as instruction. The message already removed the per-link work, so her next move was obvious without being named.

**How to apply:** Slack drafts to peers carry two things only, what we're handling and what we need from them. No mechanics of their tool, no explaining their system to them. If they push back, that is when the reasoning gets shared. Extends [[feedback-slack-voice-short-casual]] and [[feedback-no-tasks-for-ams]].
