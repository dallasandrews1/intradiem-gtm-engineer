Dallas, Sep 25 2026, after the four-account build: "ensure you'd send all this as-is if you were me." Four send-as-is reviews (one per account) all returned NOT YET with the same classes of defect.

**Rules, apply before the first build:**
- Alumni "warm door" rows: never JPMorgan Chase, AT&T or Liberty Mutual; at least 12 months overlap with Intradiem live; the guidance column names a customer only when marketing cleared the name (Humana, Optum, Virgin Media), otherwise "use the fact, keep the name out of the note". Cingular is not AT&T.
- Emails: only the account's own domains; when Salesforce holds an on-domain address for the same person and Clay's differs, Salesforce wins (Clay returned jamie.martin@td.com for Jay Martin, whose converted address is jay.martin@td.com). A leaver is anyone whose MOST RECENT current experience is not the home company (Clay's is_current keeps stale rows).
- Rep-facing vocabulary: no door, wave, layer, lane, gate, cohort, motion, record, sponsor line anywhere a rep reads, including sequence page labels ("First group", "Rachel writes, Oct 5", "Warm first calls"). "Worth a note to" becomes "Send a note to".
- The "this week" line and the first suggested step must carry a person, a reason and a ready asset; never "nothing loads until you clear the names" or "your read on the cycle".
- The "Sales Navigator" pill, counter and "In Sales Navigator:" line are hidden only when the maps came from Clay searches with no Sales Navigator list behind them (Rachel, hide_salesnav_status). Keegan's maps are built as Sales Navigator Relationship Maps, so the pill is true there and stays (Dallas, Sep 26 2026: never suggest removing it); no "enriched through Clay" or "Clay" on any page a prospect could read; no 6sense on a rep card.
- First-year rule: start date on or after (today minus 12 months); chips, counts and prose from the same rule.
- Every figure on a one-pager traces to the research file with a primary source; secondary aggregations (Business Standard, Healthcare Labyrinth, KFF) and vendor-announced deal sizes stay off one-pagers; verbatim quotes only.
- The 350,000-professionals tile is cleared 1:many in the verified-metrics skill (intradiem.com); the net-retention tile uses the repository's BusinessWire wording (above 114% in 2025). The repository itself still reads "100 percent plus" and owes both entries.
- Success manager lane suggests ("Ask Lisa Vidal ..."), never assigns dated deliverables.

**Why:** these were caught by review, not by the builders; each one would have gone to a rep as-is. Related: [[feedback-deliver-the-send-version]], [[feedback-talking-points-defend-before-delivery]], [[rachel-four-account-package-sep25]].
