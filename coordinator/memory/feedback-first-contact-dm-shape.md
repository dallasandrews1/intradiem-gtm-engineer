Dallas's real send to Melissa Spies (VP Marketing), Sep 23 2026, handing over the content desk:

> Hey Melissa! Naveen mentioned you'd want to see this: intradiem-content-desk.pages.dev
>
> Quick version: a one-pager builder (pick the product and who it's for, type the company, print) and a LinkedIn card builder (stat cards, announcements, carousels, download the PNG). Just lmk what you think and if there's anything missing or any changes you'd want to make

What he cut from my draft: the sentence pre-defending the presets ("every preset is your team's published wording... nothing you haven't already said") and the specific offer ("send it over and it's in the same day").

**Why:** to a peer, defending the claims reads as justifying, and the guardrails live in the tool, not the message. A first contact invites a reaction (what do you think, what's missing, what would you change), not a task for the reader.

**How to apply:** first-contact DMs handing over a tool or page: opener with the link on line one ("Hey Name! X mentioned you'd want to see this: link"), one "Quick version:" line naming what it does in parentheses, then an open ask ("Just lmk what you think and if there's anything missing"). Three lines. No claims posture, no same-day offer, no explaining who approved what. Related: [[feedback-deliver-the-send-version]], [[feedback-marketing-tools-never-bypass-marketing]].
