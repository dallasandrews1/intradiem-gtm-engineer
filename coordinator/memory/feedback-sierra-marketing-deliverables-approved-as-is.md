Dallas, Sep 9 2026: "whatever Sierra has given me on behalf of marketing should be read as approved as-is."

**Why:** the verified-claims gate exists to keep unapproved numbers and customer names out of prospect copy. Marketing owns that approval, and Sierra is marketing's hand-off. Flagging her figures back to her (as happened with Optum by name and the 18.4 percent productivity figure in the V04 back-office framework) relitigates a decision marketing already made and stalls the copy.

**How to apply:** when a Sierra deliverable names a customer or carries a figure, record it in `04-value-repository/Customer_Stories_Registry_Jul21.md` and `Intradiem_Value_Repository.md` as VERIFIED with the deliverable as the source and the channel scope she stated (V04: email and LinkedIn outreach, never website or public-facing), then use it. Discrepancies against a public page go in the ledger as a note, never as a blocker. House doctrine on sequence structure (Email 1 question, no calendar, one breakup, dated opener) still applies; marketing approval covers claims and content, not cadence.
