Built Sep 21 2026 on top of [[partner-event-poster-sep20]], after Dallas agreed the ask-first stage posts were the wrong shape for a channel Naveen and Haresh now read.

- Flow: `run_partner_events.sh` calls `run_partner_deliver.sh` first (`partner_deliver.py --sync`, then `--apply`), then the poster. Manifest `motions/partner_channel/briefs/deliver_manifest.json`, statuses queued, held, staged, deployed. `--sync` flips staged to deployed by itself once both URLs answer 200. Config block `deliver` in `automation/config/partner_events.json` (`live: false`, `require_record_read: true`), plus `stage_group_max`.
- Poster: a non-pilot record at Delivered 12 Minute Meeting with no deployed asset goes to a BUILD QUEUE log section and posts nothing. With an asset the post carries `[Account brief] · [one-pagers]` and asks nothing. Two or more stage events in a run go out as ONE message; the log shows one `key:` plus a `members:` line, and `--mark-posted` on that key marks every member, so the unattended posting prompt did not need editing. Weekly cap now counts messages (distinct ts), not keys. `--replay --assume-record-read` previews a deliver post.
- Customer gate: union of the Salesforce customer segment, the Jul 10 active customers export, the denylist and the install base. Category "Expand" on a pre-pipeline record is the partner's own category (83 of 251 records), not a customer flag.
- Fidelity Investments (record "CapGemini - Fidelity", AE Keegan Sanders): brief JSON `briefs/data/fidelity.json`, partner-safe page staged at `deploy-partner-pilot/briefs/fidelity.html`, two one-pagers (service-operations, workforce-platform) staged under `deploy-save-rooms/fidelity/frank/one-pagers/`, claims in `kit/CLAIMS_LEDGER_frank.md`. Lead finding: a LIVE Fidelity posting (jobs.fidelity.com 2127334, posted Aug 20) says "Fidelity is building a modern, in-house Workforce Management platform" at "its current design stage". Build-in-house account: alongside, never instead of.
- Research hygiene that mattered: the three signal-researcher passes disagreed (800 vs about 1,000 cuts; 3,300 vs 5,300 hires; 9.8M calls as half-year vs quarter), two cited job postings were 404 by the time they were opened, and a "25,000 employees" figure was not in the article cited for it. Only lines re-opened through WebFetch went on a page. WebFetch is a small model's reading, so a person re-opens the three Fidelity pages before a customer sees a one-pager.
- OPEN: (1) nothing deployed; `automation/deploy_rep_pages.sh pilot` (new target) then `rooms`. (2) `frank/fidelity` is registered with the freshness gate and reads MISSING, so the post is held until the record page is read and marked. (3) No name below the executive layer is live-checked; five profile-only names are internal-only. (4) Amerihealth Back Office and BCD Travel are queued, not built. (5) The unattended build session was DENIED by the auto mode classifier ("Create Unsafe Agents"); builds are attended until Dallas adds a Bash rule himself.

**How to apply:** "build the deliver-first package for <slug>" is the attended task: three researcher passes, re-open every line used, JSON on the Ally schema, `build_briefs.py <slug>`, a shelf block in `shelf_frank.py`, `build_shelf_generic.py`, ledger rows, then `partner_deliver.py --stage <slug>`. Never flip `deliver.live` or deploy without Dallas's explicit ask.
