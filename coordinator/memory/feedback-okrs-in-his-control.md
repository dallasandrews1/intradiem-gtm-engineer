Dallas's Sep 2 2026 instruction on his OKR draft: "make sure it's something in my control and achievable so I don't put myself in a position of weakness or trouble."

**Why:** HR treats OKRs as compliance items (Naveen lost 25 percent on a missed webinar), and a key result that hinges on a contract, an AE, an AM's clearance, or another team's adoption becomes a miss he cannot prevent. Naveen also said not to inherit his 15-meetings line.

**How to apply:** every key result is a shipped artifact or a discipline run (readiness, gates, cadence, packaging, handoffs), ends at the handoff when the next step belongs to someone else, and bakes in a few already-done items so the first check-in banks credit. Meetings, pipeline, and adoption are reported in the Friday readout, never written as targets. Draft v2 in motions/shared/OKRs_Dallas_Draft_Sep2.md.
