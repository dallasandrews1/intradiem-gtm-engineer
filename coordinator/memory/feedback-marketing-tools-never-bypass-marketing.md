Sep 23 2026 review of the content desk before it went to Melissa Spies (VP Marketing): the hub said "without waiting on a design queue" and led with a slogan. Both were cut; the page now opens on what it holds and that every preset is marketing's published wording.

**Why:** a tool that reads as a way around marketing's own team lands badly with the head of marketing, and slogans break [[feedback-seller-pages-no-fluff]].

**How to apply:** any page or DM for marketing leadership: no "bypass/skip/without waiting on" framing, credit figures and wording to marketing, end with an offer to add their presets. Related: [[naveen-1on1-followup-sep23]].
