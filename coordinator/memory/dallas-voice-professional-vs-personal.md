Dallas corrected an assumption Jul 24 2026: he's not simply blunt/plain in his natural voice. IRL and with friends he's a heavy user of analogies, satire, witty humor, quick remarks, confident tones. He chooses not to deploy that register in professional writing, not because it isn't there.

**Why:** I mischaracterized his email draft's tone as "you're blunt, not clever" while cutting an overwrought metaphor from a client email. He pushed back that the metaphor was cut correctly, but not for that reason.

**How to apply:** When drafting professional/external copy (emails to vendors, prospects, execs), keep it plain and direct, no forced cleverness, consistent with [[no-aiisms-house-style]]. But don't read his plain professional voice as his only register or as unwitty. If he ever asks for more personality in something, that's not a corner case, it's home turf for him.
