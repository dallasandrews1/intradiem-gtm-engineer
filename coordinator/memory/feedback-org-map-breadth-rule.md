Dallas, Sep 25 2026: Freymann's and Combs's reports "shouldn't be one long line like that". Sep 26, on the re-tree: "they should be the traditional org chart format we have been operating with the past few weeks, tiered from C suite to VP then director then manager", "no one would know why we split the sections of people into different org charts separated by perforated lines", "Keegan's org chart page is the standard to have. Split each org into front office and back office, and sub categories for line of business wherever applicable".

**What went wrong twice:** the Sep 25 fix invented sub-leads by title (a five-deep chain of single cards) and a 3-column leaf grid with a left rail; ten single executives rendered as ten dashed-line blocks. Keegan's pages run the same renderer and never hit any of it because his trees are shaped right.

**Rule:** every account is two maps, Front Office and Back Office. Each map is labeled blocks (executive leadership, contact centers, workforce, the lines of business, fraud and claims, shared services); a block's top people sit side by side, small trees wrap, each tree is one leader with two to six reports in a row and their reports beneath (block_labels in the set, "block" column on the sheet, INTRO line per map). A leader with more than six all-leaf reports renders as balanced wrapped rows. Never a chain of single cards, never a separate block per singleton executive. Check the render before hand-over.
