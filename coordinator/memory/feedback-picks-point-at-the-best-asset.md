Sep 24 2026, reply to Jen Lee's "pick two of your five ideas" note. First draft picked the right two but wrote pick 2 as "a back-office map for every customer", so the rep index pages (Keegan's map, room, brief, one-pager shelf, sequence) never appeared. Dallas: "would you really send this as-is? I would have thought the account master pages for Keegan would've been more impressive."

**Why:** the pages could not be a pick (her note was ideas-only; done work is scored in a separate email), but the idea IS the scaling of that work, so the honest and stronger version names Keegan's page as the bar and links it. The first thing she opens should be the best thing built.

**How to apply:** when a constraint on the reply keeps the strongest asset out of the headline, make an eligible item point at it as the standard. Never let the best work sit behind the weakest word for it ("map"). Related: [[spot-bonus-program-sep18]], [[feedback-check-whats-built-before-proposing]], [[feedback-keegan-room-is-the-bar]].
