Confirmed Sep 21 2026 (cms.gov, Groom Law Group, MedCity, Clover 8-K). Clover Insurance Co. v. HHS, S.D. Ga., May 27 2026: 10 Star measures exceeded statutory authority (data outside HEDIS, HOS, CAHPS: call center C33/D01, appeals decisions C32, medication adherence x3, Rating of Drug Plan, Getting Needed Drugs, MTM, statin use) and 10 more skipped notice and comment (including Customer Service C24, Getting Needed Care, Getting Care Quickly, Care Coordination). Jun 17 2026 CMS memo: voluntary recalculation of 2027 QBP ratings for every MAO using only HEDIS, HOS and CAHPS data, hold harmless (up only), appeal rights reserved, "not definitive policy". Elevance sued Jul 2026 over five excluded contracts ($115M claimed). This is why 34 universe contracts changed in the re-issued 2026 Summary Ratings, see [[stars-refresh-rehearsal-sep21]].

**Why:** the Stars motion's "which measures still pay" story (Measure Reconciliation tab, verified Jun 30 2026 against the CY2027 final rule only; Targeting Flags section 3) is stale on complaints, appeals, TTY and disenrollment. What measure set the October 2026 release (2027 Star Ratings) will use was NOT found.

**How to apply:** never state which measures "still count" in Naveen-facing or prospect copy until the reconciliation is redone against Clover and the October technical notes. Being current on Clover is itself a credibility signal with a plan's Stars team. Dallas also established Sep 21 2026 that the Stars data edge is public-data work and speed, not proprietary knowledge; the plan sees its own measure data in the September preview. The literal law-1 asset is what Intradiem knows from its own payer customers (Humana quotables are cleared).
