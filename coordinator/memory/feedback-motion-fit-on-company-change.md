Dallas, Aug 31 2026, while cleaning the Stars - Resurrection (Nate) campaign: "this campaign is solely focused on star ratings, so if the contacts moved to a company where that's no longer applicable, then they need to be removed as well." Applied to Candace Lowman (ATRIO -> Allymar Health Solutions, a BPaaS/BPO vendor for MA plans).

**Why:** A campaign's copy and hooks assume the buyer's company lives with the motion's problem (Star Ratings attach to plans, not their vendors). A lead at the wrong kind of company gets irrelevant copy, and a BPO/vendor contact is a partner or competitor conversation, not a prospect for that campaign.

**How to apply:** On any departure sweep, check the new employer against the motion's buyer set, not just "still employed". Same company group but a different entity still counts as a move; decide on whether the new entity is itself a motion buyer. When unsure what the new company is, look it up before deciding.
