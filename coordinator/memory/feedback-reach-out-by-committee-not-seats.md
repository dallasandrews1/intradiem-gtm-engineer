When John, Naveen, or any leader asks how many people a motion reaches, size it as the buying committee at each account: the role holders actually found (WFM/real-time champion, contact-center ops owner, performance or op-ex evaluator, back-office leader where a BO estate exists, CC technology gatekeeper, VITO via the DWO Executives campaign). Roles present vary by account (0 to 5 on the UPT tier-A set), so the total is a sum of committees, not accounts times a seat number.

**Why:** Dallas rejected "3 to 4 seats per account" on Sep 10 2026 for the UPT displacement numbers; a quota reads as spray, a committee reads as a plan and matches the ICP buying-committee work leadership already bought into.

**How to apply:** compute per-account role presence from the Audiences segments (0 credits, search-count with a lane title group plus org_name), report owners plus deputies where a lane has five or more candidates, and flag accounts with zero role holders as sourcing gaps. UPT worked example in motions/upt_displacement/lists/committee_counts_sep10.json. Related: [[upt-displacement-verint-nice-sep9]], [[naveen-icp-committee-feedback-aug13]].
