Aug 5 2026, Genna reply draft: Dallas caught a closing line ("no sends means none of these outcomes can show up either way") as reading like a deflection.

**Why:** the dependency was real and belonged in the message, but ending on absence pre-excuses a miss before anyone has asked about one. "Either way" is fatalistic and "until that's sorted" is passive with no owner, so the whole close reads as hedging instead of as information the reader needs. Last sentence carries disproportionate weight in a skim.

**How to apply:** name the dependency, name the owner and date, then close on what is already built and waiting on it. Genna version: "The lemlist case is with Naveen now and the trial runs out Aug 13. Campaigns are staged and ready to go the day that lands, so that's really what sets when outcomes start showing up." Same fact on the record, forward-facing exit. Applies to any blocker surfaced to leadership or peers, including the Friday readout risk lines. Pairs with the house rule against finished-product framing: head start plus what is next, never absence as the final note.
