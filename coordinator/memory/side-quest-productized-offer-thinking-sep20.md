Dallas asked (Sep 20 2026) what to do if Joey Fogle and Kathryn Anderson decide the side quest's end goal is an out-of-the-box white-glove offering that lifts adoption of ALL of a customer's AI tools. Not a decision yet; this is the recommendation on file.

Recommendation: a product-led service. Vendor-neutral data contract in (any AI tool that can export roster + usage), the adoption engine fires triggers, Kathryn's playbooks are the interventions, and the moat is delivering those interventions in the flow of work through Intradiem's own real-time rules engine, then proving lift at 14 days. Three tiers: Assess (CSA + baseline), Run (90-day sprint), Orchestrate (interventions delivered through the Intradiem platform, the only tier that needs it, so it drives expansion). Agents work for the adoption lead on Intradiem's side (drafting manager cards, readouts), never inside the customer's network. Rejected: USB or agent swarm in the customer network (security review kills it, and adoption is a behavior problem, not a build problem) and a standalone consulting team (does not scale, competes with the big firms).

Charter mapping that protects the $10K credit: internal pilot = customer zero; intervention framework = playbook library; telemetry = engine + dashboard + data contract; exec presentation = the commercial briefing pitching the offer with the pilot's lift number. Full commercial product stays out of scope, as set at the Aug 18 kickoff.
