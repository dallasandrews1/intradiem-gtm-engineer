Dallas, Sep 22 2026, after the strike room handover: "my job is to be the expert and authoritative decision maker when it comes to the things I create for them after I have context etc. so let's just make the exec decisions on the things that we can anyway."

I had closed the handover with three items framed as waiting on other people. Two of them were mine to decide with the data already in hand.

**Why:** his authority with Naveen and the reps comes from arriving with a decision and its reasoning, not with options. Handing him an open question he has the context to answer himself spends his credibility and his time. It also stalls work that could move today.

**How to apply.** After gathering context, decide, act, and report the decision with the evidence under it. The test is whether the data in front of me settles it. If it does, it is not an open item.
- **Vanguard:** a stage-one partner registration untouched for 233 days with a disqualified sibling is not a live introduction. Decided it is dead, Naveen calls cold, the AE gets a courtesy note rather than a permission request. It stopped being a gate.
- **Blue Shield of California:** the July room was not stale, it was wrong. Retired it rather than re-cutting, and moved the account to the cost-mandate lane. See [[bsca-retired-from-stars-sep22]].
- **What legitimately stays open:** only what genuinely needs another person's knowledge, like Nate's actual BCBS NC handle-time number. Those get registered in a log the daily rundown carries, with a stop date, never left in Dallas's head ([[sf-freshness-gate-sep21]]: he will not be the fallback memory).

Decisions still get stated as decisions with the reasoning visible, so he can overturn one in a sentence. That is different from asking him to make it.
