Sep 4 2026, Dallas on the DWO drafts: admitting you don't know the org ("I can't tell whose desk this lands on", "if this is the wrong desk, a name is all I need") reads as not having done the research and backfires. Constant apologizing and repeated exit lines ("one question and I'll stop", "I'll leave you be", "closing this out", "either answer helps me stop guessing") are wasted words.

**Why:** the pool is mapped (families, seats per account, titles). The sender is expected to know who owns what. Pressure works when every touch moves forward with something the prospect can use; exit talk signals the sender expects to fail.

**How to apply:** state what you know ("{{colleagueFirst}} owns the shared-services side of this, so I've sent the same note there"), never what you don't. No "sorry", "fair enough", "I'll be quick", "I'll stop", "no need to reply" in any touch. Exactly one breakup email at the end of the arc; every other step asks for the next thing (a call, a week, an answer). Direct asks ("twenty minutes before open enrollment, which week works?") are fine after Email 1. Related: [[feedback-calls-first-net-new]], [[nate-campaign-structure-review-sep4]].
