Two corrections Dallas gave (Jul 7 2026) building the Clay + Golden List brief for Naveen, both now standing rules for any Naveen-facing (or forward-to-leadership) deliverable:

1. **Never frame anything as telling Naveen what to do.** No "what I need from you," no "decisions/asks," no imperative verbs (Ratify / Open / Confirm). Reframe every such item as a thread to shape *together* and where Dallas would value Naveen's read. Naveen defined the role; Dallas is a peer surfacing choices, not issuing instructions.

2. **Exec showcase pieces are not seller pieces.** Cut external customer proof points (e.g. the Intercom ~$1M / Rippling 2x band). They turn a showcase of what Dallas built into an outbound sell. Keep showcase stats that describe the engine itself (universe size, credit budget, addressable-dollar model); drop "look what this does elsewhere" social proof.

**Why:** the point of these docs is to demonstrate the GTM Engineer's impact and competence, not to sell Naveen or boss him. **How to apply:** on any Naveen/leadership-facing deliverable, scan for directive framing and for borrowed proof, and strip both before delivering.
