On 2026-08-17, while prepping the GTM Engine Room page for Naveen, Dallas said: "stop mentioning anything revolving around failures or what we fixed. that will kill our credibility."

**Why:** internally, catch-and-fix stories read as the system working; to leadership they read as instability. The audience changes the meaning of the same fact.

**How to apply:** in ANY leadership-facing deliverable (Naveen, execs, partnerships, forwardable pages, readouts), describe controls and discipline in the present tense as capabilities ("customer-exclusion audited weekly on real rows"), never as incident narratives ("caught a misconfigured gate", "fixed the blackout", "hardened after crashes"). Scrub words like caught, fixed, failure, crash, leak, outage, misconfigured, hardened-after. Incident history lives in internal logs and the internal Atlas only. This refines [[naveen-icp-committee-feedback-aug13]] and applies to the Friday readout drafts too: risks can be flagged early per the operating posture, but as forward-looking risks, not past failures.

**Sep 4 2026 extension (Dallas, verbatim):** "never put anything that was a mistake or a surprise or anything that dilutes my value and strength." This covers every deliverable AND the chat summary around it: no cost surprises, no "copy lacked depth", no conditions still open, no "blocker in front of" framing. Asks are written as what Dallas will do the moment the input lands, never as what is holding him. Applied to the 15Five backfill page Sep 4.
