Dallas, Sep 26 2026, on the DWO executive review canvas: "don't explain what we fixed at the bottom, just talk about what we need from them."

**How to apply:** the closing block of any review doc handed to colleagues is the ask (score, the line you'd change, who owns which tab). What changed and why goes in `automation/logs/` and the canon entry, not on the page. Same rule as [[feedback-no-fluff-scannable-deliverables]] and [[feedback-lead-with-what-dallas-must-do]], applied to group-facing review docs.
