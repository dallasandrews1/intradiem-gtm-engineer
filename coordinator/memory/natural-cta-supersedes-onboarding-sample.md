Dallas rejected the original Point32 (Kristen Dowd) sample message that the intradiem-first-draft-engine skill calls "the gold-standard sample from Naveen's onboarding site." Its CTA ("If useful, I would value 15 minutes to hear how you are approaching your cliff edge contracts") is seller-hero framing in unnatural AI-register English. That version is SUPERSEDED as of Jul 9 2026 by the rewrite now in Norton_GTM_Preread.html / norton-preread-deploy and Message_Variant_Starter_Pack.md (variant 1A).

**Why:** the entire point of the outreach is that the prospect sees value in meeting us, not that we state what we want from them. "I would value 15 minutes" states the sender's want. Humans also contract: I'd, I'm, you'll, never "I would" / "I am" in cold copy.

**How to apply:** in all Intradiem outreach copy and any MessageGen/variant work: (1) always contractions; (2) CTA patterns like "Thought it might be worth finding time to ___. Have 15 minutes on ___?" or "Might be worth 15 minutes on ___. Open to it?"; (3) never "I would value / I'd value 15 minutes"; (4) never "trade notes" / "trading notes" / "compare notes" / "comparing notes" — use natural verbs like "talk through" or "walk through"; (5) samples shown in docs must be formatted as an actual email (subject, greeting, body, sign-off in the sending rep's name, e.g. Nathan). Keep the humility clause and public-data labeling.
