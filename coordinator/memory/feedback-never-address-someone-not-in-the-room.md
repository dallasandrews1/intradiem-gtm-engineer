Dallas, Sep 22 2026, on the Vanguard strike room and its Slack handover: "we can't have anything addressing keegan."

I had written a question addressed to Keegan Sanders in §0 of the room ("Keegan: is Don Cousineau's intro still alive...") and repeated it in a group DM Keegan is not in. Wrong on both counts: a document Naveen reads should not contain a question aimed at someone else, and a message to Naveen and Nathan should not carry an action item for a third party who will never see it.

**Why:** it reads as talking about a colleague rather than to him, it leaves an action nobody owns, and in a rep-facing document it makes the reader responsible for chasing someone else. Same family as [[feedback-partner-channel-is-not-for-aes]]: name the AE on a line, never route through them in someone else's document.

**How to apply:** an AE, partner or colleague who is not a recipient appears only as a stated fact ("Keegan Sanders is AE of record on both partner records. Any reply from a VP or above goes to him the same day"). The action that needed them becomes Dallas's own line: "I'm confirming with the AE today so you're not guessing." Never a quoted question to an absent person, never "ask X to...", in any deliverable or message.
