Sep 11 2026, on the first Inger-facing Cleveland Clinic Save Room: "that's not helpful to tell her or give her a list of contacts and emails that aren't already verified... defeats the purpose of me helping her with this whole thing. also, does this add value to her while simultaneously not creating another separate tool to remember to use?"

**Why:** the point of GTM Engineering helping an AM is removing work, not relabeling it; an unverified contact is an ask in disguise, and a page she has to remember to open is a new tool.

**How to apply:** (1) verify every contact before it reaches an AM page or note: free web check for role and LinkedIn URL, then the Work Email routine; a name that cannot be verified stays internal, a departed or retired name is stated as such and removed. (2) The AM never opens anything to get value: the Monday Outlook note carries their open items, the account status, and the verified contacts; the page is the link behind the note; the PMO list stays their record. (3) The hero says "nothing new to remember." Pairs with [[feedback-no-tasks-for-ams]] and [[feedback-deploy-every-page]].

**Addendum (Sep 11, gap review):** before an AM page ships, re-run the gap check as the AM and her SVP: every named recipient current (free bridge or LinkedIn), no merge fields or bracketed placeholders visible, a line saying drafts are for each sender to own, no internal tool names in evidence labels, and a marketing brief that names the real tool (Pardot) and lands after the meeting that confirms the lanes. A returned email that breaks the account's address pattern is rejected, not shown.
