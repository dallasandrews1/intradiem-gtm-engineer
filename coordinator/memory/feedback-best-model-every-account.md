Sep 1 2026, while setting model guidance for Frank's Partner Account Engine project: Dallas rejected "Sonnet for daily use, big model for must-win accounts" on principle: "any and all accounts really matter unless we find out otherwise, and I don't want to have missed something by assuming they don't."

**Why:** the cost of a missed trigger (a lost meeting inside a 45-day window) dwarfs token cost; nobody knows which account matters until the research is done, so tiering by assumed importance builds the miss in.

**How to apply:** for account research, brief generation, and anything where missed findings have business cost, the guidance is always "strongest model available, every run." Cost-tiering advice is reserved for mechanical or repeated tasks where output quality is capped anyway. Related: in shared projects, tell users to NAME the skill in the prompt ("Use the partner-account-brief skill: ...") instead of relying on trigger phrases; deterministic invocation beats convenience while trust is being built.
