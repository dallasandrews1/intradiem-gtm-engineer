On 2026-07-22 Dallas shared a 10-fact sales belief set (Nate Nasralla / Fluint "Selling
With" lineage) and asked to weave it into the overarching GTM-systems strategy. This is a
standing lens for the engine, not a one-off.

**The 10 facts (paraphrased):** 1) deal reviews compress to <60s / 1 page. 2) building
the business case *is* the sales process. 3) deals close in conversations about you,
without you (written content guides the journey). 4) efficiency = less activity, more
influence. 5) writing is discovery, not documentation. 6) buyers don't trust your ROI;
numbers validate a story the buying team already believes. 7) two problems: math and
drama; drama is why math goes unsolved. 8) selling priorities > solving problems. 9) you
build champions (IKEA), you don't find them. 10) multithreading = a minimally-viable
committee (social safety net), not more contacts.

**My most-interesting pick:** #3, because it defines what the GTM Engineer is *for* (the
artifact is seller-in-absentia; optimize the thing that travels without the rep) and it's
our least-instrumented gap (we can't yet see whether an asset survives the internal
forward). Load-bearing with #2 and #6. Dallas offered to send a Fluint framework/write-up
on the winner — **follow up: did that write-up land, and which fact.**

**Three facts I flagged as missing from the shortlist:** (1) "Why now" is the buyer's own
clock, not urgency you install — already wired into war-room + signal-to-play. (2) The
real competitor is "no decision"/status quo, not Verint/NICE — competitive-intel over-
indexes on named rivals. (3) The best pipeline is inside the install base — the shortlist
is all net-new; matters for NRR/expansion + renewal-receipts motion. Fit-ranked: #1, #3,
#2.
