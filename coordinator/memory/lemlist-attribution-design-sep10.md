Context: Sierra offered (Sep 9) to hand-build Pardot custom-redirect tracked links per CTA per campaign. Dallas rejected per-campaign link building as a bottleneck with ~15 sequences and more coming.

Verified Sep 10 (vendor docs):
- Pardot custom-redirect completion actions (add to SF campaign, notify) fire only for prospects already cookied by the Pardot tracking code on that browser; a cold contact clicking from a lemlist email on a fresh browser does not qualify. Pardot's Google Analytics connector captures utm_* only on first touch and only for new prospects.
- lemlist click tracking requires a custom tracking domain per sending mailbox, and lemlist's own coaching says do not track clicks in cold email (security-bot clicks, deliverability), one link max. Matches the Sep 3 doctrine.
- lemlist Salesforce integration is team-level: creates missing records as Lead or Contact, sets default property values on new records (Lead Source), field mapping from lemlist variables to SF fields, activity mapping logs sends/replies as SF activities, opportunity creation per campaign on reply or "interested". Campaign membership is not a native feature.

Proposed design (not yet confirmed by Dallas or Genna): loader stamps `campaignName` and `motion` custom variables on every lead (it already stamps cohortId); lemlist field mapping writes them to two SF lead fields; default Lead Source "GTM Engineering" (picklist value from the open Lead Source ticket); one SF campaign per motion, not per lemlist campaign, membership via a single Clay workflow on the lemlist webhook if Genna needs CampaignMember rows; UTM links generated by the loader from one lander URL and Sierra's source/medium values; Sierra puts the completion action on the lander form, one asset for every sequence.

**Why:** zero per-campaign marketing work, every campaign attributed the same way, reporting in Genna's Lead Source units.

**How to apply:** check the lemlist plan includes the Salesforce integration before promising it (activities API was 402 on Aug 14). Related: [[bo-integrated-campaign-thread-sep9]], [[pipeline-council-context-aug24]], [[lane-b-pilot-dwo-cohort-sep4]], [[lemlist-activities-plan-gate-aug14]].
