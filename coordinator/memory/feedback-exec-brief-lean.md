Dallas, Sep 26 2026, on the first cut of the John Norton pre-read: "wayy too many words and unnecessary explanations background fluff etc etc and it's just messy looking."

**What was wrong:** two-column layout at 8px type, every table cell a full sentence with context ("cleared by the AMs", "per your guidance that..."), a hero subtitle explaining the standard every campaign follows, dated bullets with three-line status paragraphs.

**What passed:** one column; a hero with title and four stats; a campaigns table of five short columns (campaign, reach, contacts, sender, status badge); an ideas table (idea, date raised, one-line status); two side-by-side lists of one-liners (requests, also in motion); footer carries the cadence. About 180 words on the page. Email body mirrors it in one-line bullets.

**How to apply:** for any exec or SLT one-pager, a cell is a phrase, never a sentence with a reason; no subtitle that explains method; one column; if a section needs a paragraph it is the wrong section.
