Sep 14 2026, sending the Elevance room to Alex Bauer: "we don't need to be giving Alex any draft copy especially not draft copy written for other people. he did not ask for that at all."

**Why:** a room is a plan, not ghostwriting. Draft messages in an AM's name read as being told what to say ([[feedback-never-tell-colleagues-what-to-say]], [[feedback-peer-messages-no-instructions]]), and a drafted message in a THIRD person's name (Mary Ann to Norwood) is worse: it commits a colleague who has never seen the page. Same for a dated move assigned to named leadership; if the AM mentions it to them, the first they hear of it is from their own AM.

**How to apply:** on any AM- or colleague-facing room, (1) no message bodies, subject lines, or claims-per-message panels; lane moves name the door and the people, not the copy; (2) executive lanes carry a role ("Executive sponsor, to agree"), never named leadership on a dated commitment, and every exec move is conditional on leadership agreeing; (3) no claims in/out table (that is marketing's guidance, per [[feedback-no-claims-guidance-to-partners]]); (4) no defensive framing lines ([[feedback-no-defensive-copy]]). Keep the drafts in the data json for internal use. Precedent: Elevance room `motions/back_office_expansion/rooms/elevance_alex/` rebuilt Sep 14 2026; Cleveland Clinic room is the shape, this is the redaction rule on top.
