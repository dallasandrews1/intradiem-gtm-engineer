On Sep 23 2026, in the Cleveland Clinic Stars meeting, Naveen ruled that the campaign copy is about Dynamic Workforce Orchestration, not Queue Optimizer, because Intradiem does not integrate with Cleveland Clinic's ACD, which is Cisco. Queue Optimizer reads queue data from the ACD; DWO runs on the WFM data the account already has, so the WFM side is unaffected.

Naveen's own example (group DM C0C3F93JU1F, Sep 23 14:22 CDT) is a RENEWAL email from the account team, subject "Before your renewal: where DWO fits in your Stars work": the customer's own results first, Humana AHT as the outside proof, an explicit no-Stars-claim line, 20 minutes before the decision. Cheryl, Sierra and Carter were asked to react; Sierra said some verbiage read unnatural. The canvas now carries a house-style version of it as the Cleveland Clinic track, with the Cleveland Clinic figures still to be filled from its Greenlight row.

Two canvases exist. The Queue Optimizer version (F0C43SHNG0L) is superseded. The DWO version is the one to share: https://intradiem.slack.com/docs/T02FUS29E/F0C425HSEQ1

**Why:** a product pitch on a platform we cannot connect to loses the room, and Cleveland Clinic is a current customer where DWO already runs.

**How to apply:** before pitching Queue Optimizer to any account, confirm the ACD. Cisco shops get the DWO story on the WFM they run. Platform lines in copy stay generic ("the WFM you already run") until the stack is confirmed. See [[hc-stars-qo-campaign-prep-sep21]] and [[stars-messaging-authored-in-claude-code]].
