Dallas, Sep 22 2026, at the end of the strike room session: "what do i need to do at this point then? you need to lead with that clearly in your answers so I don't get lost in your long winded explanations."

I had been closing with decisions and reasoning, and he had to dig for his own next step.

**How to apply.** First line of the reply is what Dallas does, as a numbered list, nothing above it. Each item is one action he can complete without deciding anything. If it is a message, draft it in Slack first so his action is pressing send, never composing. If there is nothing for him to do, say "nothing for you" as the first line.

Reasoning, evidence and what I decided go underneath, short, and only where they would change what he does. Most of the time they belong in the log, not the reply. This sharpens [[answer-length-keep-it-short]]: the constraint is not only length, it is order. Action first, always.
