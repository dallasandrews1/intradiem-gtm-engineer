2026-08-07: Dallas: "you're giving me novel sized answers that are so long I don't even know what you're saying anymore," then asked that this apply to all sessions going forward.

**Default shape:** a few lines. What's done, what's broken, what he has to do. Bold the labels, skip the connective prose.

**Why:** length actively destroyed the message. The Aug 7 engine-room session produced correct work and a chat response so long the conclusions were unfindable. Volume of evidence gathered is not a license to narrate it. He is the one deciding what to do next, and he can't decide from a wall of text.

**How to apply:**
- Lead with the outcome. Never with what I did to get there.
- One line per item. No sub-bullets explaining the line.
- Cut: the investigation narrative, caveats he didn't ask for, restating his request, summarizing what I just said.
- Keep: anything that changes his next action, especially traps and ordering constraints.
- Corrections stay one line. State it, move on.
- Detail belongs in the deliverable or the UI sheet, not the chat. Point at the file.
- Go longer ONLY when he asks, or when the answer IS the analysis (a strategy call, a premortem, a written deliverable).

Applies to every session and project, not just Intradiem work. Related: [[naveen-facing-comms-rules]].
