On Sep 21 2026 Naveen posted youtube.com/watch?v=GdHJ-52Kh3k ("The Physics of GTM: The Three Laws of Go-to-Market Success", INBOUND 2025, Varun Anand, Clay co-founder) in the GTME group DM (C0C3F93JU1F: Sierra Jones, Nathan Belfield, Cheryl Eckel, Genna Barrett-Moeller, David, Carter Woods, Dallas). He called it "a master class on what we are trying to do with GTME" and asked for thoughts on applying the laws to relaunching the Star Ratings campaign around the CMS release, first week of October 2026. Discussion is on the Sep 22 2026 cadence call.

The three laws: (1) uniqueness, win by knowing something about your best customers no one else does ("GTM alpha"); (2) no creative advantage lasts, copying and saturation flatten every tactic; (3) iteration speed decides the winner. AI raises the baseline (basic personalization is table stakes) and shortens how long a tactic works.

Closest analog in the talk to Stars: the medical-practice play. A scheduled public data release (payer code changes every 90 days), analyzed the morning it drops, outreach that same morning with the specific impact on that practice plus a ready-to-send letter. "A solution, not just a problem."

**Why:** Naveen sees this talk as the model for the GTME function, so its vocabulary (alpha, hidden signals, always-on engine, GTM engineers at the center not an assembly line) is the shared frame for the Stars relaunch conversation.

**How to apply:** Frame Stars relaunch ideas in these terms, as threads to shape together per [[naveen-facing-comms-rules]]. Release-morning, plan-specific outreach is the obvious law-1 play; stars-refresh-watcher and the October refresh runbook are the existing head start. Law 2 caution: every vendor selling to health plans will reference Stars in October, so the rating itself is commodity; the alpha is the measure-level read tied to member-service operations.

**Approach recommended to Dallas Sep 21 2026 (not yet ratified by Naveen or the group):** relaunch as a release-morning movement brief, each plan's own 2026 to 2027 movement at measure level within 48 hours of the CMS release. Arms are movement cohorts, not personas: wait-for-October accounts first, new fallers, two-years-stuck, improved-but-still-under; graduates get no send; customer plans route to their AM, never cold. Release play expires about two weeks in (AEP noise), then pivot to what plans can still move this measurement year. Lead with movement, not dollars, while economics carry the stale_econ_carryforward flag. Pre-release order: rehearse refresh_oct_release.py on the 2026 ZIP, reconcile contact coverage against the Clay Contacts table and the lemlist Stars list (the watcher's committee file is stale since Jul 1), cohort copy shells, sender switch.
