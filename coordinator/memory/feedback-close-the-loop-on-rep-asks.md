Sep 4 2026, Dallas on the draft "campaigns live" post for Nate: I left the Maximus detail out as noise. Dallas: "Nate asked me about that explicitly, why wouldn't I include that? Otherwise he's just going to ask again and be annoyed I didn't just do what he asked."

**Why:** a rep's explicit ask is a loop; the update that follows it is where the loop closes. Leaving it out reads as not having done it.

**How to apply:** every rep-facing update lists each thing the rep asked for by name, with the outcome (loaded, held and why, done, waiting on X). Short is still the rule; the ask itself is never the thing cut. Related: [[feedback-slack-voice-short-casual]], [[campaigns-started-sep4]].
