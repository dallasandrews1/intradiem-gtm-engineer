Dallas, Sep 22 2026, on the BCBS of Michigan strike room. I had gated two things behind other people: Verint ("ask Frank what the partnership permits") and Blue Cross Blue Shield of North Carolina ("not on marketing's registry, so it cannot be named in writing"). Both were wrong.

Nathan had already said in the group DM that BCBSM is a heavy Verint shop, that Intradiem has a new Verint partnership, and that the goal is to use it. He had also relayed that Jean-Anne cleared the BCBS NC name-drop. Dallas: "Nate explicitly said we could use them in our dm with him and naveen in slack, so that overrules whatever you said about frank," then "yes it can be named in writing if they said we could use them."

**Why:** this is [[feedback-rep-word-is-the-truth]] applied to clearance, not just to account facts. The registry exists to stop us inventing a reference nobody approved. It is not a second approval on top of the person who owns the relationship. Routing a rep's own clearance back through a third party is a delay dressed up as rigour, and it reads as not trusting the seller.

**How to apply:** a named clearance from the rep who owns the account or the relationship, stated in writing in Slack, clears the name for written copy immediately. Record who cleared it and when, in the room's account rules. What does NOT travel with the name is a number: if no figure exists in the Value Repository, the copy ships with the name and no quantity, and the rep's figure drops in when they give it. Still ask for the Repository row afterwards so the next room inherits it, but ask as a follow-up, never as a gate. The registry remains binding only where no rep has cleared anything.
