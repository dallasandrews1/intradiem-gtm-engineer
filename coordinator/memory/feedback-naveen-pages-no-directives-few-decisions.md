On Aug 26 2026, reviewing the operating map's calendar ("Walk the Sep 3 pre-read with you", "Lead Source ticket with Genna and Sierra", "Pre-read to John, cc the group") and its six-row decisions table, Dallas: "I need the calendar section to be less directive and more broad action items. This doesn't need to look like I'm telling Naveen or anyone else what to do, and it lowers the value of the deliverable to have so many complex open items still left to decide. It just creates more friction and decisions needing to be made."

**Why:** a page that hands Naveen a to-do list and a stack of open decisions reads as work for him, not as a map he can return to; a peer shows the state of play and carries the decisions himself.

**How to apply:** calendars on leadership pages are milestones by week ("Week of Sep 7: cadence; maps in, names cleared"), not dated tasks with owners; "decisions" become a short "also in motion" list with a state ("in signature", "in progress"), three items at most; closing sections say what Dallas is doing next, never what the reader should decide or send; no "with you", "you on copy", "whether you send it or I do". Internal prep docs keep the full decision and ordering detail. Applied to `GTM_Operating_Map_Aug26.html` the same day. Extends [[feedback-no-showy-deliverable-copy]] and [[feedback-no-tasks-for-ams]].

**Addendum (Aug 27):** headings never address the reader ("Your list, with an owner and a read date" read as awkward); a heading names the thing ("Nine campaigns"), the columns carry the owner and date. Aug 27 pass on the map: no counting headings ("Three things..."), no describing headings ("What each one is for"); eyebrow is a one-word label (In motion, Next, Data, Process, Calendar, People, Tools, Alongside, This week) and the heading names the content ("Lemlist, Lead Source, ZoomInfo", "Pre-read, call sheet, maps", "Counterparts under John", "One job per tool"). Aug 27, second pass: the closing "this week on my side" section and the "alongside" status section were cut entirely (duplicated the tools cards and the ninety-day list; read as filler). Standing test for every section: if it repeats another section or exists to show activity, it comes off. The map now ends on the tools cards. Aug 27, people section: a list of names with vague phrases adds nothing; it earns its place only when each card says which process step the person owns and on which campaigns ("Owners: Who owns which step", with Step N tags). Cards without a step (Larry, Chris) were dropped. Then removed the same day: Dallas does not want any section that tells people above him (John, Kevin, Mary Ann) what step they own; a section that could create doubt or a hole comes off rather than gets fixed. No people or owners section on leadership pages. Process section heading settled Aug 27 as "How every campaign runs" (Dallas rejected "Seven steps, one wave per review" twice; headings that count or pack two ideas with a comma read as AI).
