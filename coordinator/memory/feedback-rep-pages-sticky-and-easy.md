Dallas, Sep 25 2026, on Rachel's four-account index: "probably cleaner to have the 4 accounts on the main page side by side instead of 3 on top 1 on bottom. remember the important thing here is to make this easy to follow easy to use and to make it somewhere that the AEs don't want to leave with dopamine hits etc".

**Why:** the pages compete with everything else on an AE's screen. A page that is scanned once and closed is a report; a page that rewards coming back (checking a step off, seeing progress fill, a warm call surfacing) becomes the place the account is run from.

**How to apply:** layout first (cards side by side at desktop width, auto-fit grid, no orphan card), then one or two small, honest interactions per page: tap-to-mark-done on suggested steps persisted per viewer, a per-account progress chip that fills, subtle motion on the action. Never decorative counters, confetti or copy that has to be read. Every look change goes in the shared theme (rep_index_theme.py, theme_rep_pages.py) so all reps get it.
