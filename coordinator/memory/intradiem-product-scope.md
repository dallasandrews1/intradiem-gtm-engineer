Intradiem sells **Dynamic Workforce Orchestration** for large, structured workforces in
high-demand environments. It is NOT a "call center" tool — it spans **contact centers
AND back offices**: claims processors, lending/underwriting, billing ops, field
dispatch, fulfillment, care teams.

**Six verticals served** (intradiem.com/who-we-serve): Healthcare, Financial Services,
Insurance, Retail, Telecom, Utilities.

**Core mechanic (real-time):** reallocate idle/downtime to backlogs and training,
auto-trigger task reallocation to protect SLAs, deliver just-in-time coaching, monitor
burnout/attrition before quality drops.

**Public proof points by vertical** (from who-we-serve page; still verify tier before
external use): Healthcare $7M annual savings (top-5 US org); Financial Services 11%
handle-time reduction (major US bank); Insurance 31% reduction in stuck-on-call;
Retail 16-sec hold-time cut; Telecom 5% productive capacity recovered; Utilities
Centrica 20% training-delivery boost.

**Why this matters:** Dallas flagged (Jun 2026) that early GTM skills over-indexed on
contact-center economics (AHT, shrinkage, agents). All four built skills
([[intradiem-30-60-90-plan]] context) were broadened to cover front + back office and
all six verticals. Always frame Intradiem by workforce + vertical use case, never
default to "call center." Structural wedge vs. Verint/NICE/Calabrio/Assembled/Playvox:
those are contact-center-only WFM; Intradiem orchestrates front AND back office.
