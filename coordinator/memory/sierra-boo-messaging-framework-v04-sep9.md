Sierra Jones's marketing framework for the Back Office Integrated Campaign (lander, 6sense ads, LinkedIn ads via Syrup with ~3 week ramp, GTM and sales outreach, social). Audience COO / EVP-SVP Ops / BU presidents / shared-services leaders.
