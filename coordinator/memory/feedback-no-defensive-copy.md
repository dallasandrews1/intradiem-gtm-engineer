Dallas cut "Everything on screen already runs" and then "Nothing in here is a mock-up" from the all-hands title card. Nobody in the room is wondering whether the demo is real until you tell them to wonder.

**Why:** pre-defending reads as insecurity and hands the audience a question they did not have. The work proves itself; a disclaimer only makes it look like it needs a defense.

**How to apply:** on any deliverable, cut lines whose job is to answer an objection nobody raised ("this is real", "not a mock-up", "already running", "not theoretical"). State the thing, not its defense. Same test as the AI-deck tell: if you would not say it out loud, it does not go on screen. Related: [[feedback-no-showy-deliverable-copy]], [[feedback-titles-lead-somewhere]].

Also from the same pass: parallel clipped fragments ("Three acts on live accounts. Everything on screen already runs.") are the AI-deck voice. Read every on-screen line aloud before it ships. A title card with just the eyebrow and the headline is stronger than one with a subline that has to earn its place.
