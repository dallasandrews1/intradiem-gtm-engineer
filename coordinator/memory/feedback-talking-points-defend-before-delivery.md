Two instructions given mid-build on Frank's QuickStart package, Sep 21 2026: "don't deliver me the final output until you get the cold call talking points to a place where you would confidently defend them and their ability to help reps on the phones and you would use them as-is", then "find the answers that you need to find to get this done the right way".

**Why:** the first draft pitched a beta capability as proven, ran 38-second monologues, and left gaps flagged for someone else to fill.

**How to apply:** for any spoken or rep-facing copy, ground the capability language in marketing's current framework first (check beta versus shipped), then run two adversarial reviews before delivery: gtm-copy-reviewer for claims, and a phone-floor reviewer persona for speakability and domain truth. Iterate until the claims gate passes and the floor reviewer says yes. When a reviewer names a missing answer, search Outlook, Slack, Teams, Otter, SharePoint, the repo and the public source before calling it open; only what is written nowhere goes to a person.
