Section titles and bullet lead-ins in Dallas's emails use plain, literal wording ("Things we can start now at no cost", "Daily call list for Nate"), not coined or punchy labels ("No spend", "Dated", "Signal call list", "Stars release-day strike", "Give first, ask second").

**Why:** on Sep 21 2026 Dallas called the headings in the Pipeline Council reply to Naveen "ai coding slop or irrelevant phrasing". Coined labels read as machine-written and Naveen never used those terms ([[naveen-facing-comms-rules]]).

**How to apply:** name a section by what it is to the reader. Name an item by the thing itself or the person it's for. If a label needs the body text to make sense, it's the wrong label.

**Happened again Sep 22 2026**, on the Stars trigger-cohort page, so this rule covers PAGES and documents, not just emails. Dallas: "change the titles of all these in the link to something more succinct direct natural not coding ai slop."

The tell that session missed: three consecutive headings built on the same "What X and what Y" frame ("What we can compute this week and what needs sourcing", "The cohorts and what each one says", "What fires this week, and what waits for October"). A repeated grammatical frame across headings reads as generated even when each one is individually defensible. Also caught: coined cohort labels ("Measure deterioration", "Near the line with real exposure", "They said it out loud", "Growth outrunning the service floor") and internal shorthand used as a label ("the humility line").

What they became, as the pattern to copy: "Three things to fix in the scoring", "Which signals we can actually build", "The six cohorts", "What goes out before the release", "Three rules the score doesn't override", "Open questions". Cohorts became literal descriptions of the plan: "A service measure dropped", "Close to 4 stars with a big Medicare book", "A new Medicare or quality exec", "They fell while their market rose", "They've talked about Stars publicly", "Growing faster than service can keep up".

**Check before shipping any page or doc:** read the headings as a list on their own. If two share a grammatical frame, or if any needs the body text underneath to make sense, rewrite them.
