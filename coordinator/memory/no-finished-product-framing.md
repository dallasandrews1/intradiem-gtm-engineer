Do NOT describe Dallas's pre-built work as done or finished. Phrases like "nothing left to build," "it is built," "wired not built," "the only go-live work is four checklist items" are banned.

**Why:** Telling a hiring manager the job is already done argues Dallas out of the role. If there's nothing left to build, why do they need a GTM Engineer? It also reads as cocky. (Caught Jun 25 2026 on the Star Ratings + engine pages.)

**How to apply:** Frame pre-built work as a **running head start / foundation that he keeps building**, not a finished system. The ongoing engineering, tuning, expanding, and building what each quarter needs, IS the job and the value. "Built before day one" is fine (it means he started early / didn't wait). "Nothing left to build" is not. Always leave room for the continuous build that justifies the role. Pairs with [[no-aiisms-house-style]] and [[check-project-before-hedging]].
