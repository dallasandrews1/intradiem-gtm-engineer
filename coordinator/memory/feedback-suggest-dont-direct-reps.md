Dallas, Sep 23 2026: "We also don't need to be telling Keegan or Nathan what to do. They can be suggested next best steps etc but not next move by owner. Providing Keegan with as much immediate value as possible."

**Why:** reps and SDRs are peers, not assignees. A page that hands out tasks reads as oversight and gets ignored. A page that hands over the draft, the verified contact and the timely fact gets used.

**How to apply:** use "Suggested next steps", never "Next move, by owner", "who does what" or "lanes". Phrase rep items as suggestions ("Worth an email to...", "A good moment to..."). Lead each item with the usable thing: the draft, the contact with title, the why-now fact, the one-pager. State Dallas's side as ready assets, not tasks. Drop internal process lines from rep-facing views. Same spirit as Keegan's "this isn't me telling y'all what to do" ([[keegan-call-asks-built-sep23]]). Related: [[feedback-next-moves-must-read-alone]], [[feedback-never-tell-colleagues-what-to-say]].

**Also (same day):** no filler reassurance like "Nothing needed from you" or "Nothing for you to write". Dallas called it unnecessary. A reason line either carries a usable fact or it's left out.
