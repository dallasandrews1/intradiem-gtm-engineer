Nathan holds a copy of the Jul 16 2026 Blue Shield of California strike room. Checking it before offering a re-cut showed it was not stale, it was wrong. **Decision: retired, not re-cut.** Full reasoning in `automation/logs/stars-bsca-remotion-2026-09-22.md`; the room and PDF carry a RETIRED header pointing there.

**The three load-bearing claims, all broken by the Jul 22 re-issue and the Jun 17 Clover recalculation:**
1. "All three contracts at exactly 3.5, no full-star climbs." H4937 went **2.5 to 3.5** and H0504 went **3.5 to 4.0**, above the bonus line. The account is climbing, which removes the urgency.
2. "Customer-service stars in the mid-3s, the execution slice." The durable read on H4937 (45,627 members) is **Getting Needed Care 2 and Rating of Health Plan 2**, Customer Service **3**.
3. "~$33M forgone QBP." The gross sum across the pre-re-issue canonical rows; gross and `addressable_pct` are both pre-Clover.

**The disqualifying point:** Getting Needed Care is answered about the doctor's office and must never be attached to our service operation. So the gap sits mostly where we cannot honestly claim to reach, and Customer Service at 3 will not carry an opener against a plan that just climbed a full star.

**Where it went:** the cost-mandate lane. The July research holds, it was pointed at the wrong motion. Third layoff round in twelve months (69 positions effective Jul 28 2026, in window until about Oct 26), ~300 positions cut since Jul 2025, a funded Contact Center Modernization programme staffed Mar 2026, Ascendiun parent since Jan 2025. Holding service with a smaller roster while a named programme is already running, with no Stars claim, no CMS number and no dollar estimate.

**Open, deliberately:** whether to build the replacement now or after the October CMS release. Recommendation after October, because the same kind of re-issue that broke this room could break a rebuild written this week. The eight-person committee and its ZeroBounce-swept addresses carry forward either way.
