**Dates:** Queue Optimizer beta mid Q4 2026, GA Q1 2027. Back Office Optimizer beta Q4 2026 (Dallas's ruling Sep 18: use the timeline slide; the cover slide's September 2026 is not used), GA Q1 2027 (NOT Sept 2026 GA as the Jul launch kit assumed). Engagement Hub Q1 2027, Engagement Hub Pro late Q1 2027. Capability Cloud timeline TBD. Also named: Goal Advisor, Control Plane, Harmoniq enhancements, UPT Handle Time Assistant, Back Office Agent.

**Frame:** "the climb to service agility": Static to Dynamic (6 solutions live), Siloed to Shared (QO, BOO), Human to Hybrid (Engagement Hub, Capability Cloud, Goal Advisor). DWO = See, Anticipate, Act. QO buyer pain: SLA penalties (B2B, B2G), real-time analyst firefighting. BOO: digital twin, Capacity Insights, Scheduler Lite, Next Work Item, persona dashboards; claims, disputes, underwriting. Capability Cloud: governed 0 to 100 capability score for humans AND AI agents, open connectivity by API, MCP or push, skill files generated from evidence, partner AI agents (Parloa, Level AI).

**Where Dallas's work already maps (Sep 18 read):** back-office maps at 30+ customer accounts = named BOO beta and launch audience; buying committees exist for EH, QO, BOO; QO and BOO sales kit pages exist but predate this deck; PM builder kit proven cold on two PRDs but no PM has used it yet; pmo-action-extractor graded 19/20, AI-enabled PMO is next quarter's Product goal; his hands-on MCP and skills experience is directly relevant to Capability Cloud's MCP and skill-file pieces.
