On the Cleveland Clinic committee page I labelled ten people "already in the account team's hands" because the save plan had a drafted note and a week for each. Dallas: "that section is not accurate if it's going solely off of the original save room we built Inger that was instructing her on what her and her team should do. Those weren't a log of what was said unless you saw it from Salesforce or opportunity notes etc."

**Why:** the save room tells the rep what to do; it does not know what the rep did. The only Cleveland Clinic PMO export has zero rows and no move carries a state, so claiming "already writing to" invents contact history and would mislead marketing about who has heard from us.

**How to apply:** describe those people as "routed through Inger and Amy in the save plan, planned, not sent" and say what record would change it (Salesforce note, PMO tracker row, the rep's word per [[feedback-rep-word-is-the-truth]]). Before writing "already contacted", "in progress" or "writing to" about any account, find the Salesforce, Otter, email or tracker evidence; a drafted message in our own files never counts.
