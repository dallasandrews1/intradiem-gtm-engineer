Sep 4 2026, Dallas: "phone calls are the most important thing when it comes to successfully generating net new pipeline, and we will not succeed without it, so it needs to be a key part in everything that we do. Even if they don't answer, leaving voicemails is 10x more powerful than just sending a cold email or just sending a cold linkedin message by themselves."

**Why:** the Sep 3 doctrine reserved calls for planning, WFM and performance personas and kept C-level seats on email plus LinkedIn. Dallas overrode that for the DWO executive motion and for the standard generally: sustained pressure across three channels, with the phone as the spine.

**How to apply:** every net-new sequence carries call steps (at least three over the arc) and voicemail scripts, not "no voicemail" by default; the call-then-same-hour-email pairing is the standard follow-through; phone sourcing is a prerequisite planned before a campaign is built, never an afterthought; the EA route (main line, office of the executive) counts as a call touch when no direct number exists. Customer lanes keep Mary Ann's AM-clearance gate on calls. Related: [[nate-campaign-structure-review-sep4]], [[feedback-copy-over-word-ceiling]].
