FEEDBACK (Jun 30 2026): In any Naveen-facing or insider-facing deliverable, do NOT explain things he already knows. He wrote the role, the thesis ("pipeline is a system output"), the capabilities (enrich/personalize/automate), the goals, and the star-ratings domain. Restating his own framing back to him reads as condescending.

**Why:** Naveen is the exec sponsor and author of the strategy. Briefing an insider as an outsider misreads the power dynamic and wastes his time.

**How to apply:** For his-facing content, keep it peer-level: what's built, how it maps to the numbers, how I'd sequence it, what I want his read on. Cut thesis restatements, capability explainers, "maps to [his own goal]" tags, and value-narration ("so it survives a CFO"). The internal [[intradiem-official-brand]] review doc `GTM_Engine_for_Naveen_Review.html` was trimmed to this standard. Pairs with [[no-finished-product-framing]] and [[no-aiisms-house-style]].
