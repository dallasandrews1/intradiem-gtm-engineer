Dallas, Sep 26 2026, on the re-cut DWO executive voicemails: "these voicemails seem to have the right structure, but they sound terrible when I read them. Like no executive hearing those would actually want to call us back."

**What failed:** the written Email 1 idea ("who takes the work that's piling up is still decided by hand") read aloud verbatim; vendor phrasing ("by letting the system make that move"); the same abstract line in all three voicemails; nothing about the person in the first ten seconds.

**What replaced it:** a per-lead spoken hook variable (`vm_hook`) derived from the lead's own opener ("You're six months into the COO seat at AES", "Q4's about to put claims, renewals and year-end on the same teams at AARP"), then one plain sentence in Nathan's register, the note in the inbox as the reason, name twice, number once (Call 2 only). Third call is one either-way question with a one-word-reply exit.

**How to apply:** before shipping any voicemail, read it aloud as the rep on a Tuesday (playbook self-check 12). Structure passing the playbook is not enough; the first line must be about them and every sentence must be sayable. Derive a spoken hook from the lead's variables rather than reusing the written opener.
