- **SharePoint, sales Accounts folder** (`sales/Sales Resources/Accounts/Blue Cross Blue Shield of North Carolina/.../Blue Cross Blue Shield of NC - 553 BPO/`): eight Business Value Assessment decks, Oct 2025 to Mar 2026, by Lizzie Strausbaugh, Josh Wilkins, and Keegan Sanders with Larry Swift. All **pre-sale models**. Their own cover says "Estimations of savings were made based on size of environment and anticipated realization of benefits. Actual savings may vary."
- Two deal shapes modeled: **Captive** (271 licensed users, 247 agents, 642 AHT baseline, $888.8K year-one, 5.5x) and **BPO / 553 seats** (553 users, 503 agents, 700 AHT baseline, $543K year-one, 4.6x).
- The AHT figures in them are **modeled inputs**: 7 seconds / handle time -1% on the captive book, 13 seconds / -2% on the BPO book, headline "2 to 4% Handle Time Savings". **Lane 3.** Never ship these as a BCBS NC result.
- The testimonial quotes on the Benefits slide ("Mark B., Head of Service Delivery", a Medicare Operations Leader, a supervisor, a frontline agent) are **boilerplate template quotes with no company attribution**. Do not attribute them to BCBS NC.
- **Greenlight/Zuar: no CTO row at all** for this account (already recorded in `Customer_Value_Registry.md` line 105).
- **Otter:** no call. **Marketing's stories registry:** no story. **PMO / client services folders:** only Power Platform shortcut files, no documents.

**Conclusion: the realized number lives with the success manager, Nathan Belfield or Jean-Anne Mcmahon, and nowhere a tool can reach.** Ask a person. Do not re-run this search. Recorded on the BCBS NC row in the Value Repository so the next agent stops at the row.
