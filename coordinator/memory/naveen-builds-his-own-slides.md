Naveen builds the interactive slides himself. On the Aug 5 2026 1:1 he said plainly, "Think of an interactive slide, and I can do the interactive stuff, I'm pretty good at that." What he asks Dallas for is the **material**: the ICP, the industries, the four seats per industry, the named people and their LinkedIn URLs.

**Why:** Framing a deliverable as "here's the interactive thing I built for you" steps on work he owns and reads as though he needed rescuing. He asks for help to hit a deadline, not because he can't build it.

**How to apply:** Deliver material in whatever form is fastest to read, say "grab what you need," and leave the build to him. Ideas he asked for go in the document, not narrated in the DM. He also sends finished slides back to Dallas afterwards to check brand and font spec, which is a separate, later ask. See [[naveen-facing-comms-rules]].
