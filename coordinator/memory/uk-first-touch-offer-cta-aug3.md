Aug 3 2026, Jack Ohagan's "brutal truth" on the sync: the first-touch CTA "Have 15 minutes...?" won't work in the UK market. First touch must offer something instead, his shape: "is it worth me sending over how other airlines are approaching this?" Meeting asks stay from the day-3 LinkedIn DM onward (his own sprint design).

**Why:** a first-touch meeting ask prices a cold email at 15 minutes of a stranger's calendar; an offer prices it at one reply word, and a "yes, send it over" is a warmer qualification signal than any open. UK buyers delete first-touch meeting asks on sight, per the rep who books there.
