Dallas, Sep 22 2026, on the strike room field guide and the two account rooms: "remove all unnecessary fluff and explanations and educational fluff etc that take up space and hold no value. and make sure that the how to and the strikerooms are always organized in a way that is easy to follow digest and use."

**What counted as fluff in the version he rejected:**
- Rationale tails explaining why a rule exists, after the rule already changed behavior ("That rule is what makes the room safe to hand to anyone").
- Reassurance and encouragement ("You are allowed to sound like yourself").
- Conversational scaffolding referring back to the request ("You described the shape you wanted for Stars").
- Editorial commentary on our own material ("that is the rare one a service-operations leader cannot dismiss").
- Warm closers and callbacks, even good ones.
- Restating a thesis a second time in different words.

**The shape he wants:**
- Each section opens with the conclusion in bold, then the evidence under it. Never build up to the point.
- Tables and one-line rules beat paragraphs. A rule is one line with no "why" unless the why changes what you do.
- Banned moves get their own list, not prose.
- Cut roughly a third; the guide went from prose sections to a nine-row table plus five one-liners plus three rules.

**How to apply:** after drafting any deliverable, read every sentence and delete it unless it changes a decision, carries a sourced fact, or states a rule. Explanation of a rule already stated is the most common thing to cut. This is the standing bar for strike rooms, field guides and any rep-facing page, not a one-off edit.
