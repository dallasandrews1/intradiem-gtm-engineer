2026-08-05: Naveen asked Dallas to help design his own OKRs. Dallas explicitly instructed that the onboarding directive metrics (the four Q3 numbers in [[intradiem-q3-mandates]]) must NOT be used as the frame or in the thought process; the OKRs being set now are the foundation for the role's value and longevity. This supersedes the Q3 scoreboard framing in [[q3-scoreboard-one-pager-aug5]]; that one-pager is built on the old frame and should NOT be sent as-is.

**Why:** rep-style metrics (a meetings quota) make the role an expensive BDR that dies on one slow quarter; activity metrics make it a cost center. The chosen frame: GTM Engineer = owner of a new revenue channel + force multiplier, measured on channel economics, leverage, and compounding.

**How to apply:** the proposed three-objective set, pending Dallas/Naveen ratification:
- O1 funding: attribution live in CRM within 30 days, engine-attributed qualified pipeline >= ~5x fully loaded role+tooling cost, cost per qualified opportunity <= ~50% of next-best channel (benchmark agreed in writing month one).
- O2 differentiation: N reps/regions running motions end-to-end weekly without Dallas, signal-to-outreach under 48h, per-rep qualified-conversation rate >= 2x their own pre-engine baseline.
- O3 longevity: new motion idea-to-market under a week, defined ICP share under signal coverage, leadership reports from the engine's scoreboard.
Refused: raw activity as KRs, sole ownership of a naked meetings quota, any KR without a live data source at ratification.

**Update, same day:** Dallas ratified the three objectives ("I agree with these 3") and asked for the send-as-is one-pager.
