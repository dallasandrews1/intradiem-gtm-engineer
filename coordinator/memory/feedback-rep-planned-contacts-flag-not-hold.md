On the Cleveland Clinic committee page I first held ten people out of the campaign as "routed through Inger and Amy in the save plan, planned, not sent." Dallas: "needs to be presented differently to not make it seem like Inger or Amy aren't doing their jobs. Don't hold the 10 people from the list, just flag them as a person Inger is planning on reaching out to and to confirm with her before anyone else does."

**Why:** a page marketing and leadership read is also a page about the account team. "Planned, not sent" on a list of the rep's own contacts reads as a grade on the rep ([[feedback-dont-grade-colleagues-work]]) and hides names the campaign owner needs to see. The coordination rule (one Intradiem thread per person per week) is real; the way to carry it is a flag on the row, not a hold.

**How to apply:** any person a rep already plans to reach goes on the campaign list with a chip "<Rep> first" that names the planned note and week, and one line: confirm with the rep before anyone else writes. Count them in the campaign total. Never write "already contacted" from our own drafts either ([[feedback-save-plan-notes-are-plans-not-contact-log]]). Applied on https://save-rooms.pages.dev/cleveland-clinic/inger/committee/ and in [[cleveland-clinic-committee-list-sep23]].
