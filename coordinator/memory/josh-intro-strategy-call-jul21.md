Intro + strategy call with **Josh** (lead solutions consultant, Intradiem's go-to SME on value-based selling; offered his calendar with zero friction, told Dallas to book anytime) on 2026-07-21. Naveen in the room. Three things that matter:

1. **ACD/WFM is the single highest-value personalization lever.** Intradiem does two integrations per customer: ACD and WFM. Naveen called knowing a prospect's ACD/WFM "probably the most important thing" and couldn't believe it wasn't already in the motion. The play both he and Josh landed on: know the prospect's ACD → pull a verified customer story from a customer *on that same ACD* → lead with it. "That would be gold. You can do that at scale." This is the north star for the ACD-matched proof engine. Vendor granularity matters: capture the specific product/version, not just the vendor (old Genesys Cloud got sunset+renamed; a 7-yr-old deploy is a different product). Priority vendors: Genesys, Avaya, Five9, Amazon Connect (bigger than people think — AWS bundles it into spend commits). Note: Q Optimizer doesn't integrate well with Amazon Connect, and Genesys is trending the same way.

2. **The customer-story repository has no owner, and accepting that job is a trap.** Josh has asked around for years. His warning verbatim intent: if this doesn't come down from SLT with a VP champion who owns sourcing+maintaining it, Dallas ends up "maintaining PowerPoint nonsense, a waste of your talents." QBRs live as per-customer PowerPoints in SharePoint (Success Management owns them), with a spreadsheet/dashboard behind them (likely feeding Zuar): ACV saved, ROI, solutions deployed. **Matt Graves knows where the data actually comes from.** Naveen says Sierra is "on it," but Josh flagged that Sierra is marketing/messaging — she doesn't create customer stories either; that's Success Management. The move: Dallas owns the ENGINE that consumes verified stories; the SOURCING needs a named accountable VP owner. Get that named before it defaults to Dallas. Ties to [[self-serve-tools-not-dallas-in-loop]] and [[value-repository-frozen-jul21]].

3. **Demo ownership is transferring to Dallas.** Josh built the interactive HTML demo that solved a decade-old ~$100K "we have no working demo" problem. Split: Josh designs, Dallas owns+maintains. Hard requirement Josh raised: the demo MUST be gated (deal-room style, password + presenter certification) because it's the IP — never public. Interactive demo stays a separate gated tab, not embedded in the deck (embedding gets messy); new company deck is being forced to be interactive.

**Next action (highest-leverage, near-term proof):** run Josh's ACD validation test before scaling any ACD enrichment — pull 30-50 existing customers from Salesforce with known ACD, run the "verify technology usage" Clay waterfall (~14 credits/person), grade accuracy on specific product/version. Pass = validated the most important personalization variable + earned the spend to run at scale; fail = saved from building on bad data. Either way it's a same-week proof to put in front of Naveen and Josh. See [[matt-graves-intro-call-jul21]].
