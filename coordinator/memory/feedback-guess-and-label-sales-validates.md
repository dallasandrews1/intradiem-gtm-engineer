Aug 25 2026, after I moved tied directors up a level rather than pick between two same-titled AVPs: "It's okay to guess when we don't have the exact context for now. that's partly sales responsibility to be validating this data we curate for them."

**Why:** the maps are a head start for the AM, not a system of record; an unplaced card is less useful than a placed one with a short note.

**How to apply:** make the most plausible placement (split tied reports across tied managers), attach a one-line note ("could also be under X"), and keep every inference visibly labeled as inferred. Do not stall a deliverable on ambiguity that the AM can resolve in seconds in Sales Navigator.
