Rules for Dallas's own public-facing copy (LinkedIn About, bios), settled Jul 6 2026 after 8 iterations:

- Thesis-led, not story-led. His judgment/stateless-AI thesis + "What I care about" closer are keepers; he considers them effective, not slop.
- No salesy structure: no punchy one-line closers, no "the industry is converging" trend paragraphs, no pitch cadence.
- No stats he'd have to defend (cut the 14/80 rep stat himself).
- No previous-employer accomplishments in About once he's moved on; League proof lives as Experience-section bullets only.
- No former-client names (Optum) or internal tool names (Momentum) in public copy.
- GTM Engineer scope = marketing + sales + product + launches, never just sales systems.
- Final version: linkedin-about-v2.md in the project folder.

**Why:** he rejected 6 drafts as "AI slop," "salesy," or "cheesy" before landing here; the winning move was keeping his own thesis verbatim and cutting filler, not rewriting his voice.

**How to apply:** for any [[no-aiisms-house-style]] public copy, start from his own words, cut rather than rewrite, and never anchor his identity to a past employer.
