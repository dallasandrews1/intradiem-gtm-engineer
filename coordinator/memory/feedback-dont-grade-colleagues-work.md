Sep 22 2026. A draft Slack post opened "Genna, yours is better than what I had, so I built on it." Dallas: "Saying yours is better is condescending... we just need to say that I built this from that." It became "Genna, built this out from your signal list."

**Why:** ranking a colleague's work against your own puts you in the position of judging it, which is a manager's move, not a peer's. It also makes the message about Dallas's assessment instead of about the work. Genna is a peer in the GTM engineering group, not someone waiting to be marked.

**How to apply:** attribute and move on. "Built this from your signal list", "this comes out of Genna's triggers". Never "yours is better", "great model", "you nailed it", "Genna is right that X". Same rule inside documents: state the point on its own rather than crediting someone for being correct about it. Routing a decision back to someone is fine and is not grading, for example "Tier 2 as 5 to 7 is Genna's call".
