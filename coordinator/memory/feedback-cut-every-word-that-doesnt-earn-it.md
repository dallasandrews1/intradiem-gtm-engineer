Dallas, Sep 9 2026, on the side-quest pages: "eliminate all unnecessary words and phrases and sentences in these. if it doesn't earn its place and doesn't sound natural and human then it needs to be deleted." The pass cut roughly a third of the copy: explanatory second sentences ("so the layout can be judged before real data exists"), restated context ("the framework's first open question"), hedges, and framework-name echoes after a plain-word version.

**Why:** Every extra sentence costs a reader who already knows the domain, and padded copy reads as generated. Related: [[feedback-no-showy-deliverable-copy]], [[feedback-plain-language-no-code-terms]].

**How to apply:** Before building, write each block at the length a colleague would type in Slack; after building, reread every sentence asking what it adds and cut it if the answer is "context". Shortest form that a person would say wins. Meta fields are fragments, not sentences.
