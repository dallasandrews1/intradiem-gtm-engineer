Dallas asked (Sep 9 2026) whether the side-quest dashboard's sample data confused Joey and Kathryn and whether the page was too intricate. Decision: keep sample data (Kathryn: "easier to edit something that exists"; Derek's reference is Matt Graves's working trackers), but ship a lighter first layer. Applied: per-user tables and the threshold panel behind toggles closed by default, sample teams and managers renamed "Sample team A" / "Sample manager A" so fake numbers never read as a verdict on anyone, sample ribbon pinned to the viewport top, and the Slack draft leads with the present-mode link (`/#present` opens the slides directly).

**Why:** the full depth is right for the Derek working session ("dashboards that fire the interventions"), not for two non-technical teammates opening a link cold.

**How to apply:** for any page with a mixed audience, decide the first-look reader, put everything they didn't ask for behind a toggle, and send the walkthrough link before the page. Related: [[side-quest-measurement-layer-sep9]], [[feedback-cut-every-word-that-doesnt-earn-it]].
