From Matt Graves (Dir CSM), Jul 21 2026 — feeds sector-specific MessageGen prompts:

- **Healthcare:** SLAs are real, plans get bonuses (Star Ratings) and avoid penalties. Pull the SLA / Stars / bonus lever hard. Humana story fits here.
- **Financial services:** SLAs are mostly self-imposed (a few specialty-card contracts aside, e.g. Wells Fargo/Bilt). Projected-value framing gets "no, that's not real" almost every time. What lands: objective activity volume and time saved — handle-time alerts, dynamic delivery, coaching/training counts. Let the customer apply their own value; we supply the counts and do the math with them.
- **General rule:** never tell a customer what their value WILL be; state what happened objectively (alerts sent, sessions delivered) and let them value it. Success Managers hit pushback every time they project value.

Peer-benchmark framing works well in these conversations: "across our financial-services customers you land here vs peers" (maturity score + operational score). Because there aren't enough customers at the child-industry level, comparisons roll up to the parent industry.
