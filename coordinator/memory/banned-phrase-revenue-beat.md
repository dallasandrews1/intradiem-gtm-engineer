Aug 5 2026: Dallas flagged "revenue beat" in a Slack draft. He does not use that phrase.

**Why:** it is consultant/analyst register, not how he talks. It reads as borrowed language the moment a colleague sees it, which defeats the point of drafting in his voice.

**How to apply:** when the point is that the important timing is later, say it plainly. "The real window is October, not August." Name the month, name the thing, drop the framing noun. Same rule applies to any similar constructed phrase ("the moment", "the beat", "the inflection"). Sits alongside [[banned-phrases-comparing-notes-jul31]] and the house no-AI-isms rule.
