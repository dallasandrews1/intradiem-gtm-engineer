Source: Dallas + Jack Ohagan 1:1 strategy call (transcript "Sales Strategy Planning Meeting", supplied Jul 31 2026). Jack = Speaker 3.

**Jack's setup:** reports to Matt Rumins (runs UK sales and CX). One account manager/AE on the team, Jack is the only BDR. Owns demand generation with no assigned vertical, blank slate, anywhere across UK/Ireland/Europe. Quarterly target is only 10 meetings, "massively quality over quantity," and he hits it through LinkedIn and email alone. His stated ambition: double output if the motion is automated.

**Verticals, ranked in his words:**
- **Insurance and financial services: "the strongest proof points."** Two of the largest UK insurers are already customers. Financial services is where they want to be for enterprise scale but it's tricky to crack. This is the primary lane.
- **Airlines: "completely untouched."** His words: "now is the time where you should be going after airlines. They're going to be feeling it a lot more. It's summer, it's busy." Matt Rumins independently wants to push airlines and retail. Lane two.
- **Utilities, especially water: avoid.** The largest UK utility is a customer, but water runs **five-year budget cycles**, so if it isn't in the current cycle the deal waits five years. This kills the Ofwat C-MeX angle that [[uk-expansion-strawman-jul15]] ranked first on signal quality.
- **Retail: structurally leaky.** UK retailers offshore their agents to India, the Philippines, South Africa under a BPO, which writes off roughly 10% of his accounts.
- **BPOs: Jack's own counter-proof.** He was told repeatedly "BPOs don't work," booked a BPO nobody had ever contacted, and a POV was in place within two months. Larger agent populations mean more value. Worth keeping as a lane.

**Geography:** UKI plus Netherlands is the agreed focus. Netherlands is untouched and is where Clay earns its keep, because job titles, seniority and language all differ and a manager may hold the entire budget with no way to tell from outside. **Germany is out** (workers' rights make the technology effectively illegal). **Nordics are cold** (four-day weeks, agents aren't burnt out). South Africa is a ~6-month-out play as companies move there for the UK time zone.

**Netherlands messaging rule (Jack, from booking meetings there):** lead with the ask. "The reason I am calling you today is I want to see if this is relevant." No pitch, no "hi how are you." Blunt or they don't engage. Netherlands copy must be written separately, never localized from the UK version.

**Channel rule, hard:** LinkedIn and WhatsApp lead, phone worked into the sequence, email tactful and bucketed by account. Never blast. UK/EU solicitation rules make spray-and-pray a legal exposure, not just a deliverability one, and opt-out is required. Dallas was explicit he will not run the US "push it to the limit" approach in the UK.

**The 10-day sprint agreed on the call:** day 1 email + LinkedIn profile view + connection request. Day 2 call, leave a voicemail that references the email's subject line and names Jack at the END, not the beginning. Day 3 LinkedIn message: "shot you a note via email, thought LinkedIn might be quicker," short version of the email, "worth 15 minutes?" Buying committee spans the people strategizing about the problem down to the people living in it, with different copy per seat. Messaging escalates to bigger problems and gets more direct by day 10.

**Sales-cycle problem (the Matt-facing frame):** average UK close takes about 18 months, which Jack names as the biggest UK problem. Past outreach was spray and pray. The motion's argument to Matt is compressing the front of that cycle.

**Pricing (internal only, Jack's figures, NOT Repository-verified):** roughly $13/agent/month for two basic use cases, $20-25/agent with everything in place. Dallas read the typical deal band as ~$15-40k.

**Jack owes Dallas:** the full do-not-contact customer account list with context per account (he has spoken to nearly every account they target), delivered as a zip, plus a document of every email and LinkedIn message that has produced a booked meeting. He posted `UK-Email&Linkedin-Bookings.xlsx` to the group DM Jul 21. The DNC list is the hard gate before any UK load, see [[gate-integrity-fanout-first-run-jul27]].
