Sep 24 2026, reply to Jen Lee's "pick two ideas and consider three questions" note. Two drafts in a row ran 450 words with a "where it sits today" paragraph per pick. Dallas, twice: "would you really send this as-is?" and "it's not too wordy and explains too much unnecessary?" The send version is about 200 words: the two picks, one link each, one line per question, one hosting line, done.

**Why:** she asked which two and to think about three questions before a meeting she is booking. Answering the meeting in the email hands her reading she did not ask for and leaves nothing for the room. Dallas's real sends are always shorter than the first draft.

**How to apply:** when a leader's note names the questions, the reply is those questions answered, one line each, plus the link. No state-of-play paragraph, no repeated cost lines, no re-selling what she already read. Ask "would I send this as-is" before handing over any leadership reply, and cut until the answer is yes.
