Sep 18 2026, the Needle Movers submission to Jen Lee. Dallas asked whether each constraint needed more on its severity or whether that was over-explaining.

**Rule:** one clause, one fact from our own records, only where we have one (Inger's 12 shared maps covered the contact center only; about one in ten of 245 people checked on Keegan's accounts had left or retired; Cleveland Clinic renews in January with an RFP under way). No sentence that tells an 11-year insider why churn, pipeline or data quality matters. Skip the fact when it exposes another leader's numbers (Haresh's pre-pipeline aging) or names a partner's mistake.

**Why:** a fact she did not have raises the stakes; an explanation of her own business lowers the sender.

**How to apply:** any constraint-then-fix writing for leadership. Related: [[no-briefing-insiders]], [[spot-bonus-program-sep18]], [[deliverable-strength-framing]].
