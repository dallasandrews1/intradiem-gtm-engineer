Dallas, Aug 31 2026, after sending Naveen three Product AI goal links: "all 3 links I sent naveen are just presentations about what I built and not the actual thing I built. isn't that a problem?" Two of the three were (the PM builder page had no path to its prototype; the PMO page showed a sample instead of the register). Fixed the same night on the same URLs.

**Why:** an explainer page reads as "look what I built" and gives the reader nothing to use; the working thing is the proof. Related: [[feedback-deliver-the-new-thing-not-the-input]], [[feedback-no-showy-deliverable-copy]].

**How to apply:** before any leadership link goes out, open it and ask what the reader can DO on the first screen. If the answer is "read about it", put the artifact first (button in the hero, embed, or make the page the artifact's live output) and the mechanism below. Pages describing agents show the agent's real output, refreshed by a script.
