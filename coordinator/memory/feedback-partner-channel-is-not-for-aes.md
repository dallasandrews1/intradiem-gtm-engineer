Dallas, Sep 21 2026, after Claude listed "the post does not reach the Intradiem AE on the record" as a gap and suggested the AEs join #gtm-partner-briefs: no. The channel is about the partner channel, and what the partner team does is different from what AEs do.

**Why:** the partner motion runs through Frank Ciccone and the partner sellers (registration, the twelve-minute intro, the forty-minute meeting). An AE picks a record up once it converts. Pulling AEs into the partner team's working channel mixes two motions and two audiences, and it would also put more leadership-visible traffic in a room Naveen and Haresh read.

**How to apply:** partner jobs (event poster, deliver-first, stock rank) speak to Frank and, through him, the partner seller. Naming the AE on a line is fine because it tells Frank who owns the record; mentions, DMs or channel invites for AEs are not. AE-facing work lives in the AE packages and rooms (see [[ae-selfserve-project-sep18]], [[rep-index-pages-sep20]]). When judging whether a partner asset "reaches the people in the meeting", the person is the partner seller, and the route is Frank.
