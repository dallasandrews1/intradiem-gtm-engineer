Later Sep 21: Dallas is leaning Otter Sales Agent for the live prompts. Still unproven from Otter's own page: whether live answers are generated from a company knowledge base, which tier carries live coaching, and any Slack routing. A demo on a real call settles those before seats are priced.

The burn risk is enrichment wired upstream of export and auto-run tables. Confirm with Maya Miro that actions are uncapped on the contract.
