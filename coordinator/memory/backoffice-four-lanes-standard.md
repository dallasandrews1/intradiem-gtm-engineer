Aug 25 2026: "these all need to be our best guess at back office contact roles. remember the original set of 4 lists we made for Mary Ann last month trying to get a wholistic view of titles and roles for back office etc and she really liked it."

The four Jul 26 lists (`Back_Office_*_Jul26.csv`, 285 rows, Naveen: "on the nail"): (1) COO/SVP Executives: COO, Head of Operations, Executive Director of Operations, divisional presidents; (2) Operations Leaders: appeals, utilization management, claims, shared services, finance shared services directors and above; (3) Product Owners: enterprise workforce planning, workforce management and optimization leaders in back-office units (not the contact-center WFM sponsor line); (4) Claims/Ops IT Leaders: operations technology, technology operations, business systems implementation and architecture, claims technology.

**How to apply:** every account map covers all four lanes (lane label on each card and in the build sheet; `build_map_build_sheets.py` reserves at least three slots for lanes 3 and 4 when candidates exist). Exclude contact-center, call-center, customer-care, CX, telephony roles (sponsor line) but never exclude workforce planning or operations technology as a category.
