Sep 11 2026, on the Cleveland Clinic page after the marketing lane carried "what the one-pager can say" and "what stays out": "I don't need to tell Nicole or Inger or anyone else what they can or cannot say, that's not my place."

**Why:** Dallas is the orchestrator building assets for colleagues, not their editor or compliance desk; published rules read as policing peers and marketing, and the AM owns her account's voice.

**How to apply:** the Value Repository gate applies inside the drafting (first-draft engine, sharpener, claims ledger in the internal copy check) and never appears on a page, note, or list handed to a colleague. Account rules (sponsor-line, held moves) stay in the internal plan JSON for Dallas's own sequencing; a colleague-facing page shows routes, lanes, dated moves, drafts labeled as theirs to change, and the list.
