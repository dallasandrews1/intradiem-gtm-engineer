Aug 25 2026, after installing Clay's Website Intent table and reading that Clay resolves visitors to companies only: "the point of intent signals is to find more specific information other than company level only... company level only is the same type of stale signals we've been getting from 6sense."

**Why:** reps act on a name, not a domain. A company-level visit at a 40,000-person insurer does not tell Nate who to call; 6sense already produces that kind of account-level heat and it has not moved meetings.

**How to apply:** treat Clay's pixel as the first-party base layer (page, time, UTM, feeds ads and the Heat List) and get the person three ways, in this order: (1) our own outreach closes the loop at 0 extra cost, lemlist click events are person-level and a visit carrying the lemlist UTM names the person (families `lemlist_click`, `web_from_us`); (2) lemlist Signal Agents "Website visitors" adds person-level identification for US visitors (name, role, seniority; 20 credits per signal; company fallback; needs lemlist's own pixel), scoped to product and proof pages only; (3) third-party person-level pixels (RB2B class) only if lemlist's coverage proves thin. Person-level identification of anonymous visitors is a Jason Jones question before it goes live. Never present company-level intent alone as the intent layer to Dallas or leadership.
