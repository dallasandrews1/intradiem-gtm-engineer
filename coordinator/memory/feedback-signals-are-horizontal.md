Sep 22 2026. A Stars proposal recommended holding the executive-hire signal until after the October CMS release, on the reasoning that October reshuffles which plans are Stars targets so the enrichment spend would be wasted. Dallas: "why would you hold it until after the release changes? we can be using that exec hire signal on other campaigns and motions."

**Why the reasoning was wrong:** the signal was scoped to the 93 Stars parent organizations because that was the page being written. A new operations, service, quality or back-office leader is a why-now for back office, install-base expansion, WFM adjacency, the executive sequences and every rep package, not only for Stars. Scoped horizontally the cost spreads across every motion, the Stars slice arrives as a by-product, and the October reshuffle stops being an argument for waiting at all. It also ran against the standing aggressive Clay spend posture.

**How to apply:** before calling any signal expensive or recommending a delay, ask which other motions it feeds. Intradiem sells across contact centers AND back offices in six verticals, so people signals and firmographic signals are nearly always horizontal. Build once, point at everything. A timing argument that only holds for one motion is not a reason to hold the build.
