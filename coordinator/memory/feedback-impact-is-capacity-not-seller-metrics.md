Sep 18 2026, Needle Movers email. A premortem pass added "the measure that matters next is meetings". Dallas pushed back: his work saves the teams time and sharpens how they talk to prospects and customers; those results show over time and do not generate an immediate number the way selling does.

**Rule:** in leadership-facing work, do not volunteer meetings or pipeline as the measure of GTM Engineering. It hands the reader a yardstick he does not own (same logic as [[feedback-okrs-in-his-control]]). Express impact as capacity and quality using before/after facts from the record: Keegan's three account packages in two days; 3xG planned two to three weeks for 80 account briefs and the same brief runs in minutes; 1,016 webinar registrants routed in about a minute. The sourced hours figure DOES exist and should be used: "370 hours of sourcing and research handed back to sellers" (about 4,400 to 4,600 contacts at 5 minutes each; sourcing and research only, not writing or sequencing), shown on his all-hands results slide Sep 2026 (slide 5 of AI-Champions-Demo_GTM-Engineering.pptx, slide 88 of the master deck) with Jen Lee in the room, alongside "an account plan that took a rep a day now takes one ask and a few minutes; an org map that took an AM a week now takes days" and the problem-slide "about 1,800 hours of manual work". Use those exact figures for consistency with what leadership saw; only an UNSOURCED total ("thousands of hours") is out. Check the deck before telling him a number does not exist.

**Why:** Jen's own frame is "3X yourself" and velocity; capacity handed to other teams is the result she asked for.

**How to apply:** any impact statement, readout or OKR language. Related: [[spot-bonus-program-sep18]], [[deliverable-strength-framing]].
