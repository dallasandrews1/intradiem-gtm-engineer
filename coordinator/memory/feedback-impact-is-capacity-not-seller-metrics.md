Sep 18 2026, Needle Movers email. A premortem pass added "the measure that matters next is meetings". Dallas pushed back: his work saves the teams time and sharpens how they talk to prospects and customers; those results show over time and do not generate an immediate number the way selling does.

**Rule:** in leadership-facing work, do not volunteer meetings or pipeline as the measure of GTM Engineering. It hands the reader a yardstick he does not own (same logic as [[feedback-okrs-in-his-control]]). Express impact as capacity and quality using before/after facts from the record: Keegan's three account packages in two days; 3xG planned two to three weeks for 80 account briefs and the same brief runs in minutes; 1,016 webinar registrants routed in about a minute. Never an "hours saved" total without a source (the Sep 18 "thousands of hours" note is the one he disliked).

**Why:** Jen's own frame is "3X yourself" and velocity; capacity handed to other teams is the result she asked for.

**How to apply:** any impact statement, readout or OKR language. Related: [[spot-bonus-program-sep18]], [[deliverable-strength-framing]].
