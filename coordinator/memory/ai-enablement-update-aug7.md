**Build gotcha worth remembering for any fixed-page PDF:** `.page{height:11in;display:flex;flex-direction:column;overflow:hidden}` silently **flex-shrinks** children when content exceeds the page, and any child with `overflow:hidden` then clips its own content with no error. The header lost its second headline line and subtitle this way, and both columns later lost their last bullet. Fix is `flex:none` on header/statband/footer, `flex:1;min-height:0` on the body, and then **measure instead of eyeballing**: inject a temporary script that writes `scrollHeight/clientHeight` of the columns into `document.title`, read it with `--dump-dom | grep title`, tighten until they match, then remove the probe. Two measured passes beat five screenshot guesses, and it obeys the context-discipline rule (one render check per iteration, downscale with `sips -Z`, delete the PNGs after).
