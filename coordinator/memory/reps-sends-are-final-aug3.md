Aug 3 2026, Dallas: "Let's always assume whatever Jack or Nate sends me is the final full versions of things."

**Why:** confirm-loops were becoming the bottleneck. The engine kept gating work on "is this list complete?" asks back to the reps, which stalls the chain and makes Dallas the messenger for questions that don't change the action.

**How to apply:** when Jack or Nate sends a list, file, or answer, treat it as complete and act on it immediately. No "can you confirm this is everything" follow-ups. If they later send an updated version, that's a new final version; reconcile then. First application: the 7-account UK customer list (Aug 3) is the complete DNC scope, so the DNC-confirm gate is satisfied and FS wave-1 enrichment is unblocked. This does NOT relax the engine's own gates (real-row exclusion at load, verified claims, Dallas's explicit Start) and does not cover genuinely ambiguous third-party data (e.g. fuzzy Clay titles still get a verify ask when a wrong guess would embarrass a rep).
