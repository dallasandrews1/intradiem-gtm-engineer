# SDOH Reference — Social Determinants of Health

---

## What Are SDOH?

Social determinants of health are the **non-clinical conditions in which people are born, grow, live, work, and age** that significantly influence health outcomes. They account for an estimated 30–55% of health outcomes, outweighing clinical care alone.

---

## The 5 SDOH Domains (Healthy People 2030)

| Domain | Examples |
|--------|---------|
| Economic Stability | Employment, income, food security, housing stability |
| Education Access & Quality | Literacy, language barriers, early childhood development |
| Health Care Access & Quality | Insurance coverage, provider availability, transportation to care |
| Neighborhood & Built Environment | Housing quality, air/water quality, walkability, violence |
| Social & Community Context | Social isolation, discrimination, civic participation, incarceration |

## SDOH Screening Instruments

### AHC Health-Related Social Needs (HRSN) Screening Tool
**Source**: CMS Accountable Health Communities model
The most commonly used standardized tool in Medicaid and Medicare programs.

Core domains (required):
1. Housing instability
2. Food insecurity
3. Transportation needs
4. Utility help needs
5. Interpersonal safety

Supplemental domains (optional):
- Financial strain
- Employment
- Family/community support
- Education
- Physical activity
- Substance use
- Mental health
- Disabilities

### PRAPARE (Protocol for Responding to and Assessing Patients' Assets, Risks, and Experiences)
Used widely in FQHCs and safety-net clinics. Captures:
- Race/ethnicity/language
- Farm worker / migrant status
- Veteran status
- Refugee status
- Income / poverty
- Education
- Insurance
- Housing
- Neighborhood safety
- Social isolation

### WellRx / Accountable Communities of Health tools
Regional variations with similar domains; used in some state Medicaid programs.

## ICD-10 Z-Codes for SDOH

Z-codes allow clinical documentation of social risk. They are increasingly required in value-based contracts and CMS innovation models.

| Category | Z-Code Range | Examples |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| Housing | Z59 | Z59.0 homelessness; Z59.1 inadequate housing |
| Food | Z59.4 | Lack of adequate food |
| Employment | Z56 | Z56.0 unemployment; Z56.2 discord with employer |
| Education | Z55 | Z55.0 illiteracy; Z55.4 educational maladjustment |
| Social environment | Z60 | Z60.2 living alone; Z60.4 social exclusion |
| Family circumstances | Z62–Z63 | Domestic violence, family disruption |
| Legal | Z65 | Z65.3 imprisonment |
| Access to care | Z75 | Z75.3 unavailability of health services |

> **Design implication**: Products and content that help members understand and complete SDOH screening, or that connect them to community resources, can improve Z-code documentation rates and enable better risk stratification.

## SDOH in Quality Measurement

### HEDIS SDOH Measures
NCQA has introduced SDOH-related measures including:
- **Social Need Screening and Intervention (SNS)**: Screen rate + referral/intervention rate
- Stratified reporting by race/ethnicity on high-priority HEDIS measures
- Health Equity Summary Score (HESS): composite of stratified performance gaps

### CMS SDOH in Stars
CMS applies the **Patient Experience Factor (PEF)** adjustment to CAHPS measures for plans serving:
- Dual-eligible members
- Low-Income Subsidy (LIS) recipients

CMS is also piloting SDOH-related measures in CMMI innovation models and value-based insurance design (VBID).

### Medicaid SDOH Requirements
Many state Medicaid programs now require:
- SDOH screening at enrollment or annual health assessment
- Documentation in electronic health records
- Referral tracking to community-based organizations (CBOs)
- Reporting on Z-code utilization

---

## SDOH and Health Equity

**CMS Health Equity Index (HEI)**: A new Stars measure (2027 implementation) that will reward plans for:
- Reducing disparities in measure performance across race/ethnicity, dual status, disability, rural
- Collecting and reporting stratified data

**NCQA Health Equity Accreditation**: Voluntary program recognizing plans that:
- Collect race/ethnicity/language data
- Stratify quality data by demographics
- Have actionable health equity plans

## Connecting SDOH to Clinical Outcomes

| SDOH factor | Linked clinical outcomes | Relevant measures |
|-------------|------------------------|------------------|
| Food insecurity | Diabetes control, obesity | CDC (HbA1c), WCC |
| Housing instability | ED utilization, mental health crises | FUH, AMB |
| Transportation barriers | Missed appointments, delayed screenings | BCS, COL, PPC |
| Social isolation | Depression, functional decline | DSF, HOS (MHM) |
| Financial toxicity | Medication non-adherence | MAD, MAH, MAC |

When content or features address SDOH, connect them explicitly to the clinical outcomes they are likely to improve. This strengthens the case for investment and aligns with plan quality strategy.
