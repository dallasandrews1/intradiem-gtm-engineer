# NQF / Core Measures Reference

---

## NQF (National Quality Forum)

**Role**: Endorsement body — NQF does not create measures, it endorses them
**Process**: Rigorous multi-stakeholder review for importance, scientific validity, usability, feasibility
**Why it matters**: NQF endorsement = national credibility; CMS and NCQA preferentially use NQF-endorsed measures

### NQF Measure Criteria
1. **Importance**: Does it address a significant gap or burden?
2. **Scientific acceptability**: Is the evidence base strong?
3. **Usability**: Can stakeholders act on it?
4. **Feasibility**: Can it be reliably collected?

### NQF Measure Types
- **Process**: Was the recommended action taken? (e.g., HbA1c test performed)
- **Outcome**: Did the patient's health status change? (e.g., HbA1c <8%)
- **Structure**: Is the infrastructure in place? (e.g., care coordinator on staff)
- **Patient experience**: How did the patient perceive their care?
- **Composite**: Multiple related measures combined

### Relevance to Health Plans
Many HEDIS and Stars measures are NQF-endorsed (look for NQF# in NCQA documentation). When designing outcomes frameworks, NQF endorsement signals that a measure is:
- Scientifically defensible
- Nationally comparable
- Appropriate for public reporting

## CMS Core Measures (Hospital Quality)

**Also known as**: Joint Commission Core Measures, TJC Accountability Measures
**Used by**: Hospitals for CMS Hospital Compare, Inpatient Quality Reporting (IQR), Joint Commission accreditation

### Core Measure Sets

**Acute Myocardial Infarction (AMI)**
- Aspirin at arrival and discharge
- Statin at discharge
- Fibrinolytic therapy timing
- Primary PCI timing

**Heart Failure (HF)**
- Discharge instructions
- LVSD and ACE inhibitor / ARB therapy
- Beta-blocker at discharge

**Pneumonia (PN)**
- Blood cultures before antibiotics
- Appropriate antibiotic selection

**Surgical Care Improvement Project (SCIP)**
- VTE prophylaxis
- Appropriate antibiotic timing
- Beta-blocker continuity

**Stroke (STK)**
- VTE prophylaxis
- Antithrombotic therapy
- Anticoagulation for AFib
- Thrombolytic therapy
- Early ambulation

**Perinatal Care (PC)**
- Early elective delivery rate
- Exclusive breast milk feeding
- Antenatal steroids

**Behavioral Health (BHCR)**
- Tobacco use screening and treatment
- Alcohol use screening
- Depression screening

### Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program (HRRP)
CMS penalizes hospitals for excess readmissions within 30 days for:
- AMI
- Heart failure
- Pneumonia
- COPD
- Hip/knee arthroplasty
- CABG

> **Relevance to health plans**: Post-discharge follow-up (FUH in HEDIS/Stars) directly addresses the care gap that drives readmissions. Content and navigation features targeting these populations have dual impact on plan and hospital metrics.

## URAC Quality Measures

**URAC** accredits health utilization management and care management programs. Key outcome standards:
- Case management program effectiveness
- Disease management outcomes reporting
- Member engagement and satisfaction

Less prominent than NCQA but relevant for plans with care management programs seeking dual accreditation.

## Connecting NQF/Core to Plan Work

| Clinical area | NQF-endorsed / Core analog | HEDIS/Stars overlap |
|--------------|---------------------------|-------------------|
| Diabetes | NQF #0059 (HbA1c control) | CDC — Stars |
| Hypertension | NQF #0018 (BP control) | CBP — Stars |
| Preventive care | NQF #0034 (colorectal screen) | COL — Stars |
| Depression | NQF #0710 (screening) | DSF — HEDIS |
| Readmissions | NQF #0330 (30-day all-cause) | PCR — Stars |
| Medication adherence | NQF #0541 (diabetes meds) | MAD — Stars triple-weight |

When building outcomes frameworks or clinical content programs, referencing the NQF number provides:
- Scientific defensibility for clinical or regulatory audiences
- Cross-walk to HEDIS/Stars for plan-level reporting alignment
