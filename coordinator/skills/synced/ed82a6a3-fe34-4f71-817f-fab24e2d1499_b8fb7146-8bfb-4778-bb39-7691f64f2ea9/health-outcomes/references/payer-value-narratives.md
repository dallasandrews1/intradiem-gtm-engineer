# Payer Value Narrative Templates

Use these templates when your team needs to build a value story for a health plan partner, respond to an RFP, prepare for a business review, or make the case for a new program. Each template follows the structure payers evaluate against.

---

## The Payer Value Framework

Health plan partners evaluate vendor value across four dimensions — always address all four:

1. **Quality measure impact** — which Stars or HEDIS measures does this move, and by how much?
2. **Financial impact** — what is the PMPM cost reduction or QBP revenue uplift?
3. **Member experience** — how does this affect CAHPS scores or member retention?
4. **Equity and access** — does this close disparities or serve high-risk populations?

## Template 1: Stars Improvement Story

Use when: Presenting to a Medicare Advantage plan quality team or business development lead.

```
[PROGRAM/FEATURE NAME] is designed to improve performance on [MEASURE NAME(S)],
which [carry X weight in CMS Star Ratings / return to triple-weight in MY2027].

In our [pilot / current book of business / comparable program], members who engaged
with [feature] showed [X% improvement / X percentage point increase] in [measure rate].

At a plan level, a [X] percentage point improvement in [measure] translates to
approximately [$ amount] in additional Quality Bonus Payment revenue, based on
[plan's approximate membership / benchmark QBP calculation].

This improvement is driven by [specific mechanism: medication reminders / care gap
outreach / follow-up support / goal-setting], which addresses the primary driver of
non-performance: [root cause].
```

**Example filled in:**
> Our medication adherence program is designed to improve performance on MAD, MAH, and MAC — the three adherence measures returning to triple-weight in MY2027. In our current cohort, members receiving personalized refill reminders showed a 6.2 percentage point improvement in PDC ≥0.80 rates. For a plan with 50,000 Medicare members, a 6-point improvement in MAC alone translates to approximately $1.8M in additional QBP revenue annually. This is driven by proactive outreach 10 days before expected refill gaps — the primary driver of missed fills.

## Template 2: PMPM Cost Reduction Story

Use when: Presenting to a plan CFO, actuary, or value-based contract team.

```
[CONDITION] management is among the highest-cost areas in [plan's population].
The average annual cost for a member with [uncontrolled condition] is approximately
[$X], compared to [$Y] for a well-managed member — a difference of [$Z] PMPM.

[PROGRAM] targets members at the inflection point: [description of population].
By [mechanism], we reduce [specific event]: hospitalizations, ED visits, readmissions.

In [comparable program / pilot / published literature], this approach reduced
[event rate] by [X%], translating to approximately [$Z] PMPM in avoided costs
for the engaged population.

Our target engagement rate is [X%] of eligible members, producing an estimated
[$] in total avoided cost with a program cost of [$], yielding an ROI of [X:1].
```

**Key cost benchmarks to use:**
- Unplanned hospitalization (general): ~$12,000–15,000/admission
- Heart failure readmission: ~$15,000–25,000
- Diabetes-related hospitalization: ~$20,000–30,000
- Behavioral health crisis/ED: ~$2,500–5,000/visit
- Avoidable ED visit (primary care sensitive): ~$1,200–2,500
- Major cardiovascular event (MI, stroke): ~$50,000–100,000+

## Template 3: CAHPS and Member Experience Story

Use when: Presenting to a plan member experience team, or when Stars CAHPS measures are a known weakness.

```
CAHPS measures account for approximately 22% of the Part C Star Rating in 2026.
[PROGRAM/FEATURE] is designed to improve performance on [specific CAHPS composite]
by [mechanism].

The primary driver of low scores on [composite] is [root cause: access friction /
communication gaps / coordination failures / unresolved member concerns].

[Feature] addresses this by [specific intervention]. In member satisfaction data
from [comparable population], this approach improved [metric] by [X points /
X percentage points on top-box].

Importantly, CAHPS improvement also reinforces clinical outcomes: members who
report [getting care quickly / how well doctors communicate] are [X%] more likely
to follow through on care plans and medication adherence — creating a direct link
between experience improvement and clinical measure performance.
```

**CAHPS-to-clinical connection points:**
- Getting Needed Care → gap closure rate (members who access care close gaps)
- Getting Care Quickly → FUH-7 (timely follow-up requires timely access)
- How Well Doctors Communicate → medication adherence (understanding drives fills)
- Customer Service → plan retention and engagement depth

## Template 4: Health Equity / D-SNP Story

Use when: Responding to an RFP with equity requirements, or presenting to a Medicaid or D-SNP plan.

```
[PLAN NAME]'s D-SNP / Medicaid population faces compounding barriers that standard
care management approaches don't address: [relevant SDOH factors from population data].

Our approach is grounded in the NCQA Person-Centered Outcome framework, which is
entering HEDIS MY2027 for D-SNPs and aligns with CMS's August 2025 rule requiring
person-centered goals in D-SNP care plans.

[PROGRAM] addresses [SDOH factor] by [specific mechanism], while simultaneously
supporting performance on [measure(s)]. For example, [food insecurity screening +
navigation] reduces HbA1c-related gaps by removing the root cause of non-adherence,
not just the symptom.

In our [comparable program / pilot], we achieved [X% SDOH screening rate /
X% resource connection rate / X% reduction in disparities gap on [measure]].

CMS's EHO4All index — new in 2026 — rewards plans that improve outcomes for
underserved populations. This program is designed to perform well within that framework.
```

## Payer Vocabulary Cheat Sheet

Use these terms when speaking with plan quality and finance teams — they signal fluency:

| Term | Meaning | When to use |
|------|---------|-------------|
| QBP | Quality Bonus Payment | Revenue tied to Stars ≥4; the financial stake in Stars |
| Cut point | Threshold between star levels | "We're targeting the 4→5 star cut point on CBP" |
| PMPM | Per Member Per Month | Cost or revenue expressed per member |
| PDC | Proportion of Days Covered | Adherence metric for medication measures |
| Hybrid | Claims + medical record reporting | Higher rates possible; requires supplemental data |
| ECDS | Electronic Clinical Data Systems | EHR-based reporting; replacing hybrid by 2029 |
| Numerator event | The qualifying clinical action | "This interaction generates a numerator event for DSF" |
| Care gap | Member in denominator but not numerator | "We target members with open gaps in CDC" |
| Risk adjustment | Adjusting scores for member complexity | Relevant for adherence measures (SDS adjustment 2026) |
| EHO4All | NCQA/CMS equity index | Stars bonus for improving underserved population performance |
| D-SNP | Dual-Eligible Special Needs Plan | Medicare + Medicaid; highest complexity population |
| PCO | Person-Centered Outcome measures | Entering HEDIS MY2027; goal-directed care |
