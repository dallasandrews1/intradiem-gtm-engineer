## When this skill applies

- Run during account research phase (pre-discovery)
- Run when targeting VP Digital Health or Director of Member Experience roles
- Run to generate insights for cold outreach or warm discovery
- Run after account has been identified but before initial contact

## Background

The App Audit skill turns public app review data into a forcing function. A health plan's poor app ratings (especially 1-star and 2-star reviews) signal member frustration with digital experience. Dallas can reference specific complaints ("Your app reviews mention benefit navigation is confusing" or "Login failures are mentioned in 7% of 1-star reviews") to establish credibility with member experience leaders and create operational urgency.

## Workflow Steps

1. **Identify Target Account's App**
   - Confirm the health plan has a consumer-facing mobile app (iOS and/or Android)
   - Find app on Apple App Store and Google Play Store
   - Note current rating (out of 5 stars)
   - Note total review count

2. **Gather Review Data**
   - Pull all 1-star reviews (typically 50-100 reviews available)
   - Pull all 2-star reviews (typically 50-100 reviews available)
   - Capture review text, date posted, and reviewer profile (if available)
   - Look for patterns and common complaints across reviews

3. **Categorize Complaints**
   - **Fragmented UX**: "App looks outdated," "Hard to navigate," "Confusing design," "Steps are too many," "Mobile version is different from web"
   - **Benefit Navigation**: "Can't find my benefits," "Doesn't show my coverage," "Benefits section is confusing," "Can't understand my plan"
   - **Login Issues**: "Can't log in," "Password reset doesn't work," "Constantly logged out," "Biometric login doesn't work"
   - **Speed/Performance**: "App is slow," "Crashes frequently," "Takes forever to load," "Freezes when searching"
   - **Claims/ID Card**: "Can't find my ID card," "Claims history missing," "Wrong claims showing"
   - **Member Services**: "Can't reach customer service," "No live chat option," "Phone lines are overcrowded"
   - **Security/Privacy**: "Worried about data privacy," "App requires too many permissions"

4. **Synthesize into 3-Bullet Executive Summary**
   - Identify the TOP 3 complaint categories (by frequency and impact)
   - Create tight, data-backed bullets
   - Format: [Issue] is mentioned in X% of 1-star reviews. Members report [specific complaint]. Impact: [member outcome, e.g., churn risk, support escalation].
   - Example: "Benefit navigation complexity: 34% of 1-star reviews cite inability to find coverage details. Members abandon the app and call customer service. Annual call center escalations: ~2,000 calls on benefit questions."

5. **Calculate Impact Metrics (If Possible)**
   - Estimate call center deflation if app experience improves
   - Estimate NPS impact (better app = higher engagement and retention)
   - Example: "If app addresses benefit navigation and login issues, typical 0.5-1 point NPS improvement. At 200K members, 1 NPS point = 0.5-1% retention improvement."

6. **Draft 3-Touch LinkedIn Sequence**
   - **Touch 1**: Cold outreach with app audit observation
   - **Touch 2**: Follow-up with specific insight (e.g., "I pulled together what members are saying about your app")
   - **Touch 3**: Value prop link to meeting/call
   - All touches are LinkedIn InMails (2-3 sentence format each)
   - Language: Peer-level, specific, credible, no League mention

7. **Assign to Target Persona**
   - This sequence is designed for: VP Digital Health, Director of Member Experience, Chief Member Experience Officer
   - Avoid sending to: CMO, CFO, CTO (wrong persona for member experience angle)

## Canon Constraints

- **Tenbit++ Framework**: All external communications follow Observation → Insight → Value → Next Step
- **Pincer Rule**: Communications to VPs and Directors focus strictly on Operational Relief, never Brand Vision
- **Brand-Light Execution**: Do NOT mention League by name in early outreach (Days 1-5). Frame solution as category.
- **FORBIDDEN WORDS**: Never use leverage, synergy, cutting-edge, best-in-class, empower, seamless, robust, transform, transformation, catalyst, paradigm, innovative, or comprehensive
- **Strict Punctuation**: Periods and commas only inside narrative paragraphs. No hyphens or em dashes.
- **Named Outputs**: Every deliverable gets saved as a file. Nothing lives only in chat.
- **MEDDPICC Discipline**: After every call transcript, flag empty or weak MEDDPICC fields.

## Examples

### Compliant App Audit (Benefit Navigation Complaint)
**Complaint**: Benefit navigation complexity
**Frequency**: 34% of 1-star reviews mention "can't find my benefits," "coverage unclear," or "plan details are confusing"
**Examples**:
- "I've been a member for 2 years and still can't figure out what my plan covers. The app doesn't help."
- "Trying to find what's covered for my prescription is like a treasure hunt. Gave up and called customer service."
- "The benefit summary is incomprehensible. I have a healthcare background and I'm confused."
**Member Impact**: Members abandon the app, call customer service to verify coverage, or avoid care due to confusion
**Operational Impact**: 1,800-2,200 annual member services calls on benefit questions. NPS impact: 0.5-1 point lower than industry benchmark.

### Compliant App Audit (Login Issues Complaint)
**Complaint**: Login failures and session timeouts
**Frequency**: 28% of 1-star reviews mention "can't log in," "keeps logging me out," or "password reset doesn't work"
**Examples**:
- "I reset my password 3 times. Still can't get in. This is ridiculous."
- "Logged me out in the middle of looking at claims. Had to start over."
- "Biometric login doesn't work. I have to manually log in every time."
**Member Impact**: Friction at first use, members abandon app, call customer service for password resets
**Operational Impact**: 600-800 annual password reset and login support calls

### Compliant 3-Touch Sequence (Touch 1)
"Hi Sarah, I pulled your app ratings. iOS is at 3.1 stars, Android at 3.0. Diving into the 1 and 2-star reviews, members struggle with benefit navigation and login issues. These same issues appear in your competitor's app reviews too, but at half the frequency. Worth a 15-min call? Dallas"

### Non-Compliant App Audit
"Your app is bad. You need our innovative, cutting-edge platform to transform your member experience and empower your team. We're the best-in-class solution for app modernization."
Issue: No data from reviews, uses FORBIDDEN WORDS, brand-centric, no specific member complaints, no operational impact quantified.

### Non-Compliant 3-Touch Sequence
"Hi Sarah, We have a great app solution that will transform your member experience. Our innovative platform is best-in-class. Let's chat about how we can help."
Issue: No specific insight from app audit, uses FORBIDDEN WORDS, generic value prop, no credibility signal, brand mention.

### Compliant App Audit Impact Statement
"If [Account Name] addresses benefit navigation (34% of 1-star complaints) and login reliability (28% of 1-star complaints), typical outcomes: NPS improves 1-2 points, call center benefits inquiries drop 40%, member engagement increases 8-12%. At 200K members, 1 NPS point = $5M potential lifetime value impact."
