# Stage Ladder (motion-invariant, 5-touch)

The escalation arc is the same for every motion; only the motion slots (position, personas, signal) change. Each stage prompt = [stage header below] + [motion slots] + [voice_core]. All five carry the SAME one idea, approached from a new facet each touch, never restated.

## Cadence + defaults
- E1 Day 1 · E2 Day ~3 · E3 Day ~6 · E4 Day ~9 · E5 Day ~12 (compress or widen per motion).
- Signature: MessageGen generates NO signature (matches the output contract). The sender/signature layer applies the sign-off, first name Days 1-5, full name Day 6+.
- Thread handling (E2-E5): send as replies in the same thread. Open on a new facet, never on the silence. BANNED openers (beyond voice_core's list): "just following up," "circling back," "bumping this," "checking in," "wanted to resurface," "in case you missed it."
- CTA timing: Directors "early next week" is fine; VP and above "in the next couple weeks." Tight anchors on senior outreach read as desperation.

## Brand convention (per-motion setting)
Set this once per motion in the motion slots:
- **A (brand-light):** product name absent Days 1-5, may appear from Day 6.
- **B (once-per-touch):** product name may appear at most once from Day 1. (WFM-Adjacency = B.)

## E1 — Day 1 · Cold opener
Cold, opens on the signal-led angle or the operational moment. Full arc. Soft CTA. This is the motion's flagship prompt; the others are deltas over it.

## E2 — Day 3 · New angle, still soft
New facet of the one idea, not a re-pitch. Escalation: soft, curiosity over ask. Brand: per convention (B = product at most once, but lead with the moment; the soft touch is stronger without it). CTA low-friction, names the topic, exactly one question, ends on it. 70-95 words.

## E3 — Day 6 · Value / proof lane
The value/proof touch. Brand window fully open. Lead with the industry-level proof beat (how customers in their space use it), no named customer, no number unless the row's signal is sourced and dated within 90 days. Escalation: firmer, still their-payoff framed. VP+ timing. 80-110 words.

## E4 — Day 9 · Direct, with a deliverable
Lead with the concrete artifact you'll bring (the read/analysis specific to the motion) and make the ask specific and confident, never seller-want. One question, ends on it, VP-appropriate timing. 75-100 words.

## E5 — Day 12 · Confident close
Short, low-pressure, no guilt, no "last try"/"final attempt" language. Leave the door open, easy to restart. May end on a clean statement rather than a question (the one intentional exception). 45-70 words.

## Audit reuse (every stage)
Each stage draft feeds the SAME critic + voice-audit chain (the auditor is stage-agnostic; it only checks for unsourced numbers and dated/named incidents). Reuse, don't rebuild. A stage is READY only when its own claims-audit AND voice-audit both pass.
