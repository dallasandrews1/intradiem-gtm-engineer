# Stage Ladder (motion-invariant, 3 emails + 2 LinkedIn touches)

The arc is the same for every motion; only the motion slots (position, personas, signal) change. Each stage prompt = [stage header below] + [motion slots] + [voice_core]. All touches carry the SAME one idea, approached from a new facet each touch, never restated. Implements `motions/shared/Messaging_Doctrine_Sep3.md` (Sep 3 2026). The five-email escalation ladder this file used to hold is retired.

## Cadence + defaults
- E1 Day 1 · LinkedIn connect Day 2 · E2 Day 4 · LinkedIn message Day 7 · E3 breakup Day 11. Phone task between touches for planning, WFM and performance personas (they answer); ops directors get the emails. A blitz motion may compress to ten days; it never adds emails.
- Signature: MessageGen generates NO signature. The sender layer applies the first name only; the mailbox signature block carries the rest.
- Thread handling (E2, E3): send as replies in the same thread, subject "re: [shortened E1 subject]". Open on a new facet, never on the silence. BANNED openers: "just following up," "circling back," "bumping this," "checking in," "wanted to resurface," "in case you missed it."
- Length falls through the sequence: E1 under 110 words, E2 shorter than E1, E3 under 60.
- Meeting-with-time asks begin at the Day 7 LinkedIn message or E2, never E1. Directors: "early next week" is fine. VP and above: "in the next couple of weeks."

## Brand
Intradiem is named once in E1 inside the product sentence (voice_core). The old A/B brand convention is retired; the slot in motion_slots.md now holds the motion's product sentence.

## E1 — Day 1 · Cold opener
Opens on the dated signal or the operational moment, product sentence by sentence three, one idea, closes on one no-calendar question (benchmark question or offer note; Nate's US A/B keeps variant A benchmark, variant B offer note). This is the motion's flagship prompt; the others are deltas over it. 70-110 words.

## LinkedIn connect — Day 2
Under 300 characters, counted. References the email ("shot you a note by email, thought this might be quicker") and the topic. May be left blank where the rep prefers it.

## E2 — Day 4 · New facet
One new angle from the persona library, or one new VERIFIED number, never a re-pitch. Shorter than E1. One question that names the topic; a time-bound ask is allowed here. 60-90 words.

## LinkedIn message — Day 7
Short version of the email plus the conversation offer. 50-100 words.

## E3 — Day 11 · Breakup
Shortest. Names the re-entry condition ("if the platform migration puts the intraday question back on your desk") and wishes them well specifically, never generically. No guilt, no "last try." Under 60 words. May end on a clean statement rather than a question (the one intentional exception).

## Exec air cover (optional, per account)
One email under 80 words, only after an operational thread is live, naming the colleagues already engaged. Not a sequence.

## Audit reuse (every stage)
Each stage draft feeds the SAME critic + voice-audit chain (the auditor checks unsourced prospect numbers, unverified Intradiem numbers, category labels in the product sentence, and calendar asks in E1). Reuse, don't rebuild. A stage is READY only when its claims-audit AND voice-audit both pass.
