UK Expansion:
Extending the Engine, Not Copying the US Playbook

Prepared by  Dallas Andrews (GTM Engineer)  ·  For discussion with Naveen Thilagan · M. Rumins · J. O'Hagan (UK)

Situation

Naveen asked me on Monday to extend the engine behind the US motion to the UK business.1 That's what this meeting is for. Where the US motion stands today:

  • The Star Ratings motion is live: 143 contacts staged behind human approval gates, first sequences drafted, nothing sends without sign-off.2 Sales is already asking for more. After reviewing the Centene contact and messaging work, Nathan asked us to run a batch of his accounts next.
  • The engine stood up a second motion (back-office) in two days: built Monday, and by Tuesday night a 59-contact US and UK sample was pulled and fully email-verified at 86 credits against a 150-to-250 estimate.
  • Spend is disciplined: 864 of 5,000 monthly Clay credits used to date (as of Jul 14), and every run is estimated and logged before it happens.3

The part that matters for the UK: everything except sourcing carries over for free. Universe tables, grading formulas, tier logic, committee specs and send gates all transfer to a new market at zero credits. Only contact sourcing and signal enrichment cost money.

Complication

The engine works where one thing exists: a public, recurring customer-service score with money attached. We haven't confirmed what that score is in the UK, or whether it sits in the vertical Intradiem wants to win first.4

  • The blocker isn't data or credits. It's a choice that hasn't been made: which forcing function.5 Three candidates exist (Ofwat C-MeX in water, Citizens Advice star ratings in energy, FCA complaints data in financial services), and each points at a different universe, different personas and different messaging. Build before choosing and we build the wrong table.
  • The default path, copying the US motion onto a list of UK contact-center accounts, spends credits on a cold, unranked universe with no trigger behind it. That's the exact spend the credit discipline exists to prevent.

What it costs to leave the choice unmade:

  • C-MeX results land once a year, in the autumn. If water is the right lane and nothing is built when rankings drop, the best UK trigger of the year hits an empty table.6
  • Back Office Optimizer goes GA in September, and the financial-services lane (claims, disputes, collections) is that product's home turf. Capita and Foundever, both with large UK operations, already sit in our back-office cohort with no UK lane to run in.

Recommendation

Approve a two-week verification pass at zero credits, with water and C-MeX as the working hypothesis. If your install-base or lost-deal picture points somewhere else, that changes the pick, not the plan:

  • Week one: verify the three scoring sources and pull the public tables. Imports are free, so this costs nothing.7 Week two: a graded, tiered UK universe in its own workbook, a first-cut ICP for the lead vertical, and a priced sourcing estimate this group approves or kills.
  • The back-office build is the precedent: stood up in a day, sampled the next, 100% verification rate, well under its credit estimate.8 Same gates apply here. Nothing sends and nothing spends past the logged estimate until the owners in this room say go.

The other two options are a straight copy of the US motion, which spends credits with no trigger behind it, or waiting for the Stars motion's first results, which gives up the autumn C-MeX window and the September BOO alignment. The verification pass is the only option that costs nothing and keeps both windows open.9

Decisions needed from this meeting

  1. Pick the lead vertical. Confirm water and C-MeX as the working hypothesis, or override it with what you know about UK product fit, logos or partner motion.
  2. Send the UK picture by Friday, July 17. Current UK customers, near-misses and closed-lost deals, plus any prior work on UK regulatory scoring so we don't rebuild known ground.
  3. Agree the readout date. Graded UK universe and a priced sourcing estimate back to this group by Friday, July 24. Owner: Dallas. Cost to that point: zero credits.
US figures above come straight from the Clay usage ledger. UK regulatory details (C-MeX framework and timing, Citizens Advice cadence, FCA data granularity) get verified in week one before anything is built on them.
