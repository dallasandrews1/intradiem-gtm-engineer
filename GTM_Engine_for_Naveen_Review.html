01

What is built

Seven working pieces, live in the Cowork sidebar

Command Center

The whole engine in one view: the acquisition funnel, the install-base funnel, the gates, and the engine-sourced scoreboard.

Golden List

Accounts scored by fit and why-now. Click any one for the full strike plan, committee, and ready sequences.

Strike Room

Pick an account and it builds the pitch from the live plan: snapshot, ROI, triggers, committee, sequences per persona.

ROI Calculator

Idle-labor recovery math with every assumption exposed. Loads a real account to cross-check the engine's own quote.

Activation & White-Space Control Plane

The install-base side. Adoption gaps, expansion white-space, and churn risk per customer, each with a recommended play and an in-product message.

The orchestration underneath

A weekly conductor that drafts, screens each draft through an objective critic, holds for human approval, and only sends when deliverability is green. Clay and attribution sit under it.

The cadence

A daily 7am signal briefing and a Monday engine run. It moves whether or not I am watching it.
