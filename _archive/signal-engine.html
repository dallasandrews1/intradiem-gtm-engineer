I won't claim a live API on day one — there isn't one, and a product leader would catch it. Day one is a reporting export on a cron with 30-day rolling windows and 14-day suppression. The architecture doesn't change when the data source swaps to a live stream at day 90; only the input does. That's the point of building it as a thin signal layer instead of a pipeline rebuild.

What I'd Deliberately Not Build

✕A black-box "health score" on day one — unauditable signals die in ops review
✕Vanity signals (logins, dashboard views) that don't predict revenue
✕The full 8-signal taxonomy at once — ship the narrow wedge that proves value first
✕A second analytics platform ops has to govern when a thin layer will do
Knowing what to leave out is most of the judgment. Rule-based and auditable beats clever-but-opaque every time it touches a system of record.

Build vs. Buy — Why Not Just Point Pendo or Gainsight PX at It

Those tools instrument app clickstream for self-serve SaaS — feature adoption, funnel drop-off, in-app guides. Intradiem's expansion signal doesn't live in clicks. It lives in seat_utilization against contract, idle-time reclaimed, and automation volume across a 250k-agent install base — outcome data a generic PLG tool has no concept of.
So the asset isn't telemetry plumbing — that's commodity. The asset is the domain-specific signal logic and routing sitting on the event stream you already own. Build the thin layer; don't buy a second platform your ops team has to govern and your reps have to learn. The build-vs-buy line is exactly where a GTM Engineer earns the title.

What I'd Build First — and Why

seat_utilization. Cleanest expansion signal in the set: near-zero false positives, tied directly to revenue, trivial to validate against a closed expansion. Prove the wedge on the signal nobody can argue with, earn the right to instrument the rest.
The signal is the easy 20%. What a coaching_coverage drop means in a live contact center — that's the 80% I can't fake, which is why every threshold here is a placeholder until the people who run the motion set it.
