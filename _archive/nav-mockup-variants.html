Expansion Engine

Selecting a lane would reveal that lane's tabs (a second row) and open the first one.
[ sub-tabs for the chosen lane appear here, then the tab content ]
Trade-off: cleanest first impression and closest to what you described — but it adds a click to reach any tab, and a panelist who picks one lane may never see the others. Higher build (two-level nav state) and the risk of burying proof. Use this only if you want the entry to feel like a deliberate "choose your path."
