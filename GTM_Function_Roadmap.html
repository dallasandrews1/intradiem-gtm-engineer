03 · Stay ahead of the question

Exec answer bank

Strategy questions I will get as visibility grows, answered before they're asked. Each anchored to something built, so the answer is a demo, not an opinion.

Why build our own engine instead of buying an outbound tool or an AI SDR?

Tools rent you tactics; the engine owns the judgment. Our moat is the trigger-to-persona taxonomy, the verified-claims discipline, and signals tuned to OUR buyers, none of which ships in a vendor box. We buy commodity layers (Clay for enrichment, sequencers for delivery) and keep the brain. AI SDRs specifically: they optimize for volume of conversations and torch domains and trust; we gate volume behind deliverability and human approval, because our market is six regulated verticals, not SMB spray.

Anchored in: config-over-code engines · conductor gates · brand of the whole build
Should we be worried AI outbound is getting saturated and reply rates will collapse?

Yes, volume-only outbound is decaying, which is exactly why ours is signal-triggered instead of calendar-triggered. A message that lands the week a forcing function fires competes with five emails, not five hundred. When channel yield drops, the engine shifts mix (voice, video, events, install-base motions) because the brain is channel-agnostic; only the last mile changes. And the measurement layer tells us the moment decay starts instead of two quarters later.

Anchored in: signal library tiers · variant tracker · deliverability monitor
How does GTM engineering relate to our own product story?

It IS the product story, applied to ourselves: real-time signals trigger the right action automatically while a human keeps judgment. We run pipeline the way Intradiem runs a workforce. That makes every internal demo a live proof point a prospect can be shown, and it means when we sell "orchestration," we can say we operate on it.

Anchored in: signal-to-play skill · the platform-pitch echo story
What's the plan when Q3's targets change or Q4 lands something new?

The mandates are workloads; the engine is the operating system. A new directive compiles the same way every time: define the measurable line, pick the universe, wire the signals, budget the credits, instrument before volume, readout weekly. That compile step is itself becoming a skill, so the answer to "can you do X next quarter" is always "yes, here's the scoped motion by Friday."

Anchored in: mandate-compiler (Layer 8) · pattern-first skill design
Where should the company invest next in GTM?

In order: (1) the data layer under the install base, because expansion signals are the cheapest pipeline we will ever source; (2) the launch-kit muscle before the 2027 GA wave, so every launch lands with a repeatable motion instead of a scramble; (3) demo engineering, because interactive artifacts are compressing our sales cycle at the exact stage decks stall. Each has a working seed already; investment scales what's proven, never funds a bet.

Anchored in: signal engine · launch-kit seam · live artifact suite
How do we know what the engine produced versus what Sales would have gotten anyway?

Source tags from the first send: three Salesforce fields, ratified with Naveen in writing, and one scorecard that never blends surfaced with realized. If the engine's column is empty, I'll say so first; the credibility of the number IS the product. That honesty is also the answer to any future attribution dispute before it starts.

Anchored in: attribution loop spec · impact engine · gate #1
04 · Keep it alive

The living protocol

This page is a working surface, not a snapshot. It moves on three triggers, and it answers to one test.

After every Friday readout

Chips and horizons update to what actually shipped. Anything that slipped gets a reason on the page, never a silent re-date. The readout's running log is the input; if this page and the readout log disagree, the log wins.

The moment a mandate changes

New directive → the mandate-compiler pattern: measurable line, universe, signals, credit budget, instrumentation, readout hooks. The layers absorb the workload; this page records the recompile. Q3's numbers changing should cost hours of reconfiguration, never weeks of rebuilding.

When a layer completes

Its skills retire into the Start Here index as operating assets, and this page replaces them with the next frontier. If a quarter ends with no new frontier on this page, I have stopped being ahead, and that is the earliest warning I will get.

The standing test

If ever someone can think of me, my impact, and this role, and believe they could accomplish it without me, all three surfaces have failed. The correction starts here, and the moat is velocity and vision, never opacity: everything documented and runnable by others, while I remain the only person who could have built this quarter's version of it. Hoarding knowledge makes me a bottleneck execs route around; compounding faster than anyone can follow makes me the person they route through.
