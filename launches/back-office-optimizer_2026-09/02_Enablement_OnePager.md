# BOO Enablement One-Pager (rep's ten minutes before the call)

## What it is

Real-time workforce automation for structured back-office production work: claims, appeals/UM, payment operations, disputes, collections, document processing, enrollment, underwriting support, coding, RCM, scheduling, order-management exceptions. It watches live queues and backlogs and acts automatically: reassigns tasks, triggers training in idle windows, rebalances workloads in seconds.

## Who buys it

- **`bo_claims`** (VP/Dir Claims, Appeals, UM) — primary pain owner in payer/insurance. Pain: Monday-regrowing backlog, aging queues, TAT/SLA penalties, overtime, rework loops, surge seasons staffed by guesswork.
- **`bo_shared`** (VP/Dir Payment Ops, Disputes, Collections, Doc Processing, Shared Services, Enrollment, RCM) — primary buyer in FS/provider/BPO. Pain: cost-per-transaction scrutiny, no real-time visibility across multi-function queues, utilization they can't prove.
- **`coo_finance`** (COO, SVP/VP Ops, CFO) — economic buyer, enters LATER in the sequence, never the first touch. Pain: capacity without headcount; idle time already being paid for.

**Never pull:** IT/Security (RPA owners are an objection source, not the buyer), Clinical, Legal/Compliance, HR, contact-center titles (the front-office relationship already covers them).

## The three discovery questions

1. "When a queue starts aging at 10am, what happens between then and tomorrow's report?"
2. "What did back-office overtime run last quarter, and how much of it traces to backlog catch-up?"
3. "How do idle windows get used today — and who decides, at what speed?"

## FAQ

**Does it replace our BPM/workflow tool?** No; it acts on the state those tools expose.
**Contact-center tool repainted?** No. Same real-time discipline, purpose-built for back-office production work — back offices AND contact centers, six verticals, is the company scope.
