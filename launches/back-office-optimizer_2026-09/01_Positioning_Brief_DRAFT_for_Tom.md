# BOO Positioning Brief — DRAFT compiled for Tom Russell's review

*A compiled draft from the market-readiness work and ICP v1. Tom owns positioning and narrative; nothing below ships until he has reshaped and blessed it.*

## The problem (buyer's words, not ours)

Back-office production work (claims, appeals, payment ops, disputes, document processing, enrollment, RCM) is managed off yesterday's report. Queues age, backlogs regrow every Monday, SLA clocks run, and overtime absorbs the gap, because nothing watches the work in real time and acts while the day is still live.

## Category frame

Not RPA (which automates the task), not WFM (which plans the day), not BI (which reports on it afterward). BOO is the real-time layer that watches live queues and workforce state and **acts inside the day**: reassigning work, triggering training in idle windows, rebalancing loads in seconds. The same discipline Intradiem built for contact centers, applied to the back office, where no equivalent exists.

## One-sentence position (draft)

> Back Office Optimizer turns the back office's idle minutes and aging queues into worked items and met SLAs, automatically, while the day is still happening.

## Three message pillars (draft)

1. **The cost lives between reports.** The back office runs on yesterday's numbers; the expense (idle time, aging, overtime) accrues in the hours between them. Real-time is not a dashboard refresh rate, it is the ability to act.
2. **Capacity you already paid for.** The value comes out of time already on the payroll: idle windows become training and flexed work, backlog overtime becomes rebalanced load. Found capacity, not new headcount and not a new line item.
3. **Proven discipline, new floor.** The front office already runs this way (in our install base, their own front office literally does). BOO extends a working operating model to the biggest unautomated workforce in the building.

## What we never say

- Any ROI, payback, lift, or customer-outcome number that is not repository-verified (today: none for back office). Public-site figures are citable only as published claims, clearly attributed.
- Product tells: "workforce orchestration," "synergy," "AI-powered" filler, or any call-center framing. BOO is back-office white space; never a call-center feature bolted sideways.
- Anything that disparages the tools they own (RPA, WFM, BPM). We act between and on top of them.
